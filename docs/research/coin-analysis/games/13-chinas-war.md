# China's War 1937-41 (COIN Vol XIII)

## Identity

- Title: China's War 1937-41. COIN Series, Volume XIII (Rules p.1).
- Publisher: GMT Games. Rules of Play carry "(c) 2025 GMT Games" (Rules p.2). The Non-Player booklet shows a print stamp of 12.03.2025 (Non-Player p.13).
- Credits (Playbook p.27): Game Design Brian Train. Development Jason Carr. Original COIN Series Design Volko Ruhnke. COIN Series Development Jason Carr. Sun Wukong Solitaire System Design and Development Joe Dewhurst. Jacquard Solitaire System Design Bruce Mansfield. Map, card and counter design Matthew Wallhead.
- Setting: Second Sino-Japanese War, first four years (1937 to 1941) (1.0).
- Scale: one Population point is about 10 million civilians. The map is about 1,500 miles by 900 miles (1.3.2; Playbook p.23).
- Players: 1 to 4. Each player controls one Faction. With fewer players, a Non-Player system or multi-Faction control fills the gaps (1.5).
- Scenarios: 1937 (52-card deck) and 1938 (39-card deck) (Setup and Scenarios).
- Components: 52 Event cards (48 Events plus 4 Propaganda), 24 Non-Player cards, 144 wooden pieces (1.2).
- Playbook content: tutorial by Evan Yoak, card-by-card notes, designer notes, bibliography (Playbook p.2, p.23, p.26).
- Designer note: this is the first COIN game set in World War Two and the largest in scale (Playbook p.23).

## Factions

Four Factions. All are enemies of each other. The three Chinese Factions (NAT, WLD, CPC) fight Japan but also compete with each other (1.5.1).

| Faction | Type | Forces | Victory (checked in Propaganda Round, 7.2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| JPN (khaki) | Government / occupier, conventional | 30 Troops, 9 Police | Econ of LoCs with JPN cubes and no enemy pieces, plus Commitment, exceeds 40 |
| NAT (blue) | Government, conventional | 30 Troops, 9 Bases, 6 Guerrillas | Total Support Population plus Patronage exceeds 40 |
| WLD (orange) | Regional power, conventional | 24 Troops, 9 Bases | Total WLD-Controlled Population plus WLD Bases exceeds 25 |
| CPC (red) | Insurgent | 18 Guerrillas, 9 Bases | Total Opposition Population plus CPC Bases exceeds 20 |

(Forces: 1.4. Thresholds: 7.2.)

Resources and other values:
- All Factions hold 0 to 60 Resources on the edge track (1.8).
- Commitment (0 to 60): limits JPN Troops on the map, adds to JPN Resources and JPN victory (1.8, 6.2).
- Patronage (0 to 30): adds to NAT victory (1.8).
- Foreign Aid (0 to 10): moves to NAT Resources or Patronage in the Propaganda Round, then resets (1.8, 6.5, 6.6).
- A value cannot go below 0. Excess loss is ignored (1.8).
- NAT may move Patronage to WLD Resources one for one during an Operation, Special Activity or Event (1.5.2).

Faction notes:
- JPN. Only Faction with Police. Police count against NAT and WLD Control. JPN Troops do not count for Control (1.7). Only JPN may enter Manchukuo (1.3.5). JPN has a Large Offensive once per Campaign (2.3.8).
- NAT. Guerrillas are placed only by Partisan Action. They never March or Attack (3.3 play note). NAT is "conventional" despite Guerrillas (3.2).
- WLD. Pays an escalating cost per space: 1, 2, 3 and so on (3.2.1). NAT may take WLD Troops along on Patrol, Sweep and Assault if NAT pays WLD 1 Resource (3.2.3, 3.2.4, 3.2.5).
- CPC. Familiar insurgent set of Guerrillas (Underground or Active) and Bases (1.4.3).
- Tie-break for ranking: CPC, then WLD, then NAT (7.1). JPN is not in the tie-break list (not verified why).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- No hands. One card is played, the next card is revealed (1.1, 2.2).
- Eligible Factions act in the left-to-right order of the card's Faction icons (2.3.2).
- A Faction that did nothing on the last card, or Passed, stays Eligible. A Faction that executed an Operation or Event becomes Ineligible (2.3.1, 2.3.6).
- Options for 1st Eligible: Operation (with or without Special Activity), Event, or Pass (2.3.4).
- Options for 2nd Eligible (2.3.4):
  - 1st did Op only: 2nd may do a Limited Operation.
  - 1st did Op plus Special Activity: 2nd may do a Limited Operation or the Event.
  - 1st did the Event: 2nd may do an Operation, with Special Activity if wanted.
- Limited Operation: one space, no Special Activity. Patrol, Sweep and March may use many origins but one destination (2.3.5).
- Pass gives +3 Resources to JPN, +2 to NAT, +1 to WLD or CPC. The Faction stays Eligible (2.3.3).
- Change from series core: unequal Pass income, and the JPN Large Offensive (2.3.8).
- Large Offensive (2.3.8):
  - JPN executes a full Operation plus Special Activity at any time it is Eligible. Free of Resource cost, but costs 1 Commitment. Not possible at 0 Commitment.
  - If JPN was 1st Eligible, it stays Eligible for the next card.
  - If JPN was 2nd Eligible, it may do a full Operation plus Special Activity whatever the 1st Faction did.
  - The marker flips to "used" and returns in the Reset Phase (6.6).
- Free Operations from Events cost no Resources and do not change Eligibility (3.1.2, 5.5).
- Optional rules (Setup): No Reveal, Sudden Propaganda Discard, Eligibility Option A (order from next card), Option B (only Events can make a Faction Ineligible). Do not use them with Non-Player Factions (Non-Player 8.4.1).

## Operations and Special Activities

Costs are in Resources. JPN and NAT conventional costs are per space unless noted.

**Conventional Operations (JPN, NAT, WLD) (3.2)**

| Operation | Effect | Cost and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Recruit (3.2.2) | JPN: place or replace WLD Troops with up to 6 Police over up to 3 Provinces. NAT: up to 3 Troops per Province, plus one Base (replace 2 Troops) or Pacify. WLD: up to 3 Troops per Province, only in Provinces with a WLD Base. | JPN 3 total. NAT 2 per Province. WLD escalating. Pacify costs 2 per Terror removed or level shifted. Needs no Terror (JPN, WLD). |
| Patrol (3.2.3) | Move cubes onto LoCs. JPN and CPC Guerrillas on the LoC activate. Then Assault. | JPN 3 total. NAT 2 total. WLD 1 total and max 3 LoCs. Assault: JPN 3 per Assault. NAT and WLD get one free Assault. |
| Sweep (3.2.4) | Move Troops in, then Activate Guerrillas (1 per cube; 1 per 2 cubes in Rough). | JPN 3 per space. NAT 2. WLD escalating. |
| Assault (3.2.5) | Remove enemy pieces by Combat Value (CV) and a die roll. | JPN 3 per space. NAT 2 (plus 1 to WLD to include WLD Troops). WLD escalating. |

Assault rules (3.2.5):
- CV: JPN Troop 2. NAT or WLD Troop 1. Bases and Police 1 when defending only. Active Guerrillas total 1. Rough Province adds 1 for defenders. Maximum CV is 6.
- Underground Guerrillas cannot be assaulted. Bases go last.
- Each side rolls one die. A roll at or under own CV removes enemy CV equal to the roll. The executing Faction picks the losses on both sides.
- Disaster: NAT loses 1 Patronage if a NAT Base or 3+ Chinese Troops die. Glorious Victory: NAT gains 1 Patronage if 2+ JPN cubes die with a Troop among them. This applies whoever acts.

**CPC Operations (3.3)**

| Operation | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Rally (3.3.1) | Place 1 Guerrilla, or replace 2 Guerrillas with a Base. With a Base: flip all Underground, or place Guerrillas up to Bases plus Pop. Not in Support or Terror. | 1 per Province |
| March (3.3.2) | Move Guerrillas. Chain-march from Opposition Provinces. They Activate if entering Support or a LoC and moving group plus cubes there exceeds 3. | 1 per Province entered, 0 for LoCs |
| Attack (3.3.3) | Activate all. Roll at or under Guerrilla count: remove up to 2 JPN cubes. Lose 1 Guerrilla and 1 Patronage per Troop removed. Roll of 1 places a Guerrilla. | 1 per space |
| Agitate (3.3.4) | Activate 1+ Underground. Remove Terror, else shift to Opposition; or place Sabotage on a LoC. | 1 per Province, 0 for LoCs |

## Event cards and deck

- Deck: 52 cards. Cards 1 to 48 are Events. Cards 49 to 52 are Propaganda (Playbook p.2 tutorial names Propaganda cards #49 to #52; Playbook card list; 1.2).
- Event flavor text has no rule effect (5.0).
- Dual Use: unshaded or shaded text, never both (5.2). Unshaded is often pro-Chinese. Shaded is often pro-Japanese (Index).
- Capability: lasting for the rest of the game, shown by a marker (5.3). Momentum: lasts until the next Reset Phase (5.4). The Playbook lists about 4 Capability headings and 18 Momentum headings (counted from the Playbook text, not verified against the cards).
- Event text beats rules, but Events never place unavailable pieces, never break stacking and never give a Faction an Operation it does not own (5.1.1, 5.0). Currently played Event beats a conflicting one (5.1.2).
- Events cannot push Foreign Aid over 10, Patronage over 30, or Resources and Commitment over 60 (5.0).
- Deck build: remove the 4 Propaganda cards, shuffle the 48 Events, split into piles of 12 (Setup).
  - 1937: one Propaganda card into the bottom half of the first pile, one into each of the other three piles. 52-card deck.
  - 1938: remove one pile unseen, put a Propaganda card face up as already played, shuffle one into each of the three piles. 39-card deck.
- Historical content (Playbook p.12 onward): Embargo, German Aid, Soviet Advisors, Sino-Soviet Non-Aggression Pact, Battle of Taierzhuang, Hundred Regiments Offensive, New Fourth Army Incident, Nomonhan Campaign, Wang Jingwei, Floods, Scorched Earth, Famine, and others. Each card has a Background note.
- Faction icons on cards also hold Non-Player markers: a dotted ring means Critical, a crossed circle means Not Performed (5.0; Non-Player 8.5.5).

## Map, spaces and tracks

- Space types: Provinces (rural), LoCs (Railroad or River) and Foreign Countries. Towns are black dots and are not spaces (1.3.1, 1.3.4).
- Province Pop 0 to 3. Types: Rough, Plains, Coastal. Coastal means touched by the sea even at a Town (1.3.2).
- LoC Econ 0 to 3. Total Econ of all LoCs is 29 (6.2; Playbook p.24).
- Manchukuo: JPN only. French Indochina: out of play (1.3.5).
- Adjacency: touching spaces, Provinces across a LoC, and spaces meeting at a Town (1.3.6).
- Stacking: max 2 Bases per Province. No Bases on LoCs (1.4.2).
- Support, Neutral, Opposition: one level each, no passive or active split. Two shifts go from Support to Opposition. LoCs and Pop 0 Provinces are always Neutral (1.6; Playbook p.24).
- Control: only NAT or WLD. Count Active pieces only. Majority over all others combined. JPN Troops are ignored. JPN Police count against (1.7).
- Sabotage on LoCs and Terror in Provinces. One marker per space (1.10).
  - Sabotage costs JPN Resources where JPN cubes sit (with JPN present), blocks NAT and CPC income, and stops Transport.
  - Terror blocks JPN and WLD Recruit, CPC Rally, NAT Partisan Action and WLD Organize. Terror stays after a Propaganda Round (Playbook p.25).
- Edge track 0 to 60: Resources, Aid, Patronage, Commitment, victory markers (1.3.7).
- Boxes: Available, Casualties, Out of Play, Capabilities, Overflow (1.3.7).
- 1937 start totals: JPN 25, NAT 26, CPC 7, WLD 19. 1938 start totals: JPN 22, NAT 21, CPC 9, WLD 15 (Setup).
- Province names appear in setup (for example Shaanxi, Hebei, Sichuan, Shanghai). Full adjacency list: not verified (no map image).

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

Triggered when a Propaganda card is revealed. It swaps with the Event card about to be played. That Event plays first in the next Campaign (2.3.7, 6.0). Never two rounds in a row (6.0). Phases in order:

1. Victory (6.1). Check 7.2 thresholds. Skip on the first Propaganda Round of the 1937 scenario only.
2. Resources (6.2).
   - NAT: Econ of LoCs with no Sabotage and no JPN cubes. May then convert Patronage to Resources.
   - JPN: Commitment minus Econ of LoCs with both JPN forces and Sabotage.
   - WLD and CPC: number of Bases on the map.
3. Support (6.3). NAT spends 2 Resources per Terror removed or shift toward Support, in NAT-Controlled Provinces. CPC spends 1 Resource per Terror removed or shift toward Opposition, in Provinces with CPC pieces and no NAT Control.
4. Redeploy (6.4). Order NAT, CPC, JPN, WLD.
   - NAT and WLD Troops must leave Provinces with no own Base for Provinces with a Base. NAT may pay Patronage (1 per 2 Troops) to bring Troops from Out of Play.
   - CPC Guerrillas go to Opposition or Base Provinces.
   - JPN may move up to 6 Troops and any Police to Provinces that started with JPN cubes, or Manchukuo.
   - If this is the final round, the game ends here (6.4.1).
5. Aid and Commitment (6.5). NAT gets Foreign Aid equal to one third of JPN Troop Casualties (round down), max 10, then splits it between Resources and Patronage. JPN Commitment: minus 2 if 3 x Econ of clean LoCs is below Commitment; minus 2 if 3 x JPN Troop Casualties is above Commitment; plus 1 if that product is half of Commitment or less. Then JPN removes excess Troops to Out of Play.
6. Reset (6.6). One third of JPN and NAT Troop Casualties go Out of Play; the rest go Available. Remove all Sabotage (not Terror). Flip the Large Offensive marker. Flip all Guerrillas Underground. Discard Momentum cards. Reset Foreign Aid. Set all Factions Eligible.

Final scoring (7.3): if no one wins at the last Propaganda Round, the highest margin wins. Margin is the amount over or under the threshold. WLD margin uses Patronage in place of 25 (WLD Control Pop + Bases minus Patronage). This models a Warlord clique deposing Jiang Jieshi.

## Non-player (bot) system

Named "Sun Wukong" (Non-Player 8.1). Adapted from the Jacquard card-based system by Bruce Mansfield. Replaces any Faction (1 to 3 NP Factions).

Components: six cards per NP Faction (24 total), Priorities tables (Space Selection per Faction, shared Move and Piece), Eligibility table, Event tables, Propaganda instructions (8.1.1).

Golden rules (8.2.1): NP follows the rules except for Activation Numbers. Skip illegal instructions. Read literally. Place pieces one at a time using Space Selection Priorities. Remove from the biggest stack. Do the maximum. If in doubt, roll randomly. NP never pulls pieces from the map to refill from Available. NP tracks no Resources. A player Faction just gains or loses the stated amount.

Flow:
1. Eligibility table (8.5.2) picks Op plus Special Activity, Op only, Limited Op, Event or Pass, based on Critical or Not Performed icons, the next card and position.
2. Draw a Sun Wukong card (8.5.3). Move cards from the top to the bottom, face up, until one matches the acting Faction. Reshuffle only at setup and each Reset Phase.
3. Card flowchart: blue boxes with yes or no arrows. Many conditions use dice (1d6, 2d6, 1d3 against Available pieces). Results may redraw or flip the card.
4. Numbered instructions run in order, as far as possible. Bold text names the column of the Space Selection table. Starred instructions run last.
5. Special Activity: take the first listed activity that works, in letter order. If none works, do the Operation alone.
6. Activation Number (AN) (8.5.4): after each space, roll 1d6. A roll at or below AN stops selection. Above AN selects another space. WLD AN rises by 1 after each success. Exceptions include JPN Recruit, all Patrol destinations, JPN Large Offensive and some CPC actions.
7. Space Selection (8.5.6): read a column top down. Each dotted row narrows candidates. Ties go to the next row. Final tie: random.
8. Move Priorities (8.5.7): pick destination, pick origin with most moveable pieces, "keep" rows protect Control and Force Parity, "get" rows say what to bring.
9. Force Parity: assaulting CV at least equal to defending CV, max 6 (Non-Player Terms).
10. Events (8.5.5): Critical and effective Events are played when 1st or 2nd Eligible. Performed and effective Events are played only when 2nd Eligible. JPN picks shaded text. NAT, WLD and CPC pick unshaded. No Capabilities in the final Campaign. Momentum only in the first three cards of a Campaign.
11. Propaganda (8.5.9; Example p.25): NAT and CPC spend up to 1d6 Terror removals and shifts. Fixed Redeploy rules per Faction.

Victory vs. solitaire player: the player can win only at the final Propaganda Round. NP Factions can win at any Victory Phase after the first (8.3.1).

## Unique mechanics

- Four asymmetric Factions with two conventional armies and a Warlord ally-rival (1.5.1, 3.2).
- Victory depends on LoCs. JPN scores Econ of LoCs held alone (1.3.3, 7.2).
- Control counts only Active pieces and ignores JPN Troops. JPN Police count (1.7).
- Patronage and Commitment as hidden-cost "political capital" tracks that most actions move (3.2.5, 3.3.3, 4.2.3, 4.4.2, 6.5).
- Disaster and Glorious Victory: NAT Patronage moves with any battle (3.2.5).
- WLD escalating cost per space (3.2.1) and NAT paying WLD to use WLD Troops (3.2.3).
- JPN Large Offensive: a once-per-Campaign free Operation paid with Commitment (2.3.8).
- Persistent Terror, only JPN creates it (Playbook p.25).
- Sabotage costs JPN Resources only when JPN cubes also sit on the LoC (6.2).
- Foreign Aid scales with JPN Troop Casualties (6.5).
- Casualty recycle: one third of casualties leave play for good each round (6.6).
- Skulduggery: CPC Subvert and WLD Suborn convert pieces (Playbook p.25).
- WLD final margin uses Patronage (7.3).
- Single-level Support and Opposition (Playbook p.24).

## Relevance

- Inferred: the Sun Wukong flowchart cards, Activation Number and priority tables give a complete, deterministic-plus-dice bot pattern. A digital Falling Sky bot could reuse the draw-and-flip card logic and the "first applicable action" priority list. A PKK game could reuse it for a state bot.
- Inferred: a state that wins by holding lines and nodes (LoC Econ here) could model Turkish control of roads and garrison towns in a PKK game. The "ignore regular troops for Control, count local auxiliaries" rule could model village guards.
- Inferred: Patronage and Commitment style tracks (political will of the home government, legitimacy of the leader) fit a Turkish public-support or coalition-capital track. Disaster and Glorious Victory give a cheap way to link battles to a political track.
- Inferred: the escalating per-space cost for a fragmented Faction, and the pay-an-ally-to-use-its-pieces rule, could model a loosely controlled Faction in a PKK game. Persistent Terror that blocks recruitment could model forced evacuations of villages.
