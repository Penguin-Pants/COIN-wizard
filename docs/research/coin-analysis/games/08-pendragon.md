# Pendragon (COIN Series Volume VIII)

## Identity

- Title: Pendragon, The Fall of Roman Britain (cover of 1.0). Working title in the Playbook design notes: De Excidio Britanniae (also a scenario name).
- COIN volume: VIII. Rules file title: "Pendragon Living Rules, November 2023" (title page). Footer: (c) 2023 GMT Games. Playbook footer: (c) 2017 GMT Games.
- Designer: Marc Gouyon-Rety (rulebook cover; Playbook credits). Developer: Volko Ruhnke (Playbook credits, p.72). Solitaire systems: Volko Ruhnke, VPJ Arponen and Orjan Ariander (Playbook p.72).
- Setting: the fall of the Roman Diocese of Britain, from the first large raids of Irish, Pict and Saxon raiders to the successor kingdoms, 5th Century (1.0). Scenarios start in the late 4th Century.
- Scale: region map of Britain. 21 Regions and 2 Cities (Londinium, Eboracum), 4 Seas, plus Caledonia as an off-map raid origin (1.3). Total printed Population 40 (Playbook p.72). Turn length per card: not verified.
- Components: 22 x 34 inch board, 83 cards, 58 Stronghold pieces, 102 Troop cubes, 55 Raiders, 90 Prosperity/Plunder cubes (1.2). Cards: 72 Events, 4 Pivotal, 7 Epoch (Setup).
- Players: 1 to 4. Spare Factions run by players or by the Non-player system (1.5.1, 8.0).
- Scenarios (Setup, Scenarios):

| Scenario | Length | Start state |
|---|---|---|
| Barbarian Conspiracy | Short, up to 2 Epochs | Roman Rule, Military Dominance. No Pivotal Events. |
| Petty Tyrants | Short, up to 2 Epochs | Fragmentation. Two Pivotals and Tears of Epona in effect. |
| The Harder They Fall | Medium, up to 4 Epochs | Roman Rule, Civilian Dominance. Conspiratio Barbarica resolved. |
| De Excidio Britanniae | Full, up to 6 Epochs | Same board as Barbarian Conspiracy. All four Pivotals dealt. |

## Factions

Four Factions. Dux and Civitates are "Britons". Saxons and Scotti are "Barbarians" (1.5). All are enemies, except Britons are Friendly to each other before Fragmentation (1.5).

| Faction | Type | Resource | Pieces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dux (red) | Government, post-Roman army | Briton Resources (shared) or Dux Resources. Prestige 0 to 80 (1.8.3) | 20 Cavalry, 10 Forts (1.2) |
| Civitates (blue) | Government, Briton landholders | Briton Resources and Wealth, each 0 to 80 (1.8.1, 1.8.4) | 30 Militia, 15 Comitates, 15 Towns, 15 Hillforts |
| Saxons (black) | Insurgent, invader | Renown 0 to 80 (1.8.2) | 25 Warbands, 25 Raiders, 12 Settlements |
| Scotti (green) | Insurgent, invader | Renown 0 to 80 | 12 Warbands, 30 Raiders, 6 Settlements |

Victory conditions (7.2). The check runs in each Victory Phase (6.3).

- Dux, before Fragmentation: Prosperity (all of the map) plus Prestige must exceed 75 at Roman Rule or 60 at Autonomy, and Dux must hold Military Dominance (7.2.1).
- Dux, at Fragmentation: Population under Dux Control plus Prestige must exceed 17 (7.2.1).
- Civitates, before Fragmentation: Population under Briton Control must exceed 36 at Roman Rule or 27 at Autonomy, and Civitates must hold Civilian Dominance (7.2.2).
- Civitates, at Fragmentation: Population under Civitates Control must exceed 16 (7.2.2).
- Saxons: Renown above 30 and at least 4 Saxon Settlements on the map, before Fragmentation only. Or, at any Imperium, Population under Saxon Control above 10 (7.2.3).
- Scotti: Renown above 45 and at least 4 Scotti Settlements on the map (7.2.4). Foederati Settlements count (7.2.3, 7.2.4).

Final Epoch margins (7.3):

- Dux: Prosperity plus Prestige minus 75, 85, 60 or 70 for the four Roman Rule or Autonomy and Dominance cases. At Fragmentation: Dux Control plus Prestige minus 17.
- Civitates: Briton Control minus 38 or 36 (Roman Rule, Military or Civilian), 31 or 27 (Autonomy), 16 (Fragmentation).
- Saxons: the higher of (Renown minus 50 plus 5 per Settlement, not at Fragmentation) or (Saxon Control minus 10).
- Scotti: Renown minus 65 plus 5 per Settlement.

Ranking (7.1): if any player Faction passes, it wins. If several pass, the highest margin ranks first. Ties go to Non-players, then Civitates, then Dux, then Saxons. If a Non-player passes, all players lose.

Special notes:

- A player with two Factions uses the lower margin (1.5.1).
- Foederati: Barbarian Warbands and Settlements may be in the employ of Dux or Civitates (Foederati marker). Foederati pieces count as pieces of the employing Faction in all aspects, such as Control and Commands, except where the rules refer to their Saxon or Scotti Nationality (Home Terrain in Battle 3.6.1, and victory 7.2) (1.4.3).
- Scenarios change the Barbarian thresholds (Barbarian Conspiracy: Saxons 3 Population or Renown 24 with 3 Settlements, Scotti Renown 24 with 2 Settlements).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- Series core is kept: one card is shown ahead, Eligible Factions act in the order of the card symbols, and Factions that acted become Ineligible on the next card (2.3, 2.3.1, 2.3.6).
- 1st Eligible may Pass, execute a Command (with one Feat) or execute the Event (2.3.4).
- 2nd Eligible options (2.3.4):
  1. After Command only: Limited Command.
  2. After Command and Feat: Limited Command or Event.
  3. After Event: full Command, with a Feat if wanted.
- Limited Command: one space and no Feat (2.3.5).
- Pass: Britons get +3 Resources, Barbarians +1 Renown. A Passing Faction stays Eligible (2.3.3). Dux Pass gives Briton Resources before Fragmentation and Dux Resources at Fragmentation (2.3.3).
- Changes from the series core:
  - Pivotal Events: owned by Factions, played to interrupt a card, with a trump order (2.3.8). Scotti trump all. Saxons trump Dux and Civitates. Dux trumps Civitates. Civitates trump none.
  - Epoch cards are shuffled into the deck. A revealed Epoch card swaps with the card to be played, and the displaced card waits on the deck (2.3.7).
  - Spaces must be chosen before any resolution (3.1).
  - Feats replace Special Activities and are chosen once per Command (4.1).

## Operations and Special Activities

The game calls them Commands and Feats. Each Faction has four of each (3.0, 4.0).

**Dux Commands**

- Train: pay 3 per space to place Cavalry (1 per Fort) and up to 2 Militia. Pay 2 in up to 2 Controlled spaces to add Prosperity equal to Population (3.2.1).
- March: pay 2 per origin space. Groups need a Cavalry or red Foederati. Road movement continues through Controlled spaces along Maintained Roads. Militia move one space (3.2.2).
- Intercept: move Cavalry and red Foederati to Raider spaces and Battle. Pay 2 per origin (3.2.3).
- Battle: no cost (3.2.4).

**Dux Feats (4.2)**

- Build: pay 3 to place a Fort, or replace Militia or a Hillfort with a Town for +2 Prestige.
- Invite: settle Foederati (Saxon or Scotti), or convert blue Foederati to red for 1 per piece. Not at Fragmentation.
- Requisition: in up to 6 selected spaces with a Dux piece and a Civitates Stronghold, move 1 Resource each from Briton to Dux Resources (from Civitates Wealth once Briton Resources are zero). Convert Dux Plunder to Resources.
- Retaliate: with Intercept or Battle. Removing all enemy Strongholds in the space gives +3 Prestige and -5 enemy Renown or Wealth.

**Civitates Commands (3.3)**

- Muster: pay 2 per space. Place Militia. Pay 1 Wealth per Comitates. Or replace Militia with Hillforts or Towns (Towns cost extra Wealth).
- March: as Dux March, with Militia and Comitates.
- Trade: +1 Resource per Town. Pay 1 in up to 2 Controlled spaces to add Prosperity.
- Battle: no cost.

**Civitates Feats (4.3)**

- Rule: up to 3 Controlled spaces. Plunder becomes Wealth, then pay 2 Resources for +1 Wealth, or, in a space with no Dux piece, 1 Wealth for -1 Dux Prestige, or, only in a Region (not a City) with a Civitates Stronghold, 5 Resources and a Refugees marker for +1 Population.
- Invite: as Dux Invite, with Hillforts.
- Reinforce (Battle only): pay 2 per space. Move 1D6-2 Troops into adjacent Battles, or Muster Militia free in a Battle space.
- Pillage (March or Battle): take Prosperity as Plunder and cut an enemy Stronghold owner's Renown or Prestige.

**Saxon Commands (3.4)**

- Raid: up to 3 target spaces. Pay 1 Renown for 1D4 or 2 for 3D4 Raiders. Forts on a Patrolled Sea subtract. Raiders take Plunder up to Population. Raiders may Battle.
- Return: remove Raiders and turn Plunder into Renown.
- March: Warbands only. Pay 1 Renown per destination (2 by sea).
- Battle: no cost. Warbands first take Plunder in non-Controlled Regions.

**Saxon Feats (4.4)**

- Settle: with Return or March. Roll 4-6 to turn Returning Raiders into Warbands. Then, only if the space already has a Saxon Settlement, pay 3 Renown for 1 Warband per Settlement there (plus 1 per Population if Saxon-Controlled at the outset), or trade 3 Warbands for +1 Population, or 2 Warbands for a Settlement.
- Surprise: Coup de Main on Assault, and Deep Raid one space past a valid target.
- Ravage: no Population limit on Plunder, 4-6 on D6 cuts Population, no Assault, and enemies get a free Battle.
- Shield Wall: Warbands need double losses in Field Battle.

**Scotti Commands (3.5)**

- Raid: up to 4 targets, 1D4 free or 2D4 for 1 Renown. Caledonia raids lose Forts in Carvetii and Textoverdi.
- Return, March and Battle: as Saxon. Settle needs 5-6.

**Scotti Feats (4.5)**

- Settle (5-6 roll, no Population conversion), Surprise.
- Ransom: roll 4-6 to move 1 Resource or Renown from a Stronghold owner to Scotti Renown.
- Entreat: pay 4 Renown in one Hills Region that holds a Scotti Warband to replace a Hillfort with a Settlement and up to 2 Militia with Warbands.

## Event cards and deck

- 83 cards: 72 Events, 4 Pivotal Events (cards 73 to 76), 7 Epoch cards (77 to 83: 4 Early, 3 Late) (Setup, 5.6, Playbook p.59-61).
- Deck build: deal Event cards into piles of 12. Shuffle one Epoch card among the bottom four of each pile (Setup).
- Dual Use: unshaded text often favors Britons, shaded often Barbarians. Any Faction may pick either (5.2).
- Capabilities (yellow triskele): last the rest of the game (5.3). Momentum (orange sail): lasts to the end of the next Epoch Round (5.4).
- Free Commands and Feats: no cost, no Eligibility change (5.5).
- Event rules: events do not place unavailable pieces, cannot break stacking or Population caps (5.1.1). Later events override earlier ones, except gratia (5.1.2).
- Epoch Events run in the Epoch Event phase (5.6). Many shift the Imperium (6.4).
- Pivotal Events (5.7, Playbook p.59-60): Conspiratio Barbarica (Scotti), Adventus Saxonum (Saxons), Coel Hen (Dux), Cymbrogi (Civitates).
- Epoch cards (Playbook): Magnus Maximus, Constantine III, Rescript of Honorius, Cutting the Ties, Tears of Epona, Rhiotamus and Pendragon (Playbook p.60-61). Magnus Maximus and Constantine III are Early (Setup). Tears of Epona is Late (Setup, Petty Tyrants). The Early or Late label of the others is not verified.
- Historical content: cards carry flavor text. The Playbook holds Chronicles and Event notes that mix legend and history (Arthur, Vortigern, Cerdic) (Playbook, designer view on legend).

## Map, spaces and tracks

- Space types: Region (21), City (2), Sea (4). Caledonia is an origin only (1.3.1, 1.3.2, 1.3.7, 1.3.9).
- Terrain: Clear, Fens, Hills. Fens is Home Terrain for Saxons. Hills is Home Terrain for Scotti, and for Militia and Comitates after Cymbrogi (3.6.1).
- Strongholds: each Region has 2 Sites, each City 1. Sites are Town or Hillfort type. Towns need Town Sites. No Hillforts in Cities (1.3.5).
- Population: per space. Feats and Events may add up to printed plus one, or cut to 0 (1.3.6). A drop creates a Refugees marker. Only 4 exist (1.3.6).
- Control: needs a Stronghold. Troops and Strongholds (not Raiders) must outnumber all others. Cities need only a Stronghold. Briton Control sums Dux and Civitates until Fragmentation (1.6).
- Prosperity: up to 2 x Population in two rows in a Region. One row in a City (1.7).
- Plunder: cubes carried by Units, 1 per Unit. Return, Annona and Upkeep convert Plunder (1.7.4).
- Roads: link spaces for Briton March and Intercept while Maintained (1.3.3, 6.8.4).
- Seas: Patrolled or not. Patrol subtracts Raiders by the count of Forts on bordering Regions (1.3.7, 3.4.1).
- Tracks: edge track 0 to 80 (Resources, Renown, Prestige, Wealth, victory markers) (1.8, 1.9). Imperium track with five states (6.8). Sequence of Play and Epoch Round tracks (2.2).

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

The Epoch Round happens when an Epoch card shows (6.0). Phases in order:

1. Annona: Britons pay Foederati Warbands (Dux and Civitates in turn). Paid Warbands get 1 Plunder each, and unpaid Warbands lose their markers. Plunder on Foederati goes to their Nation's Renown. Released Warbands then Plunder (6.1).
2. Imperium: Hoarding moves Resources to Wealth up to half the Towns rounded up (6.2.1). Then check track (6.2.2):
   - At Roman Rule: Briton Control 30 or less or Prosperity plus Prestige 55 or less moves to Autonomy.
   - Briton Control 20 or less or Prosperity plus Prestige 30 or less sets Fragmentation.
   - A drop halves Prestige and Wealth, rounded up.
   - Military Dominance shifts to Civilian if Wealth exceeds Prestige by 10. Civilian shifts back if Wealth does not exceed Prestige by 6.
3. Victory: check 7.2 and end if met. In the final Epoch, run Recovery (skipped in the Barbarian Conspiracy scenario and at Fragmentation, 6.3) and use margins (6.3, 7.3).
4. Epoch Event (6.4).
5. Revenue (6.5): Dux Prestige +5 (Roman Rule) or +2 (Autonomy) at Military Dominance. At Fragmentation Dux Resources equal Dux-controlled Prosperity plus Towns plus Forts. Civitates add Resources equal to Briton-controlled Prosperity plus Towns (Hillforts after Cymbrogi). Imperial Taxation at Roman Rule: -30 (Military) or -20 (Civilian) Briton Resources. Saxons gain Renown equal to Saxon-controlled Prosperity. Scotti gain 1 per non-Foederati Settlement.
6. Upkeep (6.6): Dux stows Plunder, pays 1 per Fort or removes it, pays 10 for Roads at Autonomy, relocates Cavalry to Forts and returns Casualties by Imperium fraction. Civitates remove 1 in 3 Militia per space, pay 1 Wealth per 3 Comitates, drop half the Refugees and relocate. Barbarians Return Plunder, relocate Warbands, and reset Renown to at least 10 (Saxons) or 6 (Scotti).
7. Recovery and Reset: Prosperity added equal to Population in each Controlled Region (top row only at Roman Rule). Cities refill. Civitates Plunder becomes Wealth. Momentum is discarded and all Factions become Eligible (6.7).

## Non-player (bot) system

Included (8.0). It runs any missing Faction, up to three, and supports solitaire (8.9).

- Flowcharts: each bot has a flowchart. Numbered priorities are sequential. Lettered priorities are tie breakers (8.1.4).
- Command or Event: bots decline Ineffective Events and "raven" Events. "Shield" Events have priority, and a bot may Pass when the next card has a shield for it. "Sword" Events have special instructions (8.1.1, 8.2.1, 8.5.2).
- Dual Use: Barbarian bots pick shaded, Britons unshaded (8.2.2).
- A bot on a Limited Command gets a full Command and Feat (8.1.2).
- Pivotal Events: played as soon as the precondition holds and the card instruction is met (8.1.3).
- Randomness: equal candidates use a die roll or the Random Spaces Table (8.3.2, 8.3.3). Several die checks gate actions.
- Per Faction order of checks:
  - Scotti: Return, priority Event, Battle, March or Raid, Event (8.5).
  - Saxons: Return, priority Event, March, Battle, Raid, Event (8.6).
  - Civitates: Battle, priority Event, March, Muster, Event, Trade (8.7).
  - Dux: Intercept, priority Event, Battle, Train, Event, March (8.8).
- Battle Tactics table gives Evade, Ambush, Withdraw and Assault choices (8.4.2).
- Briton bots can attack a player Briton near victory, causing civil war (8.7.1, 8.8.3).
- Solitaire: play one Faction, or both Britons or both Barbarians. Entropy turns one Faction into a bot at Fragmentation or after both Barbarian Pivotals. Options: Introductory, Easy, Difficult (8.9).

## Unique mechanics

- Imperium track: three statuses and Dominance. It changes costs, victory thresholds, Control sharing and Prosperity recovery (6.8).
- Britons share Control and Resources, then split at Fragmentation (1.5, 1.6.1, 6.8).
- Control needs a Stronghold, and Raiders never count (1.6).
- Raiders as a separate Unit class that are rolled in by dice, subtracted by Forts and Patrolled Seas, then Returned for Renown (3.4.1, 3.4.2).
- Prosperity and Plunder cubes. The same gold moves from land to Units to Renown (1.7).
- Foederati markers: Barbarian pieces hired by Britons and paid in Annona (1.4.3, 6.1).
- Stronghold assault with Garrison, Escalade, Storm, Siege and Coup de Main (3.6.4, 3.6.6).
- Home Terrain with Evade and Ambush rolls (3.6.2).
- Wealth, Prestige and Dominance give the Britons a political fight (1.8, 6.2.2).
- Pivotal Event trump order (2.3.8).
- Spaces are chosen before any resolution (3.1).
- Army Preemption (1.5.2) and Tribute that converts Resources to Renown (1.5.2).

## Relevance

- Raid, Return and Plunder cycle (raid, then bank loot or lose it) could model PKK attacks and withdrawals to bases. Inferred.
- Raider placement reduced by a border-patrol count (Forts on a Sea) could model border defenses and mountain crossings. Inferred.
- Imperium track and the Dominance split suggest a government track that changes costs and shares power between civil and military arms. Inferred.
- For a digital Falling Sky: the Epoch cards inside the deck, Pivotal trump order and bot flowcharts with die-gated checks are reusable patterns. Inferred.
