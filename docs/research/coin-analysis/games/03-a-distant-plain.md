# A Distant Plain (COIN Vol III): summary

## Identity

- Title: A Distant Plain. COIN Series, Volume III (Rules p.1).
- Design: Brian Train and Volko Ruhnke (Rules p.1). Developer: Mike Bertucelli (Playbook, Credits).
- Rules file: 3rd Printing, (c) 2013, 2015 GMT Games (Rules p.1). Playbook file: 2nd Printing.
- Setting: modern Afghanistan, with parts of Pakistan (1.0, 1.3.5).
- Scale: national. 22 Areas amalgamate 34 provinces (Playbook p.27). One Population point is about 1 million Afghans (1.3.3). Total Afghan Population is 25 before Returnees (1.9).
- Players: 1 to 4 (1.0, 1.5). Cards: 78 (1.2).
- Scenarios (Set Up, p.23-24):

| Scenario | Years | Deck |
|---|---|---|
| Short "Obama's War" | 2009-2013 | Remove 24 Event and 2 Propaganda cards; 4 piles |
| Main "Taliban Resurgent" | 2005-2013 | Remove 12 Event and 1 Propaganda card; 5 piles |
| Extended "The Afghan Insurgency" | 2003-2013 | Use all cards; 6 piles |

- Start values differ by scenario. Example: Main starts with Aid 15, Patronage 5, Resources Govt 30 / Warlords 15 / Taliban 10 (Scenarios p.24).
- Setup text on p.23 shows older year labels (2009-2012, 2005-2012, 2003-2012). The scenario page shows 2013. Not verified which is correct.
- Optional rules: No Non-Player (1.5), Sudden Propaganda (2.3.7), Victory Deception (7.3), No Capabilities and Open Deployment (Set Up p.23).

## Factions

Four Factions. Two are COIN (Coalition, Government). Two are Insurgent (Taliban, Warlords). Insurgents are enemy to all others (1.5).

| Faction | Type | Victory threshold (7.2) | Margin formula (7.3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coalition | COIN, foreign | Pop in Support + Coalition pieces Available > 30 | Support + Available - 30 |
| Government | COIN, Afghan | COIN-Controlled Pop + Patronage > 35 | COIN Pop + Patronage - 35 |
| Taliban | Insurgent | Pop in Opposition + Taliban Bases on map > 20 | Opposition + Bases - 20 |
| Warlords | Insurgent | Uncontrolled Afghan Pop > 15 AND Warlord Resources > 40 | Lower of (Uncontrolled Pop - 15) and (Resources - 40) |

- Coalition (tan): no Resources of its own. It spends Government Resources (1.8, 3.1). Forces: 20 Troops, 6 Bases (Set Up p.23). It enters and leaves the map only by Surge (4.2.1). Pieces it loses to Attack go to Casualties (3.3.3).
- Government (blue): Resources 0 to 75. Forces: 30 Troops, 30 Police, 3 Bases. Patronage 0 to 40 counts toward victory (1.8). Aid 0 to 75 adds to its Resources each Propaganda Round (6.3.2).
- Taliban (black): Resources 0 to 75. Forces: 30 Guerrillas, 12 Bases. Only Taliban may stack in Pakistan (1.4.2).
- Warlords (green): Resources 0 to 75. Forces: 15 Guerrillas, 12 Bases.
- Taliban and Warlord players may remove their own pieces to Available while they act, to re-place them (1.4.1).
- Players may not transfer Resources between their own Factions. Non-Coalition Factions may give Resources to others while one of them acts (1.5.1). Government may convert Patronage to Warlord Resources 1 for 1 (1.5.1).
- Control: COIN (Coalition and Government together) or Taliban alone Control a Province or Kabul when its pieces exceed all other pieces combined (1.7).
- With fewer players, leftover Factions are Non-players. A no-Non-Player option exists for learning (1.5).
- Ranking: Non-player win means all players lose. Else rank by margin. Ties go to Non-players, then Warlords, Government, Taliban (7.1).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- No hands. Play one card from the deck. One card ahead is revealed (1.1, 2.2).
- Each Event card shows Faction order by symbol, left to right (2.3.2).
- Eligible Factions act in card order. 1st Eligible may Pass, execute the Event, or execute an Operation with or without a Special Activity (2.3.4).
- 2nd Eligible options depend on the 1st (2.3.4):

| 1st Eligible did | 2nd Eligible may |
|---|---|
| Operation only | Limited Operation |
| Operation and Special Activity | Limited Operation or the Event |
| Event | Operation, with Special Activity if desired |

- Limited Operation: one space, no Special Activity. Patrol, Sweep and March may draw from many spaces but end in one destination (2.3.5).
- Pass: Insurgent gains +1 Resource. A COIN Faction gives +3 Resources to the Government. A Passer stays Eligible (2.3.3).
- After the card, a Faction that executed an Operation or Event becomes Ineligible next card (2.3.6). Free Operations do not change Eligibility (3.1.2).
- Final Event card before the last Propaganda card: player Operations must be Limited, with no Sweep and no March (2.3.8).
- Non-players ignore that restriction (8.1).
- Propaganda cards trigger a Propaganda Round (2.4). Never run two Rounds in a row (6.0).
- Change from series core (Playbook p.11): Coalition has no own Resources. A Coalition Pass gives the Government +3 Resources (2.3.3).
- Sudden Propaganda option: when Propaganda shows next, swap the two showing cards and run the Round at once (2.3.7).

## Operations and Special Activities

COIN Operations: Coalition and Government share four (3.2). Cost in Government Resources.

| Operation | Effect | Cost and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Train | Place up to 6 Govt cubes in a Province with a COIN Base or in Kabul. In 1 space: Civic Action, or replace 3 cubes with a Govt Base, or (Coalition in Kabul) move 3 Patronage to Govt Resources (3.2.1) | 3 per space. Coalition needs Coalition pieces and places cubes only at a Coalition Base (3.2.1) |
| Patrol | Move cubes along LoCs or into Kabul. Activate 1 Guerrilla per cube on each LoC. One free Assault on a LoC (3.2.2) | 3 total for Govt, 0 for Coalition (3.2.2) |
| Sweep | Move adjacent Troops in. Activate 1 Guerrilla per cube. Coalition Sweep counts all cubes if a Coalition piece is present (3.2.3) | 3 per destination. Coalition pays only where Govt Troops move. Govt pays Warlords 1 per LoC entered with Guerrillas (3.2.3) |
| Assault | Remove Active Guerrillas, then Bases. Govt: 1 per 2 cubes (3 in Mountain). Coalition: 1 per cube (3.2.4) | 3 per space (Coalition Assault: 3 Government Resources per space only if Government cubes are used; Coalition-only cubes cost nothing). Provinces count Troops only. +6 Aid per Taliban Base removed by Govt (3.2.4) |

Insurgent Operations: Taliban and Warlords share four (3.3).

| Operation | Effect | Cost and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Rally | Place 1 Guerrilla or swap 2 for a Base. At a Base: flip Guerrillas Underground or place up to Bases + Pop. Taliban may buy Sharia in 1 Base space (3.3.1) | 1 per space. Taliban only in Neutral, Opposition or Pashtun spaces (3.3.1) |
| March | Move Guerrillas to adjacent spaces. Active if the destination rule and the more-than-3 count rule both trigger (3.3.2) | 1 per Province or City entered. Taliban may chain Marches through Pashtun spaces (3.3.2) |
| Attack | Activate own Guerrillas, roll d6. On roll up to own Guerrilla count, remove 2 enemy pieces. On a 1, place a Guerrilla (3.3.3) | 1 per space |
| Terror | Province or Kabul: Terror marker. Taliban shift 1 toward Opposition. Warlords set to Neutral. LoC: Sabotage marker (3.3.4) | 1 per Province or Kabul, 0 for LoCs. Needs an Underground Guerrilla (3.3.4) |

Special Activities (4.0). Each accompanies an Operation. None on a Limited Operation (4.1).

- Coalition:
  - Surge (4.2.1): only with Train. Up to 3 spaces that include Kabul or COIN-Controlled Provinces (never Pakistan). Move Coalition pieces among them and Available. Then optionally roll d6 and split it among Aid and Warlord Resources.
  - Air Lift (4.2.2): move Coalition Troops and up to 3 Govt Troops among any 3 spaces.
  - Air Strike (4.2.3): with Patrol, Sweep or Assault. Up to 3 spaces except Kabul. Remove 1 Active Guerrilla, or 1 Base only if the targeted Faction has no Guerrillas there at all. A strike in Pakistan shifts Islamabad toward Sponsorship.
- Government:
  - Govern (4.3.1): with Train or Patrol. In 1 or 2 COIN-Controlled spaces at Support or 0 Pop. Either place a +1 Pop Returnees marker (6 exist) or move the space's Pop from Aid to Patronage (max 40) and set the space Neutral. The Patronage option needs no Coalition Base there.
  - Transport (4.3.2): move Govt Troops along LoCs, then 1 step off. Pay Warlords 1 per space entered with Guerrillas.
  - Eradicate (4.3.3): with Train, Patrol or Assault. In 1 or 2 COIN-Controlled Provinces with Govt cubes and a Warlord Base. +3 Aid, +1 Patronage, remove the Base, shift 1 toward Opposition. In a Pashtun space, place a Taliban Guerrilla.
- Taliban:
  - Extort (4.4.1): in spaces with an Underground Taliban Guerrilla where Taliban outnumber enemies. Activate 1 such Guerrilla per space. +1 Resource, or the Econ value on an unSabotaged LoC. With a Warlord Base there, take 2 per Base from Warlords.
  - Infiltrate (4.4.2): with Rally or March. In 1 space with an Underground Taliban Guerrilla: remove 2 non-Coalition pieces or replace 1 with a Guerrilla. Patronage falls 1 per Govt piece removed.
  - Ambush (4.4.3): with Attack. Up to 3 spaces. Auto-success with 1 Underground Guerrilla; also gains a free Guerrilla.
- Warlords:
  - Cultivate (4.5.1): with Rally or March. Place a Base in a Province selected for the Rally or entered by the March, with Pop above 0 and more Warlord Guerrillas than Police.
  - Traffic (4.5.2): with Rally or March. In spaces with 1 or 2 Warlord Bases and no Coalition. +1 Warlord Resource per Base. +1 Taliban Resource if Taliban-Controlled. +1 Patronage if COIN-Controlled.
  - Suborn (4.5.3): up to 3 spaces. Pay 2 Resources per space. Remove up to 3 Govt cubes, or a Govt Base, or a Taliban Guerrilla; or flip Guerrillas Underground.

## Event cards and deck

- 78 cards: 72 Event cards and 6 Propaganda cards (1.2, Set Up p.23).
- Deck build: set Propaganda aside. Remove a scenario-set number of random Events. Remove 1 or 2 Propaganda cards as the scenario states. Deal the rest into equal piles. Shuffle one Propaganda card into each pile. Stack the piles (Set Up p.23).
- Removed Capability cards still take effect at start: unshaded for Coalition, shaded for Taliban. No Capabilities option skips this (Set Up p.23, Playbook p.5).
- Each card shows a four-symbol Faction order. Some symbols have gray halos for Non-player instructions (2.3.2, 8.3.1).
- Dual Use: most Events have unshaded text and shaded text. Any Faction may pick either (5.2). Unshaded often favors COIN. Shaded often favors Insurgents.
- Capabilities (5.3): marked COALITION or TALIBAN CAPABILITIES. A marker goes in that Faction's box on the played side. Effect lasts for the rest of the game. Examples: ISR, Predators, Reapers, Aerostats, Find Fix Finish, Village Stability Operations (Coalition); Roadside IEDs, Night Letters, Urban Specialists, Car Bombs, Suicide Bombers, Accidental Guerrillas, ID Cards (Taliban) (Playbook, Event Text and Background). Total counts not verified.
- Momentum (5.4): the card stays in view until the next Reset phase, then is discarded (6.6). Any number may be active. Talks and Tribal Elders use markers on cards 24 and 70 per 5.4. The playbook list shows Tribal Elders as card 45 and card 70 as Contractor Surge. Mismatch not verified.
- Free Operations (5.5) cost nothing and do not change Eligibility.
- Event rules: Event beats rules text, but never breaks availability, stacking, caps of 75 Aid or Resources and 40 Patronage, or Faction-only action types (5.1.1).
- The newest played Event wins over an older one (5.1.2).
- Historical content: each card has flavor text. The playbook gives background and sources per card (Playbook, Event Text and Background). Examples: Operation Iraqi Freedom, Karzai, Loya Jirga, Mullah Omar, Gulbuddin Hekmatyar, Haqqani, Tajiks, Hazara, Dostum, Poppy Crop Failure.
- Card 71 is titled Coup! and is numbered with the Event cards (Playbook, Event Text and Background). Its effect is not verified.

## Map, spaces and tracks

- Spaces: Provinces, the City of Kabul, and Lines of Communication (LoCs). All can hold forces (1.3.1). Towns only end LoCs and are not spaces (1.3.6).
- Provinces: Pop 0, 1 or 2. Mountain or Plains. Some are Pashtun (1.3.2). Kabul: Pop 3, Pashtun (1.3.3).
- LoCs: Econ value 1 to 4. Total Econ is 12. Never Pashtun. No Bases on LoCs (1.3.4, 1.4.2, 6.3.2).
- Pakistan: three Provinces where only Taliban may stack. They have no Support or Opposition (1.3.5, 1.6.2). The playbook names Waziristan, Northwest Frontier and Balochistan (Scenarios p.24).
- Adjacency: bordering spaces, Provinces across a LoC, and LoCs or Provinces across a Town (1.3.6).
- Stacking: at most 2 Bases of any Faction in a Province or Kabul (1.4.2).
- Support and Opposition: three levels only: Support, Neutral, Opposition. No Active/Passive split (1.6.1, Playbook p.27). Only Provinces with Pop above 0 and Kabul can shift (1.6.2).
- Control markers: COIN, Taliban, or none (1.7).
- Returnees: six +1 Pop markers raise a space's Pop (1.3.2, 4.3.1).
- Markers: Terror and Sabotage (30 total), Overflow, Capability, Deception (1.9, 3.3.4).
- Edge tracks (1.8, 1.9):
  - Resources per Faction, 0 to 75.
  - Aid, 0 to 75.
  - Patronage, 0 to 40.
  - Support + Available Coalition (threshold 30).
  - Opposition + Taliban Bases (threshold 20).
  - COIN-Controlled Pop + Patronage (threshold 35).
  - Uncontrolled Afghan Pop (threshold 15).
- Islamabad track (6.6.1), left to right: Hostility, Pressure, Tolerance, Backing, Sponsorship, plus a Hard flip side.
- Sequence of Play track on the map holds Eligible and Ineligible boxes (2.2).

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

Phases in order (6.0). Rules text skips a 6.2 heading, so a phase may be missing from the text. Not verified.

1. Victory Phase (6.1): any Faction at its threshold ends the game. Players with several Factions stop only if all of them qualify (1.5). Final Round uses margins (7.3).
2. Resources Phase (6.3):
   - Sabotage each unSabotaged LoC where Taliban Guerrillas exceed non-Taliban pieces (6.3.1).
   - Govt gains Aid plus Econ of unSabotaged LoCs (6.3.2).
   - Taliban gain 1 per Base on map. Warlords gain 1 per Base plus 1 per Guerrilla on an unSabotaged LoC (6.3.3).
   - Aid falls by 3 per piece in Casualties (6.3.4).
   - Cap is 75.
3. Support Phase (6.4):
   - Civic Action: Coalition then Government spend Govt Resources. 3 per Terror marker removed or Support level shifted. Needs COIN Control, Troops and Police. Faction with more Troops decides the amount; Coalition on ties (6.4.1).
   - Sharia: Taliban spend 1 per Terror removed or Opposition shift. Needs Taliban Control (6.4.2).
4. Redeploy Phase (6.5):
   - Desertion: Govt removes 1 in 3 cubes in each space (6.5.1).
   - Coalition then Government move Troops from LoCs and Provinces without COIN Bases to COIN-Controlled Base spaces or Kabul (6.5.2).
   - Govt may move Police to LoCs or COIN-Controlled spaces (6.5.3).
   - Taliban Guerrillas on LoCs or in Provinces without Taliban Bases move to Taliban Base spaces under Taliban Control or Pashtun Pakistan spaces. Kabul Guerrillas stay (6.5.4).
   - Update Control (6.5.5). If this is the final Round, end (6.5.6).
5. Reset Phase (6.6):
   - Casualties: remove all Bases and half the Troops (round down) from the game. Others go to Coalition Available.
   - Islamabad flips off Hard, else shifts 1 toward Backing.
   - All Factions Eligible. Remove Terror and Sabotage. Discard Momentum cards. Flip Guerrillas Underground. Play the next card.
- After the last Propaganda card without a threshold win, highest margin wins (2.4.1, 7.3).

## Non-player (bot) system

- All four Factions have flowcharts. One bot pair can run in any seat count (1.5, Playbook p.12, p.29). Rules 8.0.
- Decision order, each Faction (8.1, 8.6.2, 8.7.2, 8.8.2, 8.9.2):
  1. Capability or Momentum card per 8.1.
  2. Event if effective and it meets a Faction test (for example Coalition: shift Support up, remove Taliban Bases).
  3. Pass if resources are too low (Warlords at 0, Government under 3).
  4. Else Operation with Special Activity, using a fixed flowchart order.
- Non-players never choose Limited Operations. They run a full Operation and Special Activity (8.1).
- Dual Use: Insurgents use shaded text. COIN uses unshaded text. Single-text unshaded Events count as shaded for Insurgents (8.3.2).
- Capability play uses a die roll against unplayed Propaganda cards (8.1).
- Targeting guidelines (8.2.1): hit players before bots, then the player with the best margin, then random. Bases before other pieces. Underground before Active.
- Flowchart bullets: numbered are sequential. Lettered are nested tie-breakers (8.2.3).
- Priorities per Faction:
  - Coalition (8.6): Sweep, else Assault, else Train. Special Activities Air Lift, Air Strike or Surge.
  - Government (8.7): Assault, else Patrol, else Train, else Sweep. Special Activities Eradicate, Govern or Transport.
  - Taliban (8.8): Terror, else Rally, else March or Attack. Special Activities Extort, Infiltrate or Ambush.
  - Warlords (8.9): Rally, else March, else Terror, else Attack. Special Activities Cultivate, Traffic or Suborn.
- Spend limit: Government, Coalition and Taliban often use up to two-thirds of Resources unless under 9 (8.6.5, 8.7.5, 8.8.5).
- Randomness: Random Spaces map. Roll the green die for a region, any die for a space, then follow arrows to the next legal space (8.4). LoCs go by Econ first (8.4.1).
- Each bot has Propaganda procedures (Civic Action, Sharia, Redeploy) (8.6.6, 8.7.7, 8.8.7).
- Solitaire win test: if any Non-player has a positive margin at a Victory Phase (from the second Round on), all players lose (8.5, 7.1).
- Cross-references to solitaire victory differ in the text: the rule sits at 8.5, while 6.1 cites 8.9 and 7.1 cites 8.8. Not verified which is current.

## Unique mechanics

- First foreign COIN Faction. Its victory counts Available pieces, so withdrawal scores (Playbook p.11, 7.2).
- Two COIN Factions with a shared Control and separate goals. Coalition spends Government money (3.1).
- Patronage and Aid: Govern moves Aid into Patronage. A Coalition Base blocks it (4.3.1, Playbook p.11).
- Returnees add permanent +1 Pop (4.3.1).
- Surge as the only way to move Coalition pieces on or off the map (4.2.1).
- Casualties: Coalition losses cut Aid and half the Troops leave the game (3.3.3, 6.3.4, 6.6).
- Pashtun ethnicity changes Taliban and Warlord Rally and March rules (3.3.1, 3.3.2).
- Pakistan sanctuary with the Islamabad track, set by Events and Air Strikes (6.6.1).
- Warlords amalgam faction wins by keeping Control away from both sides plus money (7.2).
- Warlords earn money from COIN transit. Government pays them on Sweep and Transport (3.2.3, 4.3.2).
- Three-level Support only: no Active versions (Playbook p.27).
- Taliban Sharia during Rally (3.3.1).
- Optional Deception markers hide victory margins (7.3).
- Bots for all four Factions (Playbook p.12).

## Relevance

- Inferred: the Pakistan-style sanctuary plus a track of host-state posture suits a PKK game (bases in Iraq, Syria, Iran). Reuse the Islamabad track idea with Event-driven shifts and a Reset drift.
- Inferred: ethnic space tags (Pashtun versus non-Pashtun) match Kurdish versus Turkish provinces. They could change Rally and March rules the same way.
- Inferred: Patronage and Aid as a victory component for a state Faction, plus village-guard style militias as a Warlords-like amalgam faction that wins by keeping spaces uncontrolled.
- Inferred: for digital Falling Sky, the `Pass` and Limited Operation structure, the margin-based ranking (7.3) and the numbered-plus-nested bot priority format (8.2.3) can be coded directly. The two-thirds spend limit and Random Spaces map can serve bot logic.
