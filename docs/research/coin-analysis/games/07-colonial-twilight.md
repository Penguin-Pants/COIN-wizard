# Colonial Twilight (COIN Vol. VII)

## Identity

- Title: *Colonial Twilight: The French-Algerian War, 1954-62* (1.0).
- Series: COIN Series, Volume VII (cover; 1.0).
- Designer: Brian Train (rulebook cover). Playbook credits: Developer Jordan Kehrer; Non-Player Faction Rules VPJ Arponen; Art Director Rodger B. MacGowan; Map Chechu Nieto; Cards and charts Charles Kibler; Rules and Counters Mark Simonitch; VASSAL Joel Toppen and Jordan Kehrer (Playbook, Credits).
- Edition: Rules "Revised May 2018" (cover). Playbook "Revised Oct. 2018". Copyright 2017 GMT Games.
- Setting: the last eight years of French Algeria, as a department of France (1.0).
- Scale: spaces are Sectors, Cities and two Countries. One Population point is about 300,000 people (1.3.3 design note).
- Players: 2 (Government and FLN). Rules for a non-player FLN allow solitaire play (1.0, 8.0).
- Components: 22 x 34 inch board, 71 cards, 91 wooden pieces, 2 dice (1.2).
- Scenarios (10.0):

| Scenario | Years | Event cards used | Campaigns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short: The End Game | 1960-62 | 36 random cards | 3 piles of 12 |
| Medium: Midgame Development | 1957-62 | 48 random cards | 4 piles of 12 |
| Full: Algerie Francaise! | 1954-62 | all 60 cards | 5 piles of 12 |

- Each pile gets one Propaganda card shuffled in (10.0). FLN starts First Eligible in all scenarios (10.0).
- The non-player rules are tuned for the Short scenario (8.0).

## Factions

| | Government | FLN |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Government (counterinsurgent) | Insurgent |
| Pieces | French Troops (dark blue), French Police (light blue), Algerian Troops (dark green), Algerian Police (light green), Bases (1.4) | Guerrillas (Underground or Active), Bases (1.4, 1.4.3) |
| Resources | 0 to 50 (1.8) | 0 to 50 (1.8) |
| Extra track | Commitment Level on Edge Track (1.8) | France Track and Border Zone effects (1.3.8) |
| Victory marker | Total Support + Commitment (1.9) | Total Opposition + FLN Bases on map (1.9) |
| Threshold | Exceeds 35 (7.2) | Exceeds 30 (7.2) |
| Margin | Support + Commitment - 35 (7.3) | Opposition + Bases - 30 (7.3) |

- Total Support = Pop of Cities and Sectors at Support. Total Opposition = Pop of Cities and Sectors at Opposition in Algeria (1.6.2).
- Morocco and Tunisia Pop never counts for Opposition (1.6.3).
- Algerian cubes are built by Train. The FLN can Subvert them (1.5.1).
- Only FLN pieces may enter Morocco and Tunisia (1.3.4).
- Placing: only from Available, unless the type is not Available. Then pieces may come from the map. The FLN cannot take pieces from Morocco or Tunisia for this (1.4.1).
- Stacking: max two Bases per space. Max three Bases in each Country, by one Event (1.4.2).
- Control: more pieces than the other player (1.7).
- Resource floor: a player may not go below 0. Excess loss is ignored (1.8).
- Victory Deception Option: each player draws two hidden markers. Empty Threat (no effect), Hidden Agenda (+1 margin), Hidden Asset (turn a Limited Op into Op + Special Activity) (7.3).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- One card at a time. No hands (1.1).
- Two Factions only. One is First Eligible and one is Second Eligible (2.3).
- Initiative Track boxes: Event, Limited Op, Op Only, Op + Special Activity, Pass (2.3.2).
- First Eligible options: Event, Op with or without Special Activity, or Pass (2.3.2).
- The cylinder goes to the box that matches what the player did (2.3.2).
  - Op in one location, no Special Activity: Limited Op box.
  - Op in more than one location, no Special Activity: Op Only box.
  - Op with Special Activity (any number of spaces): Op + Special Activity box.
- Second Eligible picks a box next to the First Eligible cylinder (2.3.2). Then it does what that box allows.
- Adjust eligibility (2.3.5):
  - If First Eligible did Op + Special Activity or Op in multiple spaces, the Second Eligible becomes First Eligible.
  - If First Eligible did anything else (Limited Op, Event, Pass), it stays First Eligible.
- Pass gives +1 Resource to FLN and +2 to Government (2.3.3). Both may Pass. Then the order does not change (2.3.3).
- Limited Op: one location. A Garrison, Sweep or March may use many source spaces but only one destination (2.3.4).
- The France Track and Border Zone Track count as "locations". Rally on the France Track plus one space counts as Op Only (2.3.5).
- Pivotal Events can replace the current card if their preconditions hold and the First Eligible player has not yet chosen (2.3.7).
  - Second Eligible has priority if both want to play one (2.3.7).
  - The player who plays one becomes or stays First Eligible and uses the Event box. The other player may take Op + Special Activity or Pass (2.3.7).
- Changes from the series core:
  - No look-ahead at the next card (Playbook p.12, "we did remove the look one card ahead option").
  - The Pass reward differs by Faction (2.3.3).
  - The FLN is First Eligible after every Propaganda Round (6.5).

## Operations and Special Activities

### Government Operations (3.2)

| Op | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Train | 2 per location (3.2.1) | In Cities and Sectors with a Government Base: place up to 4 Algerian cubes. In one space: Pacify (remove Terror or shift one level to Support, Government Control needed, 2 Resources per step, even if free). Or shift the France or Border Zone Track one box. |
| Garrison | 2 total (3.2.2) | Move up to 6 Police among populated or Resettled spaces. In one space, Activate 1 Underground Guerrilla per Police (1 per 2 Police in Mountain). |
| Sweep | 2 per space (3.2.3) | Move adjacent Troops in. Activate 1 Underground Guerrilla per cube (1 per 2 in Mountain). |
| Assault | 2 per space (3.2.4) | Remove 1 enemy piece per Troop (1 per 2 in Mountain). Police count in Cities and Border Sectors. Active Guerrillas first, then Bases. Base removal gives +1 Commitment. |

- Recall de Gaulle widens Train to any populated Sector with Government Control, Troops and Police (3.2.1, 5.1.7).

### Government Special Activities (4.2)

- Deploy (with Train): move up to 6 French pieces among Available and up to 3 selected Cities or Sectors with a Government Base or Government Control (4.2.1). Or Resettle one Pop 1 Sector with a Government Base or Control: it becomes Pop 0, permanently Neutral, and costs Resources each Propaganda Round (4.2.1, 6.2.1).
- Troop Lift (any Op): move French Troops among any 3 Algerian spaces (4.2.2).
- Neutralize (with Garrison or Sweep): up to 2 spaces with Troops and Police. Remove up to 2 Active enemy pieces. Shift each space one level toward Opposition. Add Terror if already at Oppose. Base removal gives +1 Commitment (4.2.3).

### FLN Operations (3.3)

| Op | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Rally | 1 per location (3.3.1) | Place 1 Guerrilla, or replace 2 Guerrillas with a Base. With a Base present: place Guerrillas up to Pop + Bases, or flip all Underground. In one space: Agitate (1 Resource per Terror removed or level shifted; needs FLN Base or Control). Or shift the France Track one box. |
| March | 1 per destination space (3.3.2) | Move Guerrillas to adjacent spaces. No further move after crossing a Wilaya or International Border. Guerrillas go Active if the destination is at Support or the group crosses an International Border (a Wilaya crossing alone does not), and group size plus Government cubes (plus Border Zone status when crossing an International Border) exceeds 3. |
| Attack | 1 per space (3.3.3) | Activate all Guerrillas. Roll a die. A roll at or under the Guerrilla count removes up to 2 enemy pieces (Police, Troops, Bases). Each French piece removed costs one Guerrilla (Attrition). A roll of 1 places an Underground Guerrilla. Base removal gives -1 Commitment. |
| Terror | 1 per space (3.3.4) | Activate 1 Underground Guerrilla. If no Terror marker is there, place one and set the space to Neutral. 12 Terror markers exist. |

### FLN Special Activities (4.3)

- Extort (any Op): in populated spaces with FLN Control and an Underground Guerrilla, Activate 1 Guerrilla to gain +1 Resource per space (4.3.1).
- Subvert (with Rally, March or Terror): up to 2 spaces, each with at least one Underground Guerrilla and Algerian cubes. Remove up to 2 Algerian cubes in total. Or, in one space, replace 1 Algerian Police with an Underground Guerrilla. The Guerrilla is not Activated (4.3.2).
- Ambush (with Attack): up to 2 spaces. Activate only one Guerrilla. The Attack succeeds with no roll but removes 1 piece. No Attrition (4.3.3).

## Event cards and deck

- Deck: 71 cards (1.2). The playbook lists 60 numbered Events and 6 Pivotal Events (Playbook p.15 onward). The rest are 5 Propaganda cards (inferred from 71 - 66 and the five piles in 10.0).
- Full deck build: all 60 Events in 5 piles of 12, one Propaganda card per pile (10.0). The six Pivotal Events are not part of these piles. They are held by their Factions (Government: Mobilization, Recall de Gaulle, Coup d'Etat. FLN: Morocco and Tunisia Independent, OAS, Suez Crisis) and are played when their preconditions hold (5.1; table below). Inferred from the pile counts in 10.0.
- Short and Medium: random subsets. Unused cards stay hidden. Optionally, players reveal unused Capability cards and apply the unshaded side for Government or the shaded side for FLN at start (10.0).
- Dual Use: most Events have an unshaded and a shaded text. The player picks one (5.2).
- Capabilities: lasting markers in the Capabilities box. They last for the rest of the game (5.3).
  - Examples: SAS, Commandos, Taleb, Napalm (Playbook Event list).
  - One Event removes a Capability (Change in Tactics) (5.3).
- Two Dual Use Capabilities, OAS and Torture, bind both players (5.3).
  - Torture: Government gains short-term military power. It costs Commitment (5.3.2, Playbook).
  - OAS: either player may use it in place of a Special Activity. It places Terror and costs Commitment and FLN Resources (5.3.1).
- Momentum: lasts until the next Reset Phase. Some cards are Government or FLN only. Some affect both ("DUAL MOMENTUM") (5.4, Playbook).
- Free Operations from Events cost nothing and do not change eligibility. Pacification and Agitation still cost Resources (5.5, 3.1.2).
- Event text beats the rules, but not stacking, Available limits or the 50 cap (5.1.1).
- Pivotal Events (5.1):
  - Government: Mobilization, Recall de Gaulle, Coup d'Etat.
  - FLN: Morocco and Tunisia Independent, OAS, Suez Crisis.

| Pivotal | Precondition | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Mobilization (5.1.6) | Opposition + Bases at least 15 (Full only) | Move Out of Play pieces to Available, up to half Commitment, free. Unlocks Resettlement and the FLN Morocco and Tunisia card. |
| Morocco and Tunisia Independent (5.1.8) | Mobilization played | Countries become FLN sanctuaries. Border Zone Track starts at 0. Police in Border Sectors count as Troops in Assaults. |
| Coup d'Etat (5.1.5) | None. Once per Campaign. Government keeps the card. | Both roll 1d6. Government high: cancel Recall de Gaulle and OAS, gain Commitment equal to the low die and Resources equal to the sum. FLN high: remove Troops or Commitment equal to the low die. Tie: nothing. |
| Recall de Gaulle (5.1.7) | Coup d'Etat played once | Wider Train. Government gains Resources equal to Total Support, but not from Available French pieces. French Casualties stop cutting Commitment. Unlocks OAS. |
| OAS (5.3.1) | Recall de Gaulle in effect | Dual Capability, see above. |
| Suez Crisis (5.1.9) | FLN has 6+ Resources | Government removes 1d6 French Troops. Both lose 6 Resources. FLN gains 6 Resources at the next Propaganda Round. Troops return in Redeploy. |

- Historical content: each Event has flavor text and a background note in the Playbook (Playbook p.15 onward). Examples: Quadrillage, Leadership Snatch (1956 Ben Bella capture), Peace of the Brave, 5th Bureau, Cross-border air strike (Sakiet 1958), Torture, Challe Plan, Morice Line (Border Zone) (Playbook Event list, 5.1.8).

## Map, spaces and tracks

- Space types (1.3.1): Sectors (Mountain or Plain, Pop 0 to 2), Cities (Pop 2 or 3), Countries (Morocco, Tunisia).
- Sectors are named and numbered by Wilaya, for example V-1 (1.3.2). The Wilayas are I to VI. Wilaya names used in setup: I, II, III, IV, V, VI (10.0).
- Cities: Algiers, Oran, Constantine. Algiers and Oran sit inside one Sector. Constantine touches two Sectors (1.3.3).
- Cities can gain +1 Pop. Sectors can drop to Pop 0 by Resettlement (1.3.2, 1.3.3).
- Countries act as Mountain Sectors with Pop 1. They are always Oppose and FLN-controlled once independent (1.3.4, 5.1.8).
- Adjacency: spaces share a land border. Coastal spaces are not adjacent through water (1.3.5, 1.3.6).
- Seven Sectors are Border Sectors: three on Morocco, four on Tunisia. They count only after the Border Zone activates (1.3.7).
- 19 Mountain spaces (marker count in the Playbook Commandos and Napalm entries).
- No LoCs and no Economic Centres (Playbook p.12).
- Support and Opposition: one level each (Support, Neutral, Oppose). No Active/Passive split (1.6, Playbook p.13).
- Pop 0 Sectors are always Neutral (1.6.2).
- Edge Track runs 0 to 50. It records Resources, victory values, Commitment and Resettled Sectors (1.3.8).
- France Track: boxes from A (start) toward F. Each box has a "+Resource" for FLN and a "-Commit" for Government. The marker moves one box toward A in the Reset Phase (1.3.8, 6.2, 6.5, 8.4.5). Exact box values: not verified (on the board image, not supplied).
- Border Zone Track: 0 to 4. Government raises it by Train. It falls toward 0 in the Reset Phase. FLN loses Resources equal to its value each round (5.1.8, 6.2.2).
- Boxes: Available (Government box has French and Algerian sections), Out of Play, Casualties, Capabilities (1.3.8).
- Setup values for the Full scenario: Commitment 25, Resources FLN 8 and Government 16, Support + Commitment 25, Opposition + Bases 9 (10.0). Short scenario: Commitment 15, Resources FLN 15 and Government 20 (10.0).

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

Never run two Propaganda Rounds in a row. Extra Propaganda cards then have no effect. Skip the Victory Phase in the first round (6.0).

1. **Victory Phase (6.1, 7.2).** If a Faction exceeds its threshold, the game ends. If both do, or the deck ends first, the higher margin wins. Ties go to the FLN (7.1).
2. **Resources and Commitment (6.2).**
   - Government gains (6.2.1): Commitment Level; Population of each Government-controlled Sector with a Government Base; number of French pieces in Available (not under Recall de Gaulle); Total Support (only under Recall de Gaulle).
   - Government loses: number of Resettled Sectors (6.2.1).
   - FLN gains (6.2.2): FLN Bases on map; the France Track "+Resource".
   - FLN loses: Border Zone status (6.2.2).
   - Commitment (6.2.3): moving French pieces from Out of Play to Available costs Commitment of one-third of the pieces (round up). Moving them to Out of Play adds one-third (round down). Subtract one-third of French Casualties (round down; not under Recall de Gaulle). Subtract the France Track "-Commit".
   - The printed text for this phase is split across columns. The order of the Government bullets is not fully verified.
3. **Support Phase (6.3).**
   - Pacification: 2 Resources per Terror removed or level shifted, max 2 levels per space, needs Government Control and Troops and Police. Total spend is capped at Commitment (6.3.1).
   - Agitation: 1 Resource per Terror removed or level shifted, max 2 levels per space, in spaces with FLN pieces and no Government Control (6.3.2).
   - Final round: the game ends after this phase (6.3.3).
4. **Redeploy (6.4).** Government first: Troops go to Cities or Base spaces; Police go to Government-controlled spaces. Then FLN moves Guerrillas within a Wilaya to spaces with FLN Bases. Then fix Control.
5. **Reset (6.5).**
   - One in three French pieces and one in three Guerrillas in Casualties (round down) go Out of Play. The rest go to Available.
   - Move the France Track one box toward A. Move the Border Zone one toward 0.
   - Remove one Terror marker from each space that has one.
   - Flip all Guerrillas Underground.
   - Discard Momentum cards.
   - FLN becomes First Eligible.

## Non-player (bot) system

Applies to the FLN only. The human plays Government (8.0). Flowchart on a player aid card. Playbook has 1-player examples (8.0).

- Sequence limits apply. The bot only gets options the sequence allows. It does a Special Activity only if its priorities lead to one (8.1).
- Tie-breakers run in order a, b, c. Remaining ties use the Random Spaces Map: roll two dice (tens, then ones), re-roll a first die of 6, follow arrows to a valid space (8.2).
- General rules (8.1.2): place from Out of Play first; Bases before Guerrillas; keep at least two Guerrillas on Bases and Support spaces; remove enemy Bases first, then French before Algerian, Troops before Police; reduce Commitment first.
- Events (8.3):
  - Uses shaded text of Dual Use cards. Treats other cards as shaded.
  - Always plays Capabilities. Plays Momentum only if marked.
  - Plays a Pivotal Morocco and Tunisia card after Government plays Mobilization, when FLN is Second Eligible and the card is marked for FLN. Never plays Suez Crisis or OAS. Wins Coup d'Etat: it cuts Commitment.
- Priority order (8.4):
  1. Pass if 0 Resources and only a Limited Op is possible (8.4.1).
  2. Terror, but only if all Bases are well guarded by Underground Guerrillas (two or more per Base in Pop 1+ spaces, one or more in Pop 0 spaces) or the Government has put itself Second Eligible for next card. If that gate fails, skip straight to Rally (step 5, 8.4.2). If it passes, skip Terror for the Event when the Event cuts the Government margin as much (8.4.2). Subvert or Extort as Special Activity.
  3. Event if marked for FLN or a Capability. Otherwise play on a 1 to 4 on 1d6 if it cuts the Government margin or Resources, shifts the France Track toward F, adds FLN Bases or Resources (8.4.3).
  4. Attack (with Ambush) if it removes 2 or more French pieces. Uses sure kills first (6+ Guerrillas or Ambush) (8.4.4).
  5. Rally: build Bases from 3+ Guerrillas, reinforce Bases, shift the France Track, seed Support spaces, Agitate (8.4.5). Limited to 6 spaces and two-thirds of Resources if over 8 Resources.
  6. March if Rally fails: reach empty Bases, then Support spaces, then break Government Control, then stage 3 Guerrillas into a Pop 0 space for a Base (8.4.6).
  7. Pass if nothing works (8.4.6).
- Resource discipline: the bot will Extort at 0 Resources. It never Extorts at 5+ (8.4.2).
- Propaganda: Agitate up to two-thirds of Resources, or all in the final round. Redeploy to hold Pop + 1 Guerrillas per Base space, max 3 (8.5).
- One-player victory: the FLN wins if it ever has a positive margin at a Victory Phase. The player wins by surviving to the end with the higher margin. A margin gap of 5 or less is "Negotiated Future". 6 or more is "Vive la France!" (8.6).
- Randomness: dice only for space tiebreaks, Event rolls and Rally conditions (8.2, 8.4.3, 8.4.5).

## Unique mechanics

### Two-player structure and balance

- Only two Factions. The Initiative Track keeps flexible turn order (Playbook p.12).
- Asymmetric victory axes (7.2):
  - Government needs Support + Commitment above 35. This mixes a map measure (Support) with a political measure (Commitment).
  - FLN needs Opposition + Bases above 30. This mixes a population measure with an organization measure.
- Ties go to the FLN (7.1). Historical outcome check: FLN with the higher margin at the end is the "dominant position in negotiations" (2.4.1 note).
- Victory is checked at every Propaganda Round except the first. The FLN can win early. The Government can only win by holding on (6.1, 8.6).
- Resource asymmetry: Government has far larger capacity but a finite pool. An FLN point is worth much more than a Government point (1.8 note, Playbook p.12).
- Passing pays 2 for Government and 1 for FLN (2.3.3).

### Insurgent engine

- Underground and Active Guerrillas. Government must Activate before Assault or Neutralize (1.4.3, 3.2.4).
- Extort turns map control into income, but each use Activates a Guerrilla (4.3.1).
- The France Track gives FLN income and is a second pressure on Commitment (3.3.1, 6.2).
- Morocco and Tunisia sanctuaries: safe bases, but the Border Zone taxes income and makes crossing Active (3.3.2, 5.1.8, 6.2.2).
- Attack has a die roll and Attrition against French pieces. Ambush removes the risk but removes only one piece (3.3.3, 4.3.3).
- Terror cannot build Opposition. It only removes Support. Agitation builds Opposition and costs Resources (Playbook p.13, 3.3.4, 6.3.2).
- Subvert strips Algerian cubes, so Government must keep training (4.3.2, 3.2.1).
- Redeploy is limited to a Wilaya unless a Capability lifts it (6.4.2).

### Counterinsurgent engine

- Police and Troops differ. Police hold ground. Troops sweep and assault. Police count as Troops only in Cities and Border Sectors (3.2.4).
- Mountain halves detection and kill rates (3.2.2 to 3.2.4).
- Pacify is slow: Train space only, 2 Resources per step, and Terror must go first (3.2.1, 6.3.1).
- Resettlement is a trade: fewer people to contest but a permanent drain and no way back (4.2.1, 6.2.1).
- Commitment is the Government's political clock. Killing FLN Bases raises it. French losses lower it (3.2.4, 3.3.3, 6.2.3).
- Coup d'Etat is a repeatable dice duel that can help or hurt each side (5.1.5).

### Other design choices

- Single-level Support and Opposition (Playbook p.13).
- No LoCs or Economic Centres (Playbook p.12).
- Optional hidden Deception markers add fog of war (7.3).
- A Pivotal Event chain gates the Full scenario: Mobilization then Morocco and Tunisia; Coup d'Etat then Recall de Gaulle then OAS (5.1).
- Non-player flowchart obeys the Sequence of Play limits, unlike other COIN bots (8.1).

## Relevance

- Inferred: A 2-player PKK game can copy the Support-versus-Opposition victory split with one organizational term. Tie-to-insurgent and a final-round margin comparison (7.1, 7.3) gives a simple end rule for a short 1984 to late 1980s span.
- Inferred: Underground and Active Guerrillas plus Extort, Subvert and Terror map well to a PKK engine. Sanctuary Countries with a border-tax track (Border Zone, 5.1.8, 6.2.2) fit Syria, Lebanon and northern Iraq bases.
- Inferred: Commitment and the France Track show how to model a state's political will and outside diaspora funding as tracks that move on a Propaganda schedule. They could stand for Turkish public and Western opinion and diaspora income.
- Inferred: The Initiative Track and the single-flowchart non-player (8.0) offer a lean model for a digital Falling Sky build. It reduces faction count while keeping the Limited Op versus Op + Special Activity choice.
