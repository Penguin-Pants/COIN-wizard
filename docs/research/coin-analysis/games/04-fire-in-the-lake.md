# Fire in the Lake (COIN Series Volume IV)

## Identity

- Title: Fire in the Lake. COIN Series, Volume IV. Second Edition (rules cover, p.1).
- Designers: Mark Herman and Volko Ruhnke (rules cover; Playbook p.51 "Game Design").
- Playbook credits: Series Development Mike Bertucelli. Solitaire Systems Orjan Ariander and VPJ Arponen (Playbook p.51).
- Trung booklet: "Card-Based Non-Player Update". Solitaire System Design Bruce Mansfield. Development Jason Carr. Artwork credit Rodger B. MacGowan (Trung p.1).
- Edition: rules (c) 2018 GMT Games. Second Edition changes Air Strike (4.2.3), the Commitment Phase (6.5) and nine cards marked "2nd Ed" (1.0). Trung booklet (c) 2020.
- Setting: South Vietnam and nearby areas, main US period 1964-1972, up to the "Paris Peace" (1.0).
- Scale: national. Each Population point is about 500,000 citizens (1.3.3). Each Event series up to a Coup is a Campaign of 1-2 years (2.4.2 note).
- Players: 1 to 4. Four Factions (US, NVA, ARVN, VC). Non-players fill empty seats (1.0, 1.5, 8.0).
- Components: 22x34 inch board, 130 cards, 229 wooden pieces (1.2).
- Scenarios (2.1): Short 1965-1967 "Westy's War" (3 piles of 8 Events plus Coup). Medium 1968-1972 "A Better War" (3 piles of 12). Full 1964-1972 "Nam" (6 piles of 12).
- Options: Period Events, Deception and Handicap markers (7.3), Tournament options Political Rhythm and High Winds (Setup p.27), No Non-Player option (1.5), 1-player difficulty options (8.9).

## Factions

| Faction | Type | Victory (checked at Coup Rounds, 7.2) | Margin after final Coup (7.3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| US | Counterinsurgent (COIN), foreign | Total Support + US Troops and Bases in Available box exceeds 50 | Total Support + Available US - 50 |
| ARVN | COIN, government | COIN-Controlled Population + Patronage exceeds 50 | COIN Pop + Patronage - 50 |
| NVA | Insurgent, external | NVA-Controlled Population + NVA Bases on map exceeds 18 | NVA Pop + NVA Bases - 18 |
| VC | Insurgent, local | Total Opposition + VC Bases on map exceeds 35 | Opposition + VC Bases - 35 |

- Total Support = 2 x Pop in Active Support + 1 x Pop in Passive Support. Opposition works the same way (1.6.2).
- Highest victory margin wins at game end. Ties go to Non-players, then VC, ARVN, NVA (7.1).
- Resources: each Faction except US holds 0-75 (1.8). The US has no Resources. It spends ARVN Resources only above the marked Total Econ (1.8.1).
- Aid (0-75) is added to ARVN Resources in Coup Rounds. Patronage (0-75) counts for ARVN victory (1.8).
- Pieces (1.4): US Troops, Bases and US-led Irregulars (Special Forces). ARVN Troops, Police, Bases and Rangers (Special Forces). NVA Troops, Guerrillas, Bases. VC Guerrillas and Bases.
- Guerrillas and Special Forces are Underground or Active. Troops, Police and Bases are always Active (1.4.3).
- Stacking: at most 2 Bases per Province or City, none on LoCs. Only NVA and VC may stack in North Vietnam (1.4.2).
- Friends: US and ARVN. NVA and VC (1.5.1). NVA and VC may transfer up to half their Resources to each other (1.5.2).
- Combined play: NVA/VC player uses the higher margin at Coup Rounds, the lower after the Final Coup ("Politburo"). A US/ARVN player uses the lower margin ("Sovereignty") (1.5).
- Control: COIN Control if US plus ARVN pieces exceed NVA plus VC pieces. NVA Control if NVA pieces exceed all other pieces, VC included (1.7).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- One card is revealed ahead, so all players see the next card (2.2, 2.3.7).
- Eligible Factions act in the order of the symbols on the card (2.3.2).
- The 1st Eligible may Pass, execute an Operation (with or without a Special Activity), or execute the Event (2.3.4).
- Pass gives +1 Resource to an Insurgent or +3 ARVN Resources for either COIN Faction. A Passing Faction stays Eligible (2.3.3).
- Options for the 2nd Eligible (2.3.4):
  - 1st did Op only: 2nd may do a Limited Operation.
  - 1st did Op plus Special Activity: 2nd may do a Limited Operation or the Event.
  - 1st did the Event: 2nd may do an Operation, with a Special Activity if desired.
- Limited Operation: one space, no Special Activity. Patrol, Sweep and March may draw pieces from many spaces to one destination (2.3.5).
- Factions that acted become Ineligible for the next card. Factions that did not stay Eligible. Events can change this (2.3.6).
- Pivotal Events (Medium and Full scenarios only): each Faction holds one. It cancels the played Event if the Faction is Eligible, its red precondition holds, the 1st Eligible has not acted and no Coup shows next (2.3.8).
- Trumping order: VC over all, ARVN over US or NVA, NVA over US, US over none (2.3.8).
- Monsoon: on the last Event card before each Coup, no Sweep and no March. Air Lift and Air Strike hit at most 2 spaces. No Pivotal Events (2.3.9).
- Free Operations cost no Resources and do not change Eligibility. Pacification, Agitation and Trail Improvement still cost (3.1.2, 5.5).
- Change from series core (inferred from Playbook p.12): Monsoon applies before every Coup, not only at game end.

## Operations and Special Activities

| Faction | Operations (cost) | Special Activities |
|---|---|---|
| US | Train (3 ARVN Res only if ARVN pieces placed), Patrol (0), Sweep (0), Assault (0; 3 ARVN Res to add ARVN follow-up) (3.2) | Advise, Air Lift, Air Strike (4.2) |
| ARVN | Train (3), Patrol (3 total), Sweep (3 per space), Assault (3 per space) (3.2) | Govern, Transport, Raid (4.3) |
| NVA | Rally, March, Attack, Terror (1 per space; March 1 per Province or City entered; LoCs 0) (3.3) | Infiltrate, Bombard, Ambush (4.4) |
| VC | Same four Operations (3.3) | Tax, Subvert, Ambush (4.5) |

COIN Operations (3.2):
- Train: US places 1-2 Irregulars, or at US Bases 1-2 Rangers or up to 6 ARVN cubes. ARVN places 1-2 Rangers or up to 6 cubes at Cities or Bases. In one space, also Pacify (up to 2 levels toward Active Support, 3 ARVN Res per step) or replace 3 cubes with an ARVN Base, or US in Saigon moves up to 3 Patronage to ARVN Resources (3.2.1).
- Patrol: cubes move along LoCs and Cities and stop at enemy pieces. Activate 1 Guerrilla per cube on each LoC. Optional free Assault in 1 LoC (3.2.2).
- Sweep: Troops move in (optionally via an enemy-free LoC). Activate 1 Guerrilla per cube or Special Force in each space. Jungle gives 1 per 2 (3.2.3).
- Assault: ARVN removes 1 enemy per 2 cubes (per 3 in Highland). Police count in Cities and LoCs only. US removes 2 per Troop at a US Base, 1 per Troop elsewhere, 1 per 2 in Highland without a Base. Order: NVA Troops, Active Guerrillas, then Bases. No Underground removal. A Tunneled Base needs a die roll of 4-6 to lose its marker. Each Base removed by ARVN gives +6 Aid (3.2.4).

Insurgent Operations (3.3):
- Rally: place 1 Guerrilla or replace 2 with a Base. With a Base present, NVA places Guerrillas up to Trail value plus NVA Bases. VC places up to Pop plus VC Bases or flips all Underground. NVA may pay 2 to Improve the Trail (3.3.1).
- March: move Guerrillas and Troops one space. Guerrillas go Active at a LoC or Support space if the group plus COIN pieces there exceeds 3. NVA can chain moves through Laos and Cambodia if the Trail is above 0. At Trail 4 those moves cost 0 (3.3.2).
- Attack: Activate all Guerrillas, roll at or under their number to remove 2 enemies. NVA Troops remove 1 per 2 Troops. Attrition removes 1 attacker per US Troop or Base killed. US losses go to Casualties (3.3.3).
- Terror: Activate 1 Guerrilla. Place a Terror marker. VC shifts 1 level toward Opposition. NVA shifts Support toward Neutral. LoCs get Sabotage (3.3.4).

Special Activities:
- US Advise (with Train or Patrol, 1-2 spaces, not Train spaces): ARVN Sweep, ARVN Assault, or Activate an Irregular or Ranger to remove 2. Optional +6 Aid (4.2.1).
- US Air Lift: move US Troops plus up to 4 ARVN pieces among 4 spaces (4.2.2).
- US Air Strike: up to 6 spaces with US or ARVN pieces. Roll 1 die for hits. 1 hit removes an Active piece. 2 hits Degrade the Trail 1 box, and never more than 1 box per Air Strike. Shifts spaces toward Opposition (4.2.3).
- ARVN Govern (with Train or Patrol, 1-2 COIN-Controlled spaces with any level of Support, not Saigon and not a space selected for Training): add 3 x Pop to Aid, or move Pop from Aid to Patronage and shift toward Neutral. The Patronage option needs more ARVN cubes (Troops and Police) than US Troops in the space (4.3.1).
- ARVN Transport: move up to 6 Troops or Rangers from one space over LoCs. Then flip all Rangers Underground (4.3.2).
- ARVN Raid (with Patrol, Sweep or Assault, 1-2 spaces): move in Rangers, Activate one to remove 2 enemies (4.3.3).
- NVA Infiltrate (with Rally or March, 1-2 spaces with an NVA Base or more NVA than VC pieces): with an NVA Base, place Troops up to Trail plus Bases, then swap Guerrillas for Troops. Or, where NVA outnumber VC, shift Opposition toward Neutral and replace any 1 VC piece (4.4.1).
- NVA Bombard (1-2 spaces): remove 1 US or ARVN Troop where 3+ COIN Troops (or a COIN Base) sit in or beside 3+ NVA Troops (4.4.2).
- NVA and VC Ambush (1-2 spaces, with March or Attack): an automatic Attack that removes 1 piece. From a LoC it may hit an adjacent space (4.4.3, 4.5.3).
- VC Tax (up to 4 spaces with Underground VC, no COIN Control): Activate 1 Underground VC Guerrilla per space, gain Econ or 2 x Pop, shift toward Support (4.5.1).
- VC Subvert (with Rally, March or Terror, 1-2 spaces): remove 2 ARVN cubes or replace 1 with a Guerrilla. Patronage drops 1 per 2 pieces (4.5.2).

## Event cards and deck

- 130 cards (1.2): 120 Event cards, 6 Coup cards, 4 Pivotal Events (Setup p.26).
- Event cards carry a year (1964, 1965, 1968): 24, 48 and 48 cards. The Period Events option builds each scenario from matching years (Setup p.26).
- Each Event has flavor text (no effect), Event text and an optional shaded alternative (5.0, 5.2).
- Dual Use: the executing Faction picks the unshaded or shaded text, whichever Faction it is (5.2).
- Capabilities: lasting effects marked by Faction. A marker sits in the Capabilities box for the rest of the game (5.3).
- Momentum: effects last to the next Coup Reset Phase (5.4).
- Event text overrides rules. Events cannot break stacking, force removal of Tunneled Bases, or push Resources past 75 (5.1.1).
- Coup cards double as RVN Leader cards. The top played Coup card sets lingering Leader effects. Duong Van Minh is a Leader but not a card. Failed Attempt cancels only Minh (2.4.1).
- Playbook gives historical background for each card and Leader (Playbook p.46-49).

## Map, spaces and tracks

- Space types (1.3.1): Provinces, Cities, and Lines of Communication (LoCs). LoCs are spaces and hold forces. Towns only end LoCs.
- Provinces have Pop 0-2 and terrain Highland, Lowland or Jungle (1.3.2). Cities have Pop 1, 2 or 6 (1.3.3). LoCs are Highway or Mekong with Econ 0-2 (1.3.4).
- Foreign spaces: parts of North Vietnam, Laos (2 spaces) and Cambodia (4 spaces). US and ARVN may enter Laos and Cambodia but not North Vietnam (1.3.5, Key Terms).
- Adjacency: shared borders, Provinces split by a LoC, and LoCs or Provinces split by a Town (1.3.6). Coastal spaces matter for some Events (1.3.7).
- Support and Opposition has 5 levels for Pop 1+ spaces. LoCs and Pop 0 Provinces stay Neutral (1.6).
- Edge track: Resources, Aid, Patronage, 0-75 (1.8). Victory markers: Support + Available, NVA Pop + Bases, COIN Pop + Patronage, Opposition + VC Bases (1.9).
- Trail track 0-4 (6.7). Up to 15 Terror or Sabotage markers (3.3.4). Total Econ marker, maximum 15 (6.2.3).
- Boxes: Available, Out of Play, Casualties, RVN Leader, Capabilities, US Policy (1.4.1, 2.4, T1).
- Map image not provided. Adjacency lists are not verified.

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

A Coup Round runs after a Coup card is played (6.0). Never two Rounds in a row (6.0).
1. Victory Phase: any Faction meeting its threshold ends the game (6.1).
2. Resources Phase (6.2): Sabotage LoCs where Guerrillas outnumber COIN or next to a City without COIN Control. Degrade the Trail if COIN Controls a Laos or Cambodia space. ARVN gains Aid plus unSabotaged Econ. VC gains its Bases. NVA gains Bases in Laos and Cambodia plus 2 x Trail. Subtract 3 Aid per Casualty.
3. Support Phase (6.3): US then ARVN Pacify in up to 4 spaces combined (COIN Control, Police and own Troops, 3 ARVN Res per step, 2 levels max per space). VC Agitates in up to 4 spaces (1 Resource per step).
4. Redeploy Phase (6.4): remove US and ARVN from Laos and Cambodia (US Troops go out of play). ARVN Troops must leave LoCs and Provinces without COIN Bases. NVA Troops may go to any NVA Base. Final Round ends here.
5. Commitment Phase (6.5): take 1 in 3 US Troop Casualties and all Base Casualties out of play. Return the rest to the map. Move up to 10 minus those placed Troops plus up to 2 Bases. Withdrawal lets VC shift Pop toward Opposition.
6. Reset Phase (6.6): Trail 0 goes to 1, Trail 4 goes to 3. Remove Terror and Sabotage. Flip Guerrillas and Special Forces Underground. Discard Momentum. All Factions Eligible. Play next card.

After the Final Coup, the highest margin wins (2.4.2, 7.3).

## Non-player (bot) system

Two systems exist. Base rules 8.0 use flowcharts. The Trung booklet replaces them with cards and tables. Flowchart sheets and Trung cards, tables and the Random Spaces map are not in the text files. Their content is not verified.

Base system (8.0):
- Any Faction may be a Non-player. Each acts in its own interest and sometimes helps or harms others (8.0).
- Non-players take a full Operation plus Special Activity in place of any Limited Operation (8.1).
- They play only Events that have an effect (8.1). A gray rifle on a card symbol means decline the Event. A halo means check special Event instructions (8.4.1).
- Dual Use: NVA and VC use shaded text, US and ARVN use unshaded (8.4.2).
- Piece order: place or target Bases, then Guerrillas or Special Forces, then Troops and Police evenly. Target Underground before Active (8.1.2).
- Ties among spaces use a two-dice Random Spaces map. LoCs come last. Highest Econ first (8.2, 8.3).
- Flowcharts use sequential and nested priorities. Nested ones are tie breakers (8.1.4).
- Pivotal Events: a Non-player plays its own at once when playable and a player is 1st Eligible (8.4.6).
- Each Faction has a rule section (8.5 VC, 8.6 NVA, 8.7 ARVN, 8.8 US). Examples:
  - VC test Terror first, then Event or Pass, Rally, March, Attack (8.5.1-.5).
  - NVA test Attack first, then Event or Pass, Terror, Rally, March (8.6.1-.5).
  - ARVN test Event or Pass, Train, Patrol, Assault, Sweep (8.7.1-.5).
  - US test Capability Events, Assault, Patrol (in Monsoon), Sweep, Event, Train (8.8.2-.7).
- Randomness: dice decide whether to play Capability Events by comparing a roll to the count of Coup cards in the RVN Leader box (8.5.2, 8.6.2, 8.7.1, 8.8.2).
- US Policy (JFK, LBJ, Nixon) shifts after each Commitment by a die roll against the Leader count if Support + Available is 45 or less (8.8.1). It sets Irregular placement, Air Strike use and Commitment targets of 15, 5 or 25 Available Troops (8.8.7, 8.8.9).
- Victory: any Non-player passing a check makes all players lose (7.1). Solo rules compare the player's lowest margin to the best Non-player margin (8.9).

Trung system (T-rules, replaces 8.5-8.8 flowcharts):
- Each Non-player Faction owns 6 cards. All sit in one shared face-up deck (T1.3.1, T4.1.3).
- Draw: move top cards to the bottom until the top card matches the active Faction (T5.3.3).
- Read the card: a blue condition box leads along a green (true) or red (false) arrow (T5.3.4). Arrows may lead to "draw a new card" or "flip the card" (T5.3.5). Faces are marked by one letter or two (T5.3.1).
- The card names the Operation, then one Special Activity chosen in letter order. Starred steps run last (T5.3.6, T5.3.7).
- No legal space for an Operation: draw a new card (T5.3.6). If all six cards fail, go to the next row of the NP Eligibility table (T5.3.8).
- Activation Number: a die icon on the card. After each selected space roll 1d6. Result at or below the number stops further spaces. Higher continues (T5.4.1).
- Exceptions to that roll: NP US (except Train placing ARVN cubes), NP ARVN Patrol, Limited Operations, March onto LoCs and VC Terror on LoCs (T5.4, T5.4.2). NVA March uses number 1 at Trail 4 (T5.4.2).
- Resources: NP NVA and VC track none. NP VC keeps an Agitate Total, starting at 1d3. NP US and ARVN track ARVN Resources only if the other COIN Faction is a player (T2.1.1, T4.1.4).
- Events use four tables: Event Card (Critical, Performed or not), Effective Events, Event Instructions, Capability Effects. A 1st Eligible NP plays only Critical Events. A 2nd Eligible plays Critical or Performed. Both need an Effective result (T5.5.1-.2).
- Pivotal Events: play if the precondition holds, the NP is Eligible, the Event is not Critical for it, and 1d6 is below the Coup-card count in the RVN Leader box (T5.5.8).
- Space Selection tables: one per Faction, columns by action type. Read down, keep only spaces meeting each dotted row until one remains (T5.6.1). Ties use dice and the Random Spaces chart (T5.6.2).
- Move Priorities: pick destination, then the origin with the most movable pieces. Then apply Keep and Move rows (T5.7). Piece Priorities table handles placement, removal and Activation order (T5.8).
- Golden Rules (T2.1): follow the rules, skip illegal instructions, place pieces one at a time using the Space table, do everything to the maximum, and randomize when instructions run out (T2.1.2-.6).
- Win rule: solitaire player may win only at the final Coup. NP Factions may win at any Victory Phase (T3.1).
- Capability add-on instructions per Faction are in the booklet table, such as Abrams, Arc Light and Cadres (Trung p.8).

## Unique mechanics

- Four asymmetric Factions where only one can win. Allies are rivals: NVA versus VC, US versus ARVN (Playbook p.47).
- Insurgents field conventional Troops (NVA) beside Guerrillas (1.4).
- COIN Special Forces: US Irregulars and ARVN Rangers act like Guerrillas (1.4.3).
- Pivotal Events trump Eligibility order, with a trumping chain (2.3.8).
- RVN Leaders on Coup cards replace generic Propaganda cards (2.4.1).
- US victory counts forces held in the Available box. The Commitment Phase moves them in and out of play (6.5, 7.2).
- Casualties box, Out of Play box and Attrition against Attackers (3.3.3, 6.5).
- Trail track drives NVA growth, income and movement (6.7).
- Joint Operations: US spends only ARVN Resources above Total Econ (1.8.1).
- Aid and Patronage as a convertible pool tied to ARVN victory (4.3.1).
- Monsoon before every Coup (2.3.9).
- Tunneled Bases and Sabotage markers on LoCs (1.4.4, 3.3.4).
- NVA Infiltrate turns VC pieces into NVA pieces (4.4.1).
- Deception and Handicap victory markers (7.3, 7.1).

## Relevance

- Inferred: the Pivotal Event and Leader-card structure could model sudden political turning points (for example coups or amnesty offers) in a PKK game, and give a digital Falling Sky a way to break initiative order.
- Inferred: the Underground/Active Guerrilla flip with Sweep-style "location" costs maps well to a PKK cell structure with a cheap-to-hide, costly-to-find rule.
- Inferred: the Trail track and Laos/Cambodia sanctuaries suggest a model for PKK cross-border bases, with a "sanctuary cleared by incursion" check in the Coup Round.
- Inferred: the Trung card-deck bot plus Activation Number roll is a compact, code-friendly bot design for a digital Falling Sky, as each card is a small decision tree and the table logic is deterministic except for named dice.
