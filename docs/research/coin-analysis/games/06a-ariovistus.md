## Identity

- Title: *Ariovistus*, Expansion Rules (A1.0). Needs a copy of *Falling Sky* to play.
- COIN Series Volume VI expansion (cover: "COIN Series, Volume VI, Expansion Rules"). It is a prequel to *Falling Sky* (A1.0).
- Credits printed: rules and game design by Andrew Ruhnke and Volko Ruhnke. Playbook credits add: Vassal module Joel Toppen and Art Bennett; art director Rodger MacGowan; card, counter and display art Mark Simonitch and Chechu Nieto Sanchez; manuals and charts Charlie Kibler; producers Tony Curtis, Rodger MacGowan, Andy Lewis, Gene Billingsley and Mark Simonitch; special thanks to VPJ Arponen for the Non-players (Playbook p.11).
- Edition: copyright 2018 GMT Games, printing code 1811. Rules file has 20 pages, no ISBN or version line (Rules p.1).
- Setting: 58 BC. Caesar enters Gaul against the migrating Helvetii, the Suebi chief Ariovistus and later the Veneti (A1.0).
- Scale: the map is the *Falling Sky* map. Cisalpina becomes playable. Britannia becomes unplayable in the main scenario (A1.3.2, A1.3.4).
- Players: 1 to 4. Factions: Germans, Romans, Aedui, Belgae. The system runs the Arverni (A1.5).
- Scenarios: (1) *Ariovistus* (58 BC); (2) *The Gallic War* (58 to 50 BC), which plays *Ariovistus*, then an Interlude, then *Pax Gallica?* from *Falling Sky* (A2.1).
- How to read this file: unchanged parts say "same as Falling Sky" (FS). Deltas are listed by section. The expansion booklet uses the FS numbering with an "A" prefix (A1.0).
- Rules source of FS facts below: falling-sky--rules.md. Cited as FS section numbers.

## Factions

Overview of change: the Germans become a full player Faction. The Arverni become a game-run "Celtibot" (A1.5, Playbook p.3).

| Faction | Type | Status in Ariovistus |
|---|---|---|
| Germanic Tribes (black) | Insurgent / invader | Player Faction. Tracks Resources. Has victory (A1.5, A1.8, A7.2) |
| Romans (red) | Government | Player Faction. Same as FS, with changes (A3.2, A4.2, A7.2) |
| Aedui (blue) | Other / Gallic ally of Rome | Player Faction. New Leader Diviciacus (A1.4) |
| Belgae (yellow) | Insurgent | Player Faction. Leader Ambiorix piece is Boduognatus (A1.4) |
| Arverni (green) | Game-run "Arverni and Other Celts" | No victory. No Resources. No player (A1.5, A7.0) |

Players and spare Factions (A1.5.1, A2.1 Setup):
- 4 players: one Faction each.
- 3 players: Romans and Aedui and Germans-plus-Belgae, or Romans-plus-Aedui and Germans and Belgae.
- 2 players: Romans and Aedui versus Germans and Belgae.
- Non-players may fill any seat (A8.0).

### Germanic Tribes (new player Faction)
- Victory (A7.2): Germania Regions under Germanic Control plus Germanic Settlements under Germanic Control must exceed 6. Final Winter margin: Germania Regions + Settlements under Germanic Control - 6 (A7.3).
- Resources: tracked with a black cylinder (A1.8). Earn 2 per Ally plus Settlement on the map in Harvest (A6.4.4). Pass gives +1 (A2.3.3). Raid gains Resources (A3.4.3).
- Forces: FS Germanic pieces plus 15 added Warbands (30 total), 6 Settlements, and Ariovistus Leader with a Successor (embossing down) (A1.4, Setup "Available Forces").
- Home Regions: Germania Regions and any Region with a Settlement (A1.3.1).
- Settlement: max 1 per Region. No Tribe space needed. Counts as a Germanic Ally for Rally, Battle Losses and Besiege. Immune to Roman Build and Aedui Suborn. Placed by Settle or Events, never by Rally (A1.4, A1.4.2).
- Ariovistus Leader: limits where Germanic Special Abilities work, as other Leaders do (A4.1.2). Doubles Losses on enemy in Battle unless the enemy Defends with Fort or Citadel (A3.2.4, A3.4.4). Raiding with him allows free Intimidate (A3.4.3). Enlist may not select his Region (A4.5.1).
- Negotiation: Germans may agree to Supply Lines and Retreat in their Controlled Regions, may choose to Harass, and may give or receive Resources like other Factions (A1.5.2). In FS the Germans never agree and always Harass (FS 3.4.5).
- Other notes: use black Eligibility cylinder (A2.2).

### Romans
- Victory: Subdued + Dispersed + Roman Allied Tribes - Germanic Settlements on map must exceed 15 (A7.2). Margin: that sum - 15 (A7.3). FS: no Settlement term (FS 7.2).
- Play aid: Roman score = 30 (31 with Colony) minus non-Roman Allies, Citadels and Settlements on map (A7.2 note).
- Earnings: Harvest gives Resources equal to Victory score, so Settlements reduce income (A6.4.1).
- Other deltas: see Operations. Reference playing card lists key changes (A3.1).

### Aedui
- Victory: Aedui Allied Tribes + Citadels must exceed those of each other Faction, with Germanic Settlements counted as Germanic Allies (A7.2). Margin uses the Faction with the most, Settlements counted as Allies (A7.3).
- Leader: Diviciacus (A1.4). No Successor (A1.4, A6.6). Removed Diviciacus is out of play, never Available (A1.4.1).
- Diviciacus on map: Aedui Special Abilities only in his Region or adjacent. Suborn and Aedui Ambush stay max 1 Region (A1.4, A4.1.2, A4.4).
- Diviciacus within 1 Region: if Romans and Aedui agree, Aedui Warbands and Auxilia count as each other for both Factions' Commands, Special Abilities and Battle defense. This includes March distance and Ambush enabling or blocking (A1.4, A3.1).
- Diviciacus in Battle: absorbs Losses only when he is the last piece able to. A Loss removes him on a roll of 1 only (FS Leaders: 1 to 3) (A1.4, A3.2.4).
- If Diviciacus leaves the map, Aedui revert to FS 4.1.2 and 4.4 rules (A1.4.1).

### Belgae
- Victory: Belgic Control Value + Belgic Allies and Citadels must exceed 15, same as FS (A7.2, FS 7.2).
- Cisalpina is worth 1 Control Value for Belgic victory (A1.3.2).
- Ambiorix piece is Boduognatus, same properties (A1.4).
- Enlist is limited (A4.5.1). See Operations.

### Arverni and Other Celts (game-run)
- No victory, no Resources, no player. Green cylinders and the victory marker are not used (A1.8, A1.9, A7.0).
- Do not use the Vercingetorix piece (A1.4). The Faction has no Leader.
- Four Arverni Home Regions: Veneti, Carnutes, Pictones, Arverni. Mark each with an Arverni "Rally" marker (A1.3.1).
- Use FS Commands but always pair Battle with Ambush (A3.1, A4.3).
- Never agree to Supply Lines or Retreat. Always Harass (A1.5.2).
- Tie-break position irrelevant: they cannot win (A7.0 note).

### Arverni trigger procedure (A2.3.9, A6.2)
1. A card with the carnyx symbol appears.
2. After the 1st and 2nd Eligible actions, check At War status.
3. If At War, run the Arverni Phase (see Winter section below). Place the At War marker on the card as a reminder.
4. If At Peace, skip and go to the next card.

Notes:
- Playbook says the Arverni activate unpredictably through the campaign and not only in Winter (Playbook p.3).
- Sequence of Play aid sheets for Germans and Arverni highlight changes (A1.0).
- Frost still blocks March by Commands, including German Commands, and blocks Arverni March in the Arverni Phase (A6.2.2). In FS, Frost did not affect the Germans Phase (FS 2.3.8).

## Operations and Special Activities

Commands and Special Abilities are the FS ones unless listed. In FS the Special Abilities are called "Special Abilities" and in the expansion text also "Special Activities" in some places; use FS wording.

### Germanic Commands (A3.4, replaces FS 3.4 entirely)
Germans use Rally, March, Raid and Battle by the Gallic rules (3.3, A3.3) except:

| Command | Cost and effect |
|---|---|
| Rally (A3.4.1) | May select any Region, even Intimidated. Cost: 2 Resources outside Germania with no Settlement; 1 at a Settlement; 0 in Germania. Place one Ally only in a Germanic-Controlled Region (never Bibracte or Gergovia), or place Warbands up to Allies plus Settlements there. At end of Rally, in each Region with a Settlement or in Germania, place 1 extra Warband. No Rally in a Devastated Region |
| March (A3.4.2) | Like Gallic March. Leader and Warbands. Not into a 2nd Region. Replaces FS rule of one group per origin |
| Raid (A3.4.3) | Like Gallic Raid: 1 or 2 Hidden Warbands per Region. Germans now receive Resources. Raiders in Ariovistus's Region may Intimidate free (no Special Ability counted, allowed on Limited Command) |
| Battle (A3.4.4) | Like Gallic Battle. Ambush is optional. Defenders may Retreat as from Gallic Battle |
| Losses (A3.4.5) | Germans choose pieces within normal restrictions. Settlement or Ally absorbs Loss; Germans choose which when both are present |

FS comparison: costs 0, one Ally then Warbands up to Allies, no Resources gained, always Ambush, no Retreat (FS 3.4).

### Germanic Special Abilities (A4.6)
- Settle (A4.6.1): accompanies Rally or March only. Select Regions that are outside Germania AND adjacent to Germania or a Settlement AND under Germanic Control AND within 1 Region of Ariovistus, or in the same Region as his Successor (A4.1.2, A4.6.1). Pay 2 Resources per Region (4 if Devastated) to place a Settlement. A new Settlement can qualify the next adjacent Region in the same Settle.
- Intimidate (A4.6.2): accompanies March, Raid or Battle. Regions: with Ariovistus, or under Germanic Control and within 1 Region of him or with his Successor. In each Region, flip 1 or 2 Hidden Germanic Warbands to Revealed to place an Intimidated marker (max 1 per Region) and remove that many Warbands, Auxilia and/or Allies of one Faction that has no Leader there. Intimidated markers block Recruit and Rally by non-German Factions with no Leader there. Removed in Spring (A6.6).
- Ambush (A4.6.3): works like FS Arverni Ambush (4.3.3) with Germanic pieces and Ariovistus. Hidden Germanic Warbands must exceed Hidden Defenders.

### Roman changes
- Recruit (A3.2.1): cannot select Intimidated Regions unless a Roman Leader is there.
- Supply Line (A3.2.1): the chain must include Provincia and/or Cisalpina (FS: reach a border with Cisalpina). Applies to Build, Trade, and Quarters.
- March (A3.2.2): a group must stop on entering or leaving Cisalpina. Germans may choose to Harass; Arverni always do.
- Seize (A3.2.3): Germans and Arverni Harass. Arverni free Rally from Dispersed markers per A6.2.1.
- Battle (A3.2.4): Germans may Retreat like Gauls, including staying put with Leader and Hidden Warbands. Arverni never Retreat. Settlements absorb Losses as Allies. Ariovistus doubles enemy Losses unless the Defender has Fort or Citadel. Diviciacus rules as above. Arverni remove Scouted Warbands, then other Revealed, then Hidden, then Allies (Cities last), then Citadels.
- Besiege (A4.2.3): may remove a Settlement instead of an Ally (Attacker choice).
- Build (A4.2.1): Supply Line change only. Settlements are immune to Build (A1.4).

### Gallic changes
- Rally (A3.3.1): cannot select Intimidated Regions unless the Faction's Leader is there.
- Raid (A3.3.3): may take Resources from Germans.
- Battle (A3.3.4): same changes as Roman Battle. Germans and Gauls Retreating from each other may not stay put; they must reach an adjacent friendly-Controlled Region or be removed.

### Arverni (A4.3, A3.1)
- Only Ambush. No Entreat or Devastate. Needs more Hidden Arverni than Hidden Defenders. No Leader needed (A4.3.3).
- Arverni Rally: Warbands up to Allies plus Citadels plus 1, or 1 Warband in a Home Region with no Ally (A3.3.1 note).

### Aedui (A4.4)
- With Diviciacus on map, Suborn, Trade and Ambush only within 1 Region of him. Trade Supply Lines must include Provincia or Cisalpina.

### Belgae: Enlist (A4.5.1)
- Max 4 Germanic Warbands and Allies per Enlist. Affects neither the Germanic Leader nor Settlements.
- May not select a Region with Ariovistus.
- Free Raid by Enlist gives Resources to the Germans.
- Free Battle with up to 4 Germanic Warbands in one Region gets no Ambush and ignores other Germanic pieces there.
- Rampage may not target Germans. Rampaged Arverni are removed since they never Retreat.

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

Winter round order is FS (6.1 Victory, 6.2 Germans, 6.3 Quarters, 6.4 Harvest, 6.5 Senate, 6.6 Spring). Ariovistus changes:

1. Victory Phase (A6.1): Germans track victory. Arverni do not. Game ends if any Faction meets its condition (FS 6.1).
2. Arverni Phase (A6.2) replaces Germans Phase. Winter has no Germans Phase. The Arverni activate when cued by a carnyx card, not in Winter (A6.2 note).
3. Quarters (A6.3): order is Belgae, Aedui, Germans, Romans.
   - Arverni Warbands do not relocate (A6.3.1).
   - Belgae, Aedui and Germans may relocate Warbands and Leaders to adjacent un-Devastated Regions under their Control or a Control of an agreeing Faction (not Arverni Control). Then each Warband in a Devastated Region with no Ally, Citadel or Settlement is removed on a die roll 1 to 3 (A6.3.2). In FS, Germans return to Germania automatically (FS 6.3.1).
   - Romans same as FS. Supply Lines must include Provincia or Cisalpina (A6.3.3).
4. Harvest (A6.4):
   - Romans: Victory score (it already subtracts Settlements, so subtract them once only) (A6.4.1).
   - Arverni earn nothing (A6.4.2).
   - Germans: 2 x (Allies + Settlements) (A6.4.4). In FS, Gaul Factions earn 2 x (Allies + Citadels) and Aedui +4 River Tolls (FS 6.4.2, 6.4.3). Assumed unchanged for Belgae and Aedui (not changed in A6.4; inferred).
5. Senate (A6.5): Legions placed into Provincia at most 2 from Intrigue and/or Adulation rows per Senate Phase (A6.5.2). In The Gallic War, the first Senate Phase after the Interlude does not shift the Senate marker (A6.5.1).
6. Spring (A6.6): usual steps plus remove all Intimidated markers. Aedui never receive a Successor to Diviciacus.

### Arverni Phase detail (A6.2)
At War test: the Arverni are At War if any non-Arverni Forces are in an Arverni Home Region or with an Arverni Ally or Citadel in any other Region. Otherwise At Peace.

Select targets: roll on the "Roll to Select Targets" table. Choose a Target Region among the Regions that make the Arverni At War: either the trigger Region with most enemy pieces or a named Home Region with enemies. If the roll does not give a valid Region, go down the column, wrapping to the top. Then roll again for a Target Faction in that Region (named Faction or the Faction with most pieces). Place the Target marker. Ties are random.

Then act in this order:
1. Rally (A6.2.1) in At War Regions only: first replace Allies in Cities with Citadels; then place Allies (Cities, then Home Regions, then elsewhere); then place Warbands. Random among equals. Not in Intimidated or Devastated Regions.
2. March (A6.2.2), none on Frost: flip Warbands Hidden. Groups leave Regions (not the Target Region) with Warbands beyond what Control needs. Go to the Target Region first, then to at most one more destination with Aedui or Roman Control (Aedui first) where moving in would remove that Control. Adjacent moves only.
3. Raid (A6.2.3): only with Hidden Warbands not needed for Ambush; only vs Factions with more than 0 Resources and no Fort or Citadel; target Faction first, then players, then Non-players.
4. Battle with Ambush (A6.2.4): where Hidden Arverni exceed enemy Hidden pieces and a Loss is possible. Target Region first, then others in random order. Target Faction first, then players before Non-players.

## Non-player (bot) system

Section 8 is replaced by the FS Second Edition Non-player booklet (A8.0, A1.2.1). Ariovistus adds a full Germanic Non-player, adjustments for Roman, Aedui and Belgic Non-players, and the Arverni "Celtibot". Rules 8.1 to 8.4 apply to the Germans with amendments.

Recommended Non-player sets (A8.0): 3 players, Belgae or Aedui Non-player; 2 players, play Romans and Germans and use Belgae and Aedui bots; 1 player, play Romans or Germans and use the other three bots.

### General amendments (A8.2 to A8.4)
- Event Instructions for Laurels and Carnyx are on the Ariovistus Non-player foldout (A8.2.1).
- Dual Use: Non-player Germans use shaded text (A8.2.2).
- Settlements have equal priority with Citadels for placement or removal (A8.2.3, A8.3.1).
- Non-player Germans decline Events that tell them to select Gallic Factions (Celtic Rites, Druids, Remi Influence) (A8.2.3 note).
- In 8.4, read "Arverni" as "Germans", "Gauls" as "Gauls or Germans", "Germans" as "Arverni" (A8.4).

### Non-player Germans flowchart (A8.7)
Order of checks when Eligible:
1. Battle or March under threat (A8.7.1). Condition: Ariovistus, or 6 or more Germanic Warbands, in a Region where an enemy has an Ally, Citadel, Legion or at least 4 pieces. Battle only where Germans inflict more Losses than they suffer and Ariovistus takes none, assuming all Defender rolls remove. If Ariovistus meets the condition but cannot battle safely, Germans do not Battle at all and March instead.
   - Battle order: Ariovistus fights an enemy with fewer mobile pieces first; then other enemies. Random among equals.
   - Ambush when the enemy's Retreat could lower removals or a Counterattack could hurt Germans. If Ambush happens in the first Battle, Ambush in all. If no Ambush is possible, Intimidate before Battle.
   - Intimidate priority: player Allies (Romans, then Aedui, then Belgae); player Roman Auxilia, Aedui Warbands, Belgic Warbands; Non-player Roman then Aedui Allies; other Non-player Roman or Aedui pieces. Place markers only when pieces are removed, or in Ariovistus's Raid Region.
   - If no Battle, or if an enemy is at victory margin 0 or better and Ariovistus has 12 or more Warbands: March all mobile forces out of qualifying Regions and the Leader's Region. Destinations favor Regions with most Dispersed Tribes if Romans are at victory; then Allies of Aedui/Belgae at victory; then Regions without Germanic Control.
   - Settle after March if Intimidate was not used. If nothing, Command only.
2. Pass or Event (A8.7.2). Pass on a die roll of 1 to 4 if Germans are 1st on the next card but not on the current card. Otherwise play the Event unless it is Ineffective, adds a Capability in the final year, or is "No Germans" (crossed swords); check Laurels and Carnyx instructions.
3. Raid (A8.7.3). If fewer than 4 Resources, roll 1 to 4 to try. Raid only if it gains 2 Resources. Targets: Romans or Aedui first, then Belgae, then no Faction. Free Intimidate in Ariovistus's Region. If it cannot gain enough, Pass.
4. Rally and Settle (A8.7.4). If it would place an Ally, a Settlement, 4 or more Warbands, or add Control. Settle first, then Allies, then Warbands starting at Settlement Regions and Germania. If not possible, Raid.
5. March to expand or mass (A8.7.5). Up to 3 Regions, leaving 1 Warband behind per origin. Add Control to up to two Regions (near Germania first). Then bring the largest group toward the Leader or the Leader toward a non-German-Controlled Region. Intimidate or Settle after. If not possible (Frost), Rally, then Raid.
6. Quarters (A8.7.6). Leave Drought Devastated Regions without Ally or Settlement. Join the Leader with the largest Warband group; keep Control.

## Unique mechanics

- Settlements: a piece that moves victory by converting land into Germanic points while lowering Roman and Aedui scores (A7.2).
- Second-order victory: Germans win by Controlling Germania plus Settlements. They need to defend home while spreading (A7.2, Playbook p.2).
- Game-run faction that wakes on a card icon and a trigger test, not on Eligibility (A2.3.9, A6.2).
- Target system with a table and two die rolls (A6.2).
- Intimidate: a marker that blocks Recruit and Rally for non-Germans without a Leader (A4.6.2).
- Leader that doubles Losses (Ariovistus) and a Leader that resists Losses (Diviciacus) (A3.2.4).
- Diviciacus lets two Factions treat their units as interchangeable if both agree (A1.4).
- Interlude in The Gallic War: reset board with fractional removals, Britannia expedition, then switch the Germanic player to the Arverni role (A2.1).
- Playbook design notes: a five-player game was rejected because of 120 initiative permutations and the wish to reuse FS cards (Playbook p.3).

## Relevance

- Inferred: Germanic Settlement is a small model for a "presence" piece that scores and has costs; a PKK game could use a similar zone-holding marker for liberated or controlled villages.
- Inferred: the Arverni "At War" trigger icon is a light rule for a game-run Faction. A PKK game could use it for state forces or tribal militias that react only when threatened.
- Inferred: the Intimidate marker and Leader-bound Special Ability range show how to give an insurgent a deterrence tool and a geographic limit tied to a Leader.
- Inferred: for a digital Falling Sky, the expansion offers a clear delta structure (new Faction, Home Regions, Settlement, Interlude); the Arverni Phase and Non-player Germans flowchart are ready as algorithms (A6.2, A8.7).
