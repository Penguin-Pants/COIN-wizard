# Sovereign of Discord (expansion to Fire in the Lake): deltas from the base game

Convention: "Base game" means *Fire in the Lake* (FitL). Rule numbers with a "P" prefix are Sovereign of Discord (SoD) rules. Plain numbers are FitL rules that SoD keeps. Base-game rule text is not in the files read. Statements about the base game come only from what the SoD text says about it.

## Identity

- Title: *Sovereign of Discord*. A prequel expansion. A copy of FitL is required (P1.0).
- Series: COIN Series, Volume IV (rulebook cover).
- Rules author as printed: Stephen Rangazas (rulebook cover).
- Playbook credits: Game Design Stephen Rangazas. Original FitL design Mark Herman and Volko Ruhnke. Development Joe Dewhurst. COIN Series Development Jason Carr. Minh Mạng solitaire design Joe Dewhurst. Solitaire development Kevin Crooks. Jacquard system design Bruce Mansfield (Playbook, Game Credits).
- Edition: (c) 2023 GMT Games (rulebook footer). No edition number printed. Not verified.
- Setting: South Vietnam, 1961 to 1963. Kennedy policy, the Militant and Moderate debate in Hanoi, the rise of the NLF and the last years of the Diem regime (P1.0).
- Scale and players: 1 to 4 players. New 2-player scenario Diem's War: VC vs ARVN (P1.5).
- Components added (P1.2): 80 cards (Event, Pivotal, Coup), 6 orange Strategic Hamlet houses, 6 US Advisors (green cubes taken from FitL Troops), NVA Cadre counters, Washington Track, Hanoi Track, Regime Stability Track, 2-player Initiative tile, 14 Non-player cards (Minh Mạng bot), 40 bonus FitL Event cards.
- Rule numbering: SoD rules use the base number with "P" in front. Unchanged base rules are skipped (P1.0).
- Not used in SoD scenarios: foreign countries (North Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia are out of play) (P1.3.5), Tunnel Bases (P1.4), the Trail Track (P6.7), the Commitment Phase (P6.5), Momentum Events (P5.1.1), NVA Resources (P1.8), NVA Control (P1.7). Set aside forces: 40 NVA Troops, 20 NVA Guerrillas, 9 NVA Bases, 10 ARVN Troops, 3 Rangers, 2 ARVN Bases, 34 US Troops, 5 US Bases, 3 Irregulars (P1.4).
- Scenarios (P2.1):

| Scenario | Players | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Four Player 1961-1963 "Riding the Tiger's Back" | 4 | Main scenario. |
| Two Player 1961-1963 "Diem's War" | 2 | VC and ARVN only. |
| Short Prequel 1963 "Sink or Swim with Ngo Dinh Diem" | any | One campaign. Then Fall of Diem. Not for stand-alone play. No victory. |
| Full Campaign 1961-1972 "Diem to Paris" | 4 | SoD then Fall of Diem then FitL Full 1964-1972 deck. |
| Extended Campaign 1961-1975 | 4 | As above, then Paris Peace into Fall of Saigon. |
| Short "Long 1964" | any | FitL scenario, 1964 Events, no Pivotal Events. |
| Short 1967-1969 "Turning Point" | any | FitL scenario, Tet Offensive card acts as first Coup. |

- Variants (P2.1 end): US Coup Involvement (US may turn a named Coup into a Failed Attempt and keep the RVN Leader), Shorter FitL Campaigns (8-card stacks), Uncertain Victory (no win before the last Coup in long campaigns).

## Factions

Same four Factions as base game: US, ARVN, NVA, VC (P1.5). Thresholds are lower and "exceeds" is used (P7.2). Same as base game for faction identity and type.

| Faction | Type | Victory formula (exceeds) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| US | Government | Total Support - 2 x Washington Track level, over 24 | New formula. No Available count. |
| ARVN | Government | COIN-Controlled Population + Patronage, over 40 | Threshold 40. |
| NVA | Insurgent | Uncontrolled Population + NVA Cadres on map, over 20 | New formula. No NVA Control. |
| VC | Insurgent | Total Opposition + VC Bases on map, over 35 | Same number. Opposition ignores Provinces with Strategic Hamlets. |

- Final margins (P7.3): US Support - 2 x Washington - 24. NVA Uncontrolled + Cadres - 20. ARVN COIN-Controlled + Patronage - 40. VC Opposition + VC Bases - 35.
- Three new threshold markers show 24, 20 and 40 on the edge track. A fourth marks VC 35 (P1.9).
- Two new score markers: "Uncontrolled + Cadres" (NVA) and "Support - 2x Washington" (US) (P1.9).
- NVA has no Resources. Its cylinder is set aside. NVA adds to VC Resources through Direct. When NVA Passes it adds 1 VC Resource (P1.8, P4.4.1).
- NVA has no Control. NVA wins by leaving spaces Uncontrolled (P1.7).
- Regime Stability sets ARVN cost. Stable 2, Unstable 3, Chaotic 4 Resources per space (P6.10.2).
- Diem's War: no US or NVA pieces or markers. The Washington and Hanoi Tracks still feed ARVN and VC (P6.8, P6.9). US and NVA victory markers are set aside (P7.4 box).
- New pieces (P1.4):
  - Strategic Hamlet (ARVN, 6): Provinces only, one per Province, outside the 2-Base limit (P1.4.2). They count for COIN Control. They block VC Rally and Tax (P3.3.1, P4.5.1). Start Out of Play. Become Available through the ARVN Pivotal Event or some Events.
  - Advisor (US, 6): count for COIN Control. Help ARVN Sweep and Assault.
  - NVA Cadre: sits on a VC Base, one per Base, max 2 per space. Does not count for Control (P1.4.2, P1.7).
- Design note: Advisors stand for the many US advisers. Cadres stand for Northern political direction, not armed forces (P1.4 design note).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- 4-player: same as base game 4-player sequence. Each Faction gets a new Pivotal Event (P2.3.8).
- Diem's War: 2-player sequence copied from *Colonial Twilight* (P2.3 note). Rules match Fall of Saigon Black April: one visible card, First and Second Eligible, boxes Event, Limited Op, Op Only, Op and Special Activity, Pass. Pass gives +1 Resource for VC or +3 for ARVN. Both may Pass. Order shifts only after an Op with Special Activity or a multi-space Op. Pivotal Event: Second Eligible wins a conflict. No Monsoon (P2.3.1 to P2.3.7).
- Ignore Faction symbols and "stay Eligible" and "Ineligible" text in 2-player play (P2.3.1, P5.1.1).
- Coup cards: the three SoD Coup cards are not RVN Leaders (P2.4.1). The last one, Officer Coup, ends the game at once with no Coup Round (P2.4.2).
- One fixed RVN Leader: Ngo Dinh Diem for the whole game (P2.4.3).
- Coup card names named in the files: Buddhist Crisis and Officer Coup (P6.10.1, P2.4.2). The first Coup card name is not verified.
- Event text changes (P5.1.1): in Diem's War ignore Event text for US or NVA Operations, Special Activities, pieces and Cadres. Events may still move the Washington and Hanoi Tracks. No Momentum Events.

## Operations and Special Activities

Same as base game except as listed.

**US (P3.2, P4.2, P6.8.2)**
- Only Train, Advise (as Operation) and Interdict. No Patrol, Sweep or Assault. At Washington 0 the US cannot Advise or Interdict as an Operation (it can still Train).
- Train: costs 3 per space where ARVN pieces are placed. May place ARVN pieces in Cities with US pieces (P3.2.1).
- Advise (Special Activity, P4.2.1): accompanies Train or Interdict. May Activate an Underground Irregular, not Rangers. Sweep in place and Assault with ARVN only where a US Advisor is present. Each such Sweep or Assault affects one extra VC piece. Aid added equals Washington level. Removing a VC Base also removes its Cadre.
- Advise as an Operation (P3.2.5): 0 Resources, up to Washington spaces, Aid equal to Washington level. May combine with Air Lift.
- Interdict (new Operation, P3.2.6): pick Provinces with VC Bases within one space of US pieces, up to Washington level (1 if Limited). In each, shift one level toward Active Opposition and roll a die. If the roll is at or below the VC pieces there, VC Resources fall by 2. On a 1 or 2, also remove one Cadre from a VC Base there. Cost is not stated in the text read. Not verified.
- Air Lift (P4.2.2): not Available at Washington 0. Two spaces at Washington 1 to 2. Three spaces at 3 to 4. Move up to 4 Troops, Irregulars or Rangers (6 at Washington 4). Then redeploy Advisors to any COIN-Controlled or City spaces. Max two spaces in Monsoon. Accompanies Train, Advise or Interdict.
- No Air Strike for the US (P4.0).

**ARVN**
- All ARVN Operations cost by Regime Stability (2 to 4 per space, or per Patrol) (P3.2.1 to P3.2.4, P6.10.2).
- Sweep: one US Advisor may go with moving Troops if US agrees. Strategic Hamlets count with cubes and Rangers when Activating Guerrillas (P3.2.3).
- Assault: removing a VC Base with a Cadre also removes the Cadre. A removed VC Base adds Aid 3, not 6 (P3.2.4).
- Govern (P4.3.1): instead of adding Aid or moving Aid to Patronage, place a Strategic Hamlet in a COIN-Controlled Province, even with no Support. Shift that Province one level toward Active Opposition. Choose per selected space.
- Raid: a removed VC Base takes its Cadre (P4.3.3).
- Diem's War: ARVN may Air Lift as if US, ignoring Irregulars and Advisors (P4.3.4).

**NVA (P3.3.5 to P3.3.7, P4.4)**
- NVA's four FitL Operations are replaced. NVA may only Infiltrate, Direct or Propagandize. At Hanoi Track 0 NVA cannot Infiltrate or Direct as an Operation (P3.3).
- Infiltrate (Operation): choose Provinces in the South up to Hanoi level (1 if Limited). In each place 1 VC Guerrilla, or 1 Cadre on a VC Base that has none. In one space, shift Opposition one level toward Neutral (P3.3.5).
- Direct (Operation or Special Activity, P3.3.6, P4.4.1): up to 2 spaces with VC Guerrillas. In each, a free VC Attack that removes 1 extra enemy piece, or from a VC Base with a Cadre a free March to an adjacent space not chosen for Direct. Then add VC Resources equal to Hanoi level. As an Operation it covers Hanoi level spaces. It may combine with Bombard. It may accompany Infiltrate or Propagandize.
- Propagandize (P3.3.7): pick 1 or 2 Cities with Support next to Cadres (two at Hanoi 0 to 1, one at Hanoi 2 and up). For each, roll a die. If the roll is at or above Total Support in that City (not the map-wide total), shift the City one level toward Neutral. Then optionally roll one more die. If it is at or below Washington level, Aid falls by 3.
- Bombard (changed, P4.4.2): accompanies any NVA Operation. Pick spaces with 2 or more VC Guerrillas and enemy pieces: one space at Hanoi 2, two at Hanoi 3 to 4, none at Hanoi 0 to 1. Remove 1 enemy piece per space (US to Casualties). Diem's War: VC may use Bombard as a Special Activity with any VC Operation (P4.5.4).

**VC**
- Rally: not in a space with a Strategic Hamlet (P3.3.1). Tax: not in such a space (P4.5.1).
- March: Strategic Hamlets count with cubes and Rangers when Activating Guerrillas (P3.3.2).
- Attack: Strategic Hamlets are removed after Troops, Police and Rangers. Advisors after ARVN cubes and Special Forces. Advisors go to Casualties with no Attrition. Strategic Hamlets go to Available (P3.3.3).
- Subvert: in one space, VC may remove a Strategic Hamlet instead of ARVN cubes (no Guerrilla replaces it). It counts toward Patronage loss. VC may also remove one Cadre per selected space, but only instead of removing any ARVN pieces in that space (P4.5.2).
- Ambush: same as base game (P4.5.3).

## Event cards and deck

- 80 SoD cards: Event, Pivotal Event and Coup cards (P1.2). Event cards are split into 1961, 1962 and 1963 sets of 24, so 72 Event cards (P2.1). There are three Coup cards, the last one Officer Coup (P2.4.1, P2.4.2) and four Pivotal Events (Playbook P76 to P79). 72 + 3 + 4 = 79, so one of the 80 cards is not identified in the rule text read (not verified). It is not a fourth Coup card.
- Mini-deck build (P2.1): VC, ARVN, NVA, then US each openly pick one card per year (only VC and ARVN in Diem's War). Add 4 random cards per year (6 in Diem's War). Shuffle each year's 8 cards. Shuffle that year's Coup card among the bottom 4. Stack 1961 on 1962 on 1963.
- First-game draft suggested (P2.1 play note): 1961 Taylor Rostow Mission, Politburo, Catholic Refugees, Exterminate Traitors. 1962 Mike Mansfield, Ho Chi Minh Trail, Ngo Dinh Nhu, Coup Threat. 1963 Nhu Must Go, Nguyen Chi Thanh, Nhu's Counterplot, Thich Quang Duc.
- Pivotal Events (Playbook cards P76 to P79): all are "Play after NLF Spreads". None if a Coup is next.
  - US Best and Brightest: shift Washington 1 either way. Move 4 Patronage to ARVN Resources.
  - NVA Le Duan: shift Hanoi 2 spaces toward Militants. VC Resources +5.
  - ARVN Strategic Hamlets: also needs Opposition + Bases at 20 or more. All Hamlets to Available. Place 3 in Provinces and shift each 1 step toward Active Opposition.
  - VC Summer Offensive: also needs 20 or more VC Guerrillas in the South. Free Marches, then free Attacks that remove Hamlets first.
- Coup cards: Buddhist Crisis lowers Regime Stability (P6.10.1). Officer Coup is the last card. If Stability is Chaotic, VC removes one Hamlet and US shifts two Cities one level toward Active Support. Otherwise Patronage +6 (P7.3, Playbook).
- Capabilities exist (the Reset step removes SoD Capability markers, P7.4 Step 5). No Momentum Events (P5.1.1).
- Bonus Events: 40 extra FitL cards. 8 for 1964, 16 for 1965, 16 for 1968. Shuffle them into FitL decks. Period Events option recommended (P2.1).
- Historical content: the Playbook carries long background notes per card on Diem, Nhu, the Buddhist crisis, advisers and strategic hamlets (Playbook, Event Text, Tips and Background).
- Design notes (Playbook p.41): the designer lists limits of the model, such as civilian harm and many-model design. Not read in detail.

## Map, spaces and tracks

Same map as base game. North Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia are out of play (P1.3.5). Same as base game except as listed.

- Washington Track (P6.8): levels 0 to 4. Governs US Advise spaces and Aid, Interdict spaces, Air Lift, Advisors available (two each at levels 1, 2, 3), and US victory. Gives ARVN 2 Troops and 2 Police at each of levels 1 to 3 (P6.8.2, P6.8.3).
- Hanoi Track (P6.9): levels 0 to 4. Governs Infiltrate, Direct, Propagandize and Bombard spaces and Available Cadres (two each at levels 1, 2, 3). Gives VC 5 Guerrillas and 1 Base at each of levels 2 and 3 (P6.9.2, P6.9.3). Two "On Track" markers cover the VC 8th and 9th Base spots (P6.9.3).
- Track moves (P6.8.1, P6.9.1): Events control normal movement. Reset applies an automatic drift: 0 goes to 1 and 4 goes to 3.
- Placing pieces (P6.8.4, P6.9.4): when a track rises, pieces on it go to Available at once (Advisors to COIN-Controlled or City spaces). When it falls, nothing is removed at once. Pieces return to the track in Reset.
- Regime Stability Track (P6.10): Stable, Unstable, Chaotic, plus a flip side "Crisis" when lowered again from Chaotic. Improving from Crisis flips to normal side. Each fall from Stable to Unstable or Unstable to Chaotic cuts Aid by 5 (P6.10.3).
- Support and Opposition: Total Opposition ignores the Population of Provinces with Strategic Hamlets (P1.6.2).
- Control: no NVA Control. Advisors and Hamlets count for COIN Control (P1.7).
- Four-player start: Aid 10, Patronage 10, Econ 15, Resources VC 5 and ARVN 20, Hanoi 1, Washington 0, Stability Stable (P2.1). Diem's War uses the same values with VC First Eligible.
- Short Prequel 1963 start: Aid 15, Patronage 10, Hanoi 3, Washington 3, Stability Unstable (P2.1).

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

Base game Coup Round order is kept, with one phase removed (Commitment, P6.5) and one dropped track (Trail, P6.7). Deltas:

1. Victory (P6.1, P7.2): use the new formulas. Solitaire: only the final Coup can award the player a win (bot rule P8.3.1).
2. Resources (P6.2): in Diem's War, add 3 x Washington level to Aid before Aid goes to ARVN Resources (P6.2.3). NVA keeps no Resources (P6.2.4). In Diem's War, add Hanoi level to VC Resources (P6.2.4). US Casualties do not reduce Aid (P6.2.5).
3. Support (P6.3): US may Pacify where there is COIN Control, Police and Advisors (P6.3.1). The Diem Leader card adds 3 Patronage in this phase and lets VC Agitate in one extra space (P6.3.1 note).
4. Redeploy (P6.4): US may redeploy Advisors to COIN-Controlled or City spaces (P6.4.2). No NVA Redeploy (P6.4.3). Step 6.4.5 is dropped (P6.4.5).
5. Commitment: none (P6.5).
6. Reset (P6.6): return US Casualties to the Washington Track (or the US Base to Saigon). Move Washington and Hanoi from 0 to 1 and 4 to 3. Pieces on tracks at or below the current level enter play. Pieces above it leave Available and the map, until the track spaces are full (VC example: Hanoi at 2 forces VC to remove 5 Guerrillas and 1 Base first from Available, then from the map). In 2-player play set VC First Eligible and skip "All Eligible" and the new top-card reveal.
7. Trail: the Trail Track is not used (P6.7).

**Game end (P7.3)**: Officer Coup ends the game. Apply its effect (Chaotic: VC removes a Hamlet and US shifts two Cities toward Active Support. Otherwise Patronage +6). The Faction with the highest margin wins.

**Fall of Diem (P7.4)**: optional bridge into FitL when nobody exceeds a threshold. It runs in five steps.
- Step 1, North Vietnam: remove the Hanoi Track and Cadres. Move NVA pieces to Available. Trail set to 1. "Ninth Plenum": place 3 NVA Guerrillas and 1 Base each in North Vietnam, Central Laos and the Parrot's Beak. If Hanoi was 2 to 3 or higher, add Southern Laos. If Hanoi was 4, add Northeast Cambodia and Trail 2. Swap to the "NVA Control + Bases" marker.
- Step 2, Viet Cong: remove Guerrillas above two per space. Put a Base where Guerrillas were removed and no Base exists. VC may Tunnel one Base. Provinces with a VC Base shift toward Active Opposition. Other Provinces shift toward Neutral.
- Step 3, United States: remove the Washington Track. Place 10 US Troops and 2 Bases Out of Play, then move all US Casualties and all US Troops, Advisors and Bases on the map to Available before Commitment. "US Commitment" depends on Washington level: levels 0 to 1 place 6 Troops and 1 Base (Aid 10), levels 2 to 3 place 9 Troops and 2 Bases (Aid 15), level 4 places 9 Troops, 3 Bases and 3 more Troops from Out of Play (Aid 20). Advisors become US Troops. Swap to the "Support + Available" marker.
- Step 4, South Vietnam: remove the Stability Track. Remove 1 in 3 ARVN cubes per space. Redeploy Troops. If Stability was Chaotic, Hamlets are removed, Diem is replaced by Duong Van Minh, Patronage set to 15 and Cities shift one level toward Passive Support. Otherwise Diem stays and Hamlets stay until a new Leader covers Diem.
- Step 5, Reset: fix Control. Resources VC 5, NVA 10, ARVN 30. Remove SoD Capabilities and Terror and Sabotage markers. Flip Guerrillas and Special Forces Underground. Build a new FitL deck (Full 1964-1972 or "Long 1964"). Play the next card.
- If Diem survives, Hamlets keep affecting ARVN Sweep and Govern, VC Rally March Attack Tax and Subvert, and Opposition. NVA Attack removes Hamlets in the same order as VC. Both must remove US Troops before Hamlets (P7.4 Important box).

## Non-player (bot) system

Minh Mạng, by Joe Dewhurst. Replaces base game section 8.0 for SoD games (P8.1). Uses the card-based Jacquard design (Playbook credits).

- Cards: NP ARVN and NP VC each have 6 cards, shuffled together face up. NP US and NP NVA each use one single card kept beside the board (P8.1.1, P8.4.1, P8.5.3).
- Aids: separate aid sets for 4-player and Diem's War. Tables: Space Selection (one per Faction), Move, Pieces, Eligibility and four Event tables (P8.1.1).
- Card reading: same as Tây Sơn, move the top card to the bottom until a matching Faction. Blue boxes with green and red arrows. Flip or redraw arrows (P8.5.3).
- Activation Number: 1d6 roll after each space. At or below the number ends the Operation. NP ARVN's number equals the Regime Stability value. No rolls for NVA, NP US Advise and Interdict, ARVN Patrol, VC LoC March and Terror (P8.5.4).
- Follow-up actions after a failed roll: NP US Pacifies or moves Patronage after Train. NP ARVN places a Base or Pacifies. NP NVA shifts Opposition after Infiltrate (only if VC is a player). NP NVA adds VC Resources after Direct (only if VC is a player and VC has under 6) (P8.5.4).
- Resources: NP VC uses its Resource cylinder as an "Agitate Total". It starts at 1d3, gains from Tax and the Hanoi level in Coup Rounds, loses 1 per US Interdict success and is spent on Agitate (P8.2 terms, P8.4.1). NP VC Taxes only in 1961 and 1962 (before Buddhist Crisis) (P8.7.7).
- Pivotal triggers (P8.5.4): NP US at COIN Control + Patronage over 38 or under 6 cards left. NP ARVN at 2 or more Provinces at Active Opposition. NP NVA at Hanoi under 3. NP VC at COIN Control + Patronage over 38, or 4 or more Guerrillas next to a Hamlet with Opposition (the VC line is read from the text, wording is split across lines).
- Events: US and ARVN use unshaded text. VC and NVA use shaded text, unless the Event Instructions table says otherwise (P8.5.5). 1st Eligible NP US at Washington 0 and NP NVA at Hanoi 0 also play Performed Events.
- Faction priorities for Events (P8.8): NP ARVN raises Patronage, Aid, Regime Stability and Washington, and shifts toward Passive Support. NP VC and NVA raise Hanoi and shift toward Active Opposition or Neutral. NP US lowers Hanoi and Patronage.
- Bot play highlights: NP ARVN Governs to place Hamlets at Opposition first (P8.7.5). NP VC Subverts to remove Hamlets (P8.7.7). NP NVA Infiltrates to place Cadres first, then Guerrillas (P8.6.6). NP US trains to Pacify. NP US always agrees to Advisors moving with ARVN Sweep unless an Advisor is already in the space (P8.6.5).
- If Diem survives into FitL, NP ARVN always Governs to place Hamlets and NP VC always Subverts to remove them (P8.7.5, P8.7.7). Use the FitL bot after Fall of Diem. Use Tây Sơn from the Paris Talks on (P8.2).
- Solitaire: the player may win only at the final Coup in 1963 (P8.3.1). Bonus Events can run under either FitL bot (P8.10).
- Randomness: Activation rolls, 1d3 Pacify caps, 2d6 gates for card choice, random tie-breaks (P8.5.4, P8.6.5, Example of Play).
- An 8-page worked solitaire example is in the bot book (P8.9). Not read in detail.

## Unique mechanics

- Washington and Hanoi Tracks: five-level tracks that control how much each outside sponsor does (P6.8, P6.9). Pieces wait on the tracks.
- Regime Stability Track: sets ARVN Operation cost, with a Crisis flip side (P6.10).
- NVA with no Resources, no Control and no troops. NVA scores by Uncontrolled spaces and Cadres (P1.7, P7.2).
- Cadres: tokens on VC Bases that give NVA influence over VC pieces (P1.4).
- Strategic Hamlets: ARVN pieces that deny VC options and cancel Opposition scoring (P1.6.2, P3.3.1).
- US Advisors: soft power pieces that count for Control (P1.4).
- New US Operation Interdict and three new NVA Operations (P3.2.6, P3.3.5 to P3.3.7).
- Fixed RVN Leader (Diem) and a final Officer Coup that can end the game (P2.4).
- Fall of Diem bridge into FitL, with leftover track values changing the FitL start (P7.4).
- Prequel built to teach the game in 2-player form (P1.0).

## Relevance

- Inferred: the Hanoi Track pattern, a small level track that releases pieces and caps an outside sponsor's action count, fits a PKK game with external patrons or safe-haven support.
- Inferred: the Cadre token that sits on a Base and directs insurgent pieces without counting for Control is a model for a party-over-guerrilla layer.
- Inferred: the Regime Stability Track, which sets government Operation cost, is a simple way to show state weakness.
- Inferred: for a digital Falling Sky, the Minh Mạng habit of a single fixed card for minor Factions and a shuffled deck for major ones shows a low-cost bot design.
