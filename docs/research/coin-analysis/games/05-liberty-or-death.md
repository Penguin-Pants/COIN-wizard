## Factions

Four Factions. Two sides: Royalists (British and Indians) and Rebellion (Patriots and French) (1.5.1). Allies do not share victory (1.5.1). Each Faction has two victory conditions (7.0).

| Faction | Type | Victory check (7.2) | Game-end margin (7.3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| British | Government, Royalist | Support exceeds Opposition by more than 10 AND CRC greater than CBC | (Support - Opposition) + CRC - CBC |
| Patriots | Insurgent, Rebellion | Opposition exceeds Support by more than 10 AND Patriot Forts + 3 greater than Villages | (Opposition - Support) + (Patriot Forts + 3 - Villages) |
| French | Foreign ally, Rebellion | Opposition exceeds Support by more than 10 AND CBC greater than CRC. Treaty of Alliance (TOA) must be played. | (Opposition - Support) + CBC - CRC |
| Indians | Insurgent ally, Royalist | Support exceeds Opposition by more than 10 AND Villages - 3 greater than Patriot Forts | (Support - Opposition) + (Villages - 3 - Patriot Forts) |

Rules for victory:
- At a victory check, both conditions must show a positive margin (7.1, 7.2).
- At game end, margins add together. Negative totals can still win. The highest total wins (7.3).
- Total Support = 2 x Pop at Active Support + 1 x Pop at Passive Support. Opposition works the same way (1.6.3).
- CBC counts British Regular, Tory and Fort casualties. CRC counts French Regular, Continental and Patriot Fort casualties (1.6.4).
- Non-player win at a check means all players lose (7.1). Without TOA, the French rank last (7.1).
- Ties break in order: Non-players, Patriots, British, French, Indians (7.1).

Resources: 0 to 50 per Faction (1.8). Pass gives +1 (Patriots, Indians) or +2 (British, French) (2.3.3).

Pieces (Available Forces table, rules p.35):

| Faction | Pieces | Leaders |
|---|---|---|
| British | 25 Regulars, 25 Tories, 6 Forts | Gage, Howe, Clinton |
| Indians | 15 War Parties, 12 Villages | Brant, Cornplanter, Dragging Canoe |
| Patriots | 20 Continentals, 15 Militia, 6 Forts | Washington |
| French | 15 Regulars, 3 Squadrons/Blockades | Rochambeau, Lauzun |

Leader capabilities (1.10):
- Washington: doubles Rebellion Win the Day shift in the space. -1 to Defender Loss Level when Rebellion defends there.
- Rochambeau: French March and Battle with Patriot Command at no cost.
- Lauzun: +1 to Defender Loss Level when French attack in the space.
- Gage: first Reward Loyalty shift is free in the space.
- Howe: before a British Special Activity, may first lower FNI one level.
- Clinton: British Skirmish removes one more Militia in the space.
- Brant: War Path removes one more Militia in the space.
- Cornplanter: Gather builds Villages for one War Party in the space.
- Dragging Canoe: Raid may move one extra space if it starts in the space.

Notes:
- Indians cannot Control a space and cannot enter Cities (1.4.2, 1.7).
- French start off map. Before TOA they only fund the Patriots, place Patriot units and prepare forces (2.3.9).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

Core flow follows the COIN series (2.3):
- Two cards are visible: the current card and the next card (2.2).
- Eligible Factions act in left-to-right order of the symbols on the card (2.3.2).
- 1st Eligible may Pass, execute a Command with or without a Special Activity, or play the Event (2.3.4).
- 2nd Eligible options depend on the 1st (2.3.4):

| 1st Eligible did | 2nd Eligible may |
|---|---|
| Command only | Limited Command |
| Command and Special Activity | Limited Command or the Event |
| Event | Command, with Special Activity if wanted |

- Limited Command: one space, no Special Activity (2.3.5).
- Pass gives Resources and keeps the Faction Eligible. The next Eligible Faction takes the slot (2.3.3).
- A Faction that executed a Command or Event is Ineligible on the next card (2.3.1, 2.3.6).
- A Command counts as executed if it selects at least one space. Exception: Roderigue Hortalez et Cie counts when at least one French Resource is paid (2.3.4).
- Free Commands do not cost Resources and do not change Eligibility (3.1.2, 5.3).

Changes from the series core:
- Winter Quarters card (replaces Propaganda or Coup): when it is revealed as the next card, it swaps with the played card. The Winter Quarters Round runs at once (2.2, 2.3.7, 2.4).
- Winter is Coming option: play the old card first, then run Winter Quarters (2.3.7).
- Brilliant Stroke: each Faction holds one card. The French hold two, including the TOA card (2.3.8).
  - It cancels the current Event card if the holder is Eligible, the 1st Eligible has not acted and no Winter Quarters card shows (2.3.8).
  - The holder's Leader must be in a space in at least one Limited Command (1.10).
  - Executes with no Resource cost, except British Reward Loyalty. All Factions become Eligible afterward (2.3.8).
  - Trump order: TOA cannot be Trumped. Indians Trump any other Faction except via TOA. French Trump Patriots or British. British Trump Patriots. Patriots cannot Trump (2.3.8).
- Treaty of Alliance: playable when French Preparations exceed 15. Preparations = Available French Regulars + Squadrons/Blockades + CBC (2.3.9). The French must be Eligible. The French execute only that Event (2.3.9).
- A Campaign is the series of cards up to one Winter Quarters card, one war year (2.4).

## Operations and Special Activities

Commands cost Resources per space unless noted (3.1). Special Activities cost nothing extra and run before, during or after the Command (4.1). Limited Commands get no Special Activity (4.1).

British Commands (3.2):
- Muster: 1 Resource per space. Place up to 6 Regulars in one City, adjacent Colony or the West Indies. Place up to 2 Tories (1 if Passive Opposition) in spaces with or next to British Regulars or Forts. In one space, replace 3 cubes with a Fort or Reward Loyalty (3.2.1).
- Garrison: 2 Resources total. Move Regulars into Cities. Activate 1 Militia per 3 cubes. Displace Rebellion units from one City with British Control and no Patriot Fort. Unavailable at FNI 3 (3.2.2).
- March: 1 Resource per destination. Tories move 1 for 1 with Regulars. Sea movement between Cities. Activate 1 Militia per 3 British cubes at destinations (3.2.3).
- Battle: 1 Resource per space (3.2.4).

British Special Activities (4.2):
- Common Cause: use War Parties as Tories in March or Battle. Activates them (4.2.1).
- Skirmish: remove Rebellion pieces with Regulars in one space. Not with Battle, Garrison or Muster there (4.2.2).
- Naval Pressure: before TOA, +1D3 Resources. After TOA, +1D3 if FNI is 0, else lower FNI one level (4.2.3).

Patriot Commands (3.3):
- Rally: 1 Resource per space without Active Support. Place 1 Militia or replace 2 units with a Fort. With a Fort: place Militia up to Forts + Pop, or move Militia in and flip all Underground. One Fort space may swap Militia for Continentals (3.3.1).
- March: 1 Resource per destination. French pay 1 more per space they enter. Activate 1 War Party per 2 Continentals at the destination (3.3.2).
- Battle: 1 Resource per space. French pay 1 more per space they join (3.3.3).
- Rabble-Rousing: 1 Resource per space. Place a Propaganda marker and shift one level toward Active Opposition. Needs Rebellion Control plus a Patriot piece or Underground Militia (3.3.4).

Patriot Special Activities (4.3):
- Persuasion: in up to 3 Rebellion-controlled Colonies or Cities with Underground Militia, Activate one Militia, +1 Resource and a Propaganda marker each (4.3.1).
- Partisans: Activate Underground Militia to remove Royalist pieces in one space, not with Battle (4.3.2).
- Skirmish: Continentals remove British pieces in one space (4.3.3).

Indian Commands (3.4):
- Gather: 1 Resource per Province, first Reserve Province free. Only in Provinces at Neutral or Passive Support or Opposition. Place 1 War Party, or build a Village by replacing 2 War Parties. With a Village: place up to Villages + 1 War Parties or move War Parties in (3.4.1).
- March: 1 Resource per destination Province. War Parties cannot enter Cities (3.4.2).
- Scout: Indians and British each pay 1. War Parties take British cubes into an adjacent Province, then Skirmish (3.4.3).
- Raid: up to 3 Provinces at Opposition. 1 Resource each. Activate a War Party, place a Raid marker, shift one level toward Neutral (3.4.4).
- Indians cannot Battle (3.6.1).

Indian Special Activities (4.4):
- Trade: British choose a transfer to the Indians, or Indians gain 1 Resource if the British give 0. Needs Underground War Party and Village (4.4.1).
- War Path: remove Rebellion pieces in one space (4.4.2).
- Plunder: with Raid, if War Parties exceed Rebellion pieces, take Resources equal to Pop from Patriots. Indians lose one War Party (4.4.3).

French Commands (3.5):
- French Agent Mobilization (before TOA): 1 Resource. Place 2 Militia or 1 Continental in Quebec, New York, New Hampshire or Massachusetts (3.5.1).
- Roderigue Hortalez et Cie: pay X French Resources, Patriots gain X + 1 (3.5.2).
- Muster (after TOA): 2 Resources. Place up to 4 Regulars in a Rebellion Colony, City or the West Indies. May swap 2 Regulars for a Patriot Fort if Patriots pay 1 (3.5.3).
- March (after TOA): 1 Resource per destination. Patriots pay 1 more per space with Continentals (3.5.4).
- Battle (after TOA): 1 Resource per space. Patriots pay 1 more per space they join (3.5.5).

French Special Activities (4.5):
- Preparer la Guerre: move a Squadron to the West Indies, or 3 Regulars from Unavailable to Available, or gain 2 Resources (4.5.1).
- Skirmish (after TOA) (4.5.2).
- Naval Pressure (after TOA): raise FNI one level and place or rearrange Blockades (4.5.3).

Battle procedure (3.6):
- Force Level (attack limits: British Attack counts Tories only up to the number of British Regulars. Rebellion Attack adds French Regulars or Continentals only if that Faction paid, up to the acting Faction's own cube count. Defense counts all that Side's cubes) = cubes + half Active Militia or War Parties (rounded down) + Forts when defending. Roll one D3 per 3 Force Level, max 3 dice. Force Level 2 or less rolls nothing (3.6.3, 3.6.4).
- Defender Loss Level modifiers: +1 if half the attackers are Regulars, +1 if any attacker Underground, +1 if attacking Leader, +1 French with Lauzun, -1 per Defending Fort, -1 for Washington, -1 for British in a Blockaded City or in the West Indies with a Squadron (3.6.5).
- Attacker Loss Level modifiers: +1 if half the defending cubes are Regulars, +1 if any defender Underground, +1 for a defending Leader, +1 per defending Fort, -1 for British Defending in a Blockaded City or in the West Indies with a Squadron (3.6.6).
- Regulars, Continentals and Forts count as 2 losses. Other pieces count as 1 (3.6.7).
- Removal order is fixed. Underground pieces are ignored (3.6.7).
- Win the Day: only if the loser removes a cube or a Fort and removes 2 or more pieces, and not in the West Indies. Shift Support or Opposition by half the loser's removed pieces (round down, max 3 levels). If Rebellion wins, Washington can double this to no more than 6. Rebellion winner gets a free Rally (3.6.8).

## Event cards and deck

- 109 cards: 96 Event cards, 8 Winter Quarters cards, 5 Brilliant Stroke cards (Set-Up p.36).
- Event cards show years, a Faction order, flavor text and Event text (5.0).
- Dual Use: most Events have unshaded text (often Royalist) and shaded text (often Rebellion). Any Faction may pick either (5.2).
- Event text beats rules, except for stacking, pre-TOA French placement and Availability (5.1.1).
- Events may give free Commands that keep Eligibility unchanged (5.3).
- No Capabilities or Momentum markers are listed in this rulebook (not verified beyond the rules text read).
- Sword and Brown Bess Musket icons under Faction symbols steer Non-player decisions (8.1, 8.3.1).
- Deck build: deal 10 Event cards per Campaign. Shuffle one Winter Quarters card into the bottom 4 cards of each pile. Stack the piles (Set-Up p.36).
- Period Events Option: cards carry year bands '75-'76, '77-'78 and '79-'80, 32 cards each. Long, Medium and Short scenarios draw from the matching bands (Set-Up p.36).
- Brilliant Stroke cards are Events held by a Faction. TOA card is #109 (5.1.4, Set-Up p.37).
- Each Winter Quarters card also carries an Event that resolves at the end of the Reset Phase (6.0).
- Historical content: 12 Propaganda markers and 12 Raid markers carry historical flavor on their faces only (3.3.4, 3.4.4).

Space types (1.3, Spaces List p.40):

| Type | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cities | 7 | Quebec City 1, Boston 1, New York City 2, Philadelphia 1, Norfolk 1, Charles Town 1, Savannah 1. Total Pop 8. |
| Colonies | 11 | NH 1, MA 2, CT-RI 2, NY 2, NJ 1, PA 2, MD-DE 2, VA 2, NC 2, SC 2, GA 1. Total Pop 19. |
| Indian Reserve | 4 | Quebec, Northwest, Southwest, Florida. Pop 0. Always Neutral (1.3.4, 1.6.2). |
| West Indies | 1 box | Pop 0. Not in play before TOA. Holds only Regulars, British Forts and Squadrons (1.3.7, 1.4.2). |

- New York City and Philadelphia are displaced east on the map for playability (1.3.3).
- Adjacency: spaces that touch. British and French March also allow sea movement between Cities (3.2.3, 3.5.4). Map image not read, so adjacency lists are not verified.
- Stacking: at most 2 Forts or Villages (any Faction) per space (1.4.2).
- Underground and Active flags apply to Militia and War Parties (1.4.3).
- Five alignment levels: Active Support, Passive Support, Neutral, Passive Opposition, Active Opposition. Pop 0 spaces stay Neutral (1.6.1).
- Control: Rebellion if Patriot plus French pieces exceed Royalist pieces. British if Royalists exceed Rebellion and at least one British piece is present. Indians alone give no Control (1.7).
- Tracks on the edge: Resources (0 to 50), Total Support, Total Opposition, CBC, CRC, French Preparations (1.6.3, 1.6.4, 2.3.9). CBC and CRC may pass 50. Reset both down together (1.6.4).
- Available Villages and Patriot Forts share one track, offset by three (1.6.5).
- French Naval Intervention (FNI) track: 0 to 3 Blockades on Cities. FNI must stay 0 before TOA. FNI cannot exceed Available Blockades (1.9).
- Blockade effects: no British Muster of Regulars in or near the City, no Garrison to or from it, no British March into it or neighbors, its Pop counts as 0 for Support and Resources (1.9).
- Overflow boxes, Unavailable boxes and a Casualties box hold other pieces (1.3.8, 1.3.9, 1.6.4).

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

Winter Quarters Round (6.0), in order:
1. Victory Check (6.1): any Faction with both positive margins wins. French also need TOA played (7.2). A lone solo player never wins here (8.8).
2. Supply (6.2):
   - British cubes need a British Fort or a British-controlled City. Else pay 1 per space, remove cubes, or shift the space toward Active Opposition (6.2.1).
   - Patriots need a Patriot Fort or a Rebellion-controlled Colony or City. Else pay 1 per space or lose half the units (6.2.1).
   - French need a Patriot Fort or Rebellion Colony or City. Else move, pay or return to Available (6.2.1).
   - Indians need a Village or Reserve Province. If no Villages exist, place one in a Reserve Province (6.2.1).
   - Free French Battle in the West Indies if both sides are present (6.2.2).
3. Resources (6.3):
   - British: Forts + Pop of non-Blockaded British Cities, +5 if British Control the West Indies.
   - Indians: half of Villages (rounded down).
   - Patriots: Patriot Forts + half of Rebellion-controlled spaces (rounded down).
   - French: before TOA, 2 x Squadrons in the West Indies. After TOA, FNI + Pop of Cities not British-controlled, +5 if Rebellion controls the West Indies.
4. Support (6.4): British Reward Loyalty then Patriot Committees of Correspondence. Each Resource removes a marker or shifts a level, max 2 levels per space. If this is the final round, the game ends and 7.3 applies.
5. Redeployment (6.5): Leader Change from the first Faction on the next card, Leader Redeployment (Indians, French, British, Patriots), British Release Date, French FNI drop of one level.
6. Desertion (6.6): remove 1 in 5 Patriot Militia and Continentals, then 1 in 5 Tories.
7. Reset (6.7): remove Raid and Propaganda markers, mark all Eligible, Casualties to Available, flip Militia and War Parties Underground, reveal the next card, resolve the Winter Quarters Event.

The final round skips Redeployment, Desertion and Reset (6.4.3).

## Non-player (bot) system

Section 8 covers all four Factions. Flowcharts are on separate foldouts (8.0).

General rules (8.1):
- Limited Command slots become full Command plus Special Activity.
- Event or Command: a sword icon means ignore the Event. Otherwise check the "Event or Command?" box. A musket icon means special Event instructions apply.
- If Resources are 0, the Faction Passes.
- No voluntary removal of own pieces to place elsewhere.
- Non-player French play TOA as soon as Eligible when WI Squadrons + Available Regulars + half of CBC exceed 15 (8.1).
- Non-player Leaders follow the largest moving group (8.1).
- Dual Use: Patriot and French use shaded text. British and Indians use unshaded (8.3.2).
- Ineffective Events are skipped (8.3.3).
- Support/Opposition shifts pick the best space for the side (8.3.6).

Randomness: Random Spaces table uses 1D3 for the column and 1D6 for the row. It walks down the column to the first valid candidate (8.2). Other dice decide Muster, Rally and Gather triggers.

Per Faction, the order of Commands (priority list):

| Faction | Order tried |
|---|---|
| British | Garrison (10+ Regulars and a Rebel-controlled City without a Patriot Fort), then Muster (D6 below Available Regulars), then March, then Battle (8.4) |
| Patriots | Battle, then Rally, then Rabble-Rousing, then March (8.5) |
| French | Before TOA: Roderigue Hortalez or Agent Mobilization. After TOA: Muster, March, Battle (8.6) |
| Indians | Raid (if Support + 1D6 does not exceed Opposition), Gather, March, Scout (8.7) |

- Each Faction has rules for Supply, Leader Redeployment, Desertion and Brilliant Stroke (8.4.7 to 8.4.11, 8.5.5 to 8.5.9, 8.6.7 to 8.6.11, 8.7.5 to 8.7.9).
- One-player mode: play one Faction, or British plus Indians, or Patriots plus French (8.8). Use the lower margin of two Factions. The player must avoid any Non-player win. Final result is the player margin minus the best Non-player margin: 5 or less is a stalemate, 6 or more is a win (8.8).
- Difficulty option: from the second Winter Quarters, no player Faction may trail a Non-player in margin (8.8).

## Unique mechanics

- Four Factions with competitive victory conditions. Each pair is compared: Support against Opposition, CBC against CRC, Forts against Villages (7.2, Playbook p.14).
- Two-stage victory: all-positive margins at checks. Summed margins at game end, with no 10-point hurdle (7.1, 7.3).
- Winter Quarters card swap: the round happens on reveal, not on a Propaganda card draw (2.3.7).
- French enter the war late by the Treaty of Alliance, a Brilliant Stroke card gated by the French Preparations total (2.3.9).
- Brilliant Stroke cards and a Trump chain between Factions (2.3.8).
- West Indies box and French Naval Intervention with Blockades on Cities (1.3.7, 1.9).
- Leaders with capabilities that change over time by Leader Change (1.10, 6.5.1).
- Casualty tracks (CBC, CRC) that feed victory and TOA (1.6.4).
- Combined actions between allies: Patriots and French pay each other for joint March and Battle. British use Indian War Parties through Common Cause. Indians Scout with British cubes (3.3.2, 3.4.3, 4.2.1, Playbook p.14).
- Battle uses dice (D3 per 3 Force Level), Loss Levels and Win the Day shifts (3.6).
- Supply phase: each Faction must keep units in supply, with Faction-specific rules (6.2).
- Desertion phase removes 1 in 5 Patriot and Tory units (6.6).

## Relevance

- Inferred: the Patriot cube-and-Militia model (Underground Militia, Active/Underground flip, Rally with Forts) maps to a PKK guerrilla force. The Underground flag and the Rabble-Rousing and Persuasion cycle fit support-building in a population-centered war (1.4.3, 3.3, 4.3).
- Inferred: Supply and Desertion phases give a cheap way to model attrition and retreat to safe areas. A PKK design could tie Supply to border sanctuary spaces, as Indians do with Reserve Provinces (6.2.1, 6.6).
- Inferred: the French pre-TOA state is a template for an external patron that funds and trains an insurgent before it commits. It gives a pattern for a foreign-backed faction with limited early actions (2.3.9, 3.5.1, 3.5.2).
- Inferred: for a digital Falling Sky, the Non-player flowcharts with fixed priority lists plus a Random Spaces table are a ready template for a bot engine. The Event or Command? test with sword and musket icons is easy to encode (8.1, 8.2, 8.3).
