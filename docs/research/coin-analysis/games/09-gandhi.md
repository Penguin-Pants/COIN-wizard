# Gandhi: The Decolonization of British India, 1917-1947

## Identity

- **Title:** Gandhi: The Decolonization of British India, 1917-1947 (map title from image). COIN Series Volume IX (1.1).
- **Credits (Playbook p.52):** Game Design: Bruce Mansfield. Series Developer: Jason Carr. Series Designer: Volko Ruhnke. Map: Knut Gruenitz. Cards, charts, manuals: Charlie Kibler. Counters: Mark Simonitch. Art Director: Rodger B. MacGowan. Producers: Gene Billingsley, Tony Curtis, Andy Lewis, Rodger B. MacGowan, Mark Simonitch.
- **Rules file:** "Gandhi Living Rules, November 2023" (page 1). Copyright 2023 GMT Games. The aid sheets carry "Living Version November 2023".
- **Setting:** end of the British Raj in India, 1917 to 1947, up to India and Pakistan (1.0).
- **Scale:** one map of India. Spaces are Cities, Provinces, States and Railways (1.3.1). Population is a political weight, not a head count (Playbook p.47).
- **Players:** 1 to 4 (1.5). Each Faction can be a player or a Non-player (NP). 2-player rule: one player runs Raj plus Revolutionaries, the other runs Congress plus Muslim League (1.5, Setup). This differs from a "Raj vs Congress" pairing. Not verified: any rule for a pure Raj vs Congress duel.
- **Scenarios (Setup):**
  - Short, 1930 to 1947: 6 piles of 6 Events, 3 Campaign cards. Raj 32 Resources, Revolutionaries 5.
  - Main, 1917 to 1947: 4 piles of 12 Events, 1 Campaign card in the bottom six of each pile. Raj 35, Revolutionaries 8. Start Restraint 4, Unity 3.
- **Components:** 72 Event cards, 7 Campaign cards, 24 Arjuna cards, 125 wooden pieces, 4 dice (1.2).

## Factions

Four Factions. Two are nonviolent (NV): Congress and Muslim League (1.5). Victory is checked at each Campaign card (7.2).

| Faction | Type | Victory (exceeds) | Resources | Forces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| British Raj | COIN (government) | Total Support + Population of Raj-Controlled spaces > 38 (7.2) | Yes, 0 to 50 (1.8) | 15 Troops, 45 Sepoys (1.2) |
| Indian National Congress | NV | Total Opposition > 20 (7.2) | None | 16 Activists, Gandhi (leader) (1.4.5) |
| Muslim League | NV | Muslim Opposition + 2 x Population of Muslim States > 14 (7.2) | None | 16 Activists, 5 Bases |
| Revolutionaries | Insurgent | Population of Unrest spaces + Revolutionaries Bases > 14 (7.2) | Yes, 0 to 50 | 16 Guerrillas, 5 Bases |

- **Total Support** = 2 x Population in Active Support + 1 x Population in Passive Support. Opposition works the same way (1.6.3).
- **Victory margin** = Faction value minus its threshold (7.3). Ties go to Muslim League, then Congress, then Revolutionaries (7.1).
- **Two Factions, one player:** use the lower margin of the two. Play ends only if both meet their goals (1.5).
- **Raj Control:** only Raj can Control. Cubes must exceed all other Active pieces in a City or Province (1.7.1). Control is not a Congress or League asset.
- **NV pieces:** Activists are Active only in Protest spaces or on Railways. They are Inactive elsewhere (1.4.3). Gandhi is always Active and cannot be eliminated, only arrested (1.4.5).
- **Guerrillas:** Active or Underground, one piece at a time (1.4.3).
- **Congress and League do not pay Resources.** The tracks Restraint and Unity limit how many spaces they select (3.3).
- **Passing:** Raj gets +3 Resources, Revolutionaries +1. An NV Faction may move one Activist from Out of Play to Available (2.3.3).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- Standard COIN card-driven sequence (2.3). Two Eligible Factions act per Event card, in the order of icons on the card (2.3.2).
- **1st Eligible options:** Pass, Operation only, Operation plus Special Activity, or the Event (2.3.4).
- **2nd Eligible options:** after Op only, a Limited Op. After Op plus Special Activity, a Limited Op or the Event. After the Event, an Op with or without a Special Activity (2.3.4).
- **Limited Op:** one space, no Special Activity. Garrison, Sweep and Demonstrate may use many origins but only one destination. March may use many destinations but only one origin (2.3.5).
- **Eligibility:** a Faction that acted is Ineligible for the next card. A Faction that did not act is Eligible (2.3.6).
- **Look ahead:** one card of the draw deck is visible (2.2).
- **Campaign card:** when revealed as the upcoming card, swap it with the current card and run a Campaign Round at once (2.4).
- **Differences from series core:**
  - Raj Troops act free. Sepoys cost Resources equal to Restraint per space (3.2).
  - A Viceroy card, taken from each Campaign card, gives Raj a capability for one Campaign (2.5).
  - A Crisis state gives each Faction a special power (6.8).
  - Some Special Activities must be "accompanying" a given Operation (4.1.2).

## Operations and Special Activities

**Raj (cost: Restraint per space if Sepoys are used, free for Troops only) (3.2):**

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Deploy | Place up to 6 cubes in each selected City. Move 2 cubes from Out of Play to Available (3.2.1). |
| Garrison | Move cubes along Railways and Cities into Raj-Controlled spaces. Activate 1 Guerrilla per cube on each Railway (3.2.2). |
| Sweep | Move cubes into spaces. Activate 1 Guerrilla per cube (3.2.3). |
| Assault | Remove Active Guerrillas and Bases. Arrest Active Activists and Gandhi. 1 piece per cube, or per 2 Sepoys if Sepoys only (3.2.4). |
| Treaty (SA) | In States only: remove Active pieces, 1 per Sepoy, or move Sepoys in (4.2.1). |
| Govern (SA) | Up to 2 spaces with cubes: remove an Active piece, or (only with no Activists in the space) a Protest or Strike marker, or (only with no Active adversaries in the space) buy Imperialism for 1 or 2 levels (3 Resources per step) (4.2.2). |
| Martial Law (SA) | 1 space (2 in Crisis): remove 1 Activist per cube, remove Protest, add Unrest, shift 1 level to Neutral (4.2.3). |

- Raj cubes cannot exit Protest spaces in Garrison, Sweep and Treaty (1.10). Troops never enter States (1.4.2).
- Arresting Gandhi: Restraint -1, then Congress places up to 2 Protest markers (3.2.4).

**Congress and Muslim League (free; limited spaces) (3.3):**

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Rally | Place Activists (Congress up to Population; League 1 + Bases, or swap Activists equal to Unity for a Base). Then move 1 Activist from Out of Play, or release a die roll of Activists from Jail (3.3.1). |
| Demonstrate | Move Activists into non-Railway spaces. Place Protest if no Raj Control (3.3.2). |
| Civil Disobedience | Place Protest, Activate Activists, move Activists onto Railways (3.3.3). |
| Non-Cooperation | Congress: Protest spaces with Congress Activists and no Raj Control (in Crisis Congress may also select Raj-Controlled spaces). Muslim League: spaces with Protest, League Activists and a League Base, plus up to Unity spaces with Protest and League Activists, all with no Raj Control. Shift each selected space 1 level toward Active Opposition. Restraint -1 after all spaces (3.3.4, 6.8). |
| Negotiate (SA) | Restraint +1. Congress releases Gandhi or removes a Muslim State. League removes Unrest or places a Muslim State (4.3.1, 4.4.1). |
| Satyagraha (Congress SA) | Move Gandhi, then remove Protest and Unrest, or add an Activist (4.3.2). |
| Infiltrate (League SA) | Replace a Sepoy, Guerrilla or Protest with an Activist. In Crisis, place a Muslim State (4.4.2). |
| Persuade (SA) | Up to 2 spaces: remove 1 Active adversary, or Activate Guerrillas. May lower Unity (4.3.3, 4.4.3). |

- Space limits: Congress selects spaces up to Restraint, plus Protest spaces and the Gandhi space. League selects up to Unity, plus spaces with a League Base (3.3).

**Revolutionaries (1 Resource per space, Unrest costs Restraint) (3.4):**

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Rally | Place Guerrillas (1 + Bases) or swap Guerrillas equal to Unity for a Base (3.4.1). |
| March | Move Guerrillas. Underground ones move along Railways and Cities. Origin costs 1, Railway origin 0 (3.4.2). |
| Attack | Activate all Guerrillas, roll at or below their number to remove 2 pieces. May add Unrest (3.4.3). |
| Unrest | Spaces with an Underground Revolutionaries Guerrilla, or in Crisis only spaces with both Congress and League Activists. Pay Restraint per space. Activate 1 Underground Guerrilla if present. Non-Railway: add Unrest marker and shift 1 level toward Neutral. Railway: add a Strike marker if none. Then Restraint -1 (3.4.4). |
| Aid (SA) | Any spaces. Per space either Activate one Underground Guerrilla there and gain Resources equal to the space's Population, or pay 1 Resource to flip all Guerrillas there Underground (4.5.1). |
| Infiltrate (SA) | One space with an Underground Guerrilla: replace a Sepoy, Activist or Protest with a Guerrilla. Or, during Crisis only, place a Base in a State or Province with no Raj Control and room for a Base (4.5.2). |
| Assassinate (SA) | One space selected for the accompanying Revolutionaries Unrest Operation (3.4.4). Remove any 1 Active piece there, not Gandhi. Unity -1 if NV removed (4.5.3). |

## Event cards and deck

- 72 Event cards plus 7 Campaign cards (1.2). Every Event is dual use: the executing Faction picks the unshaded or the shaded text (5.2). Unshaded often favors Raj. Shaded often favors NV and Revolutionaries (5.2).
- Event text beats the rules. Events never place unavailable pieces and never break stacking (5.1.1).
- Events can place Protest regardless of Restraint (5.1.1). Arrest sends pieces to Jail. Removal sends them to Available (5.1.3).
- Deck build: separate piles of 6 or 12 Events, Campaign cards shuffled in as per scenario (Setup). Unused cards stay unseen (Setup).
- **No Capabilities or Momentum.** The Viceroy replaces them: seven Viceroys (Chelmsford, Reading, Irwin, Willingdon, Linlithgow, Wavell, Mountbatten) each give Raj one capability (2.5.1, 3.2, 4.2).
- Each card carries a Faction order. Underlined icons mark NP Critical Events (2.3.2, 8.8.1).
- **Historical content (Playbook p.33 and following):** each card has Background text. Examples: Chauri Chaura, Jallianwala Bagh, Salt March (card 55), Quit India (card 36), INA (card 69), Simon Commission, Round Table Conferences.
- Revolutionaries are an umbrella for many groups, for example HSRA, Jugantar, Hindu Mahasabha, INA (Playbook p.48).
- Not verified: the full list of all 72 Events.

## Map, spaces and tracks

- **Space types (1.3.1):** 5 Cities (Pop 1), 13 Provinces (Pop 1 or 2), 7 Princely States, Muslim States (created in play), Railways. Counts are from the image (see board description). Cities and Provinces can be Controlled. States and Railways never are (1.7.2).
- **Muslim spaces:** 10, marked with a crescent (1.3.4, 1.6.4). A Muslim State marker goes only in a Province, which then acts as a State (1.3.5).
- **Railways:** no Population, no Base, no Support. Each has an Economic Value. Raj earns Econ if no Strike (1.3.6).
- **Support and Opposition:** five levels per City, Province and State (1.6.1). Active counts double (1.6.2).
- **Resources:** Raj and Revolutionaries only, 0 to 50 (1.8).
- **Victory markers:** one per Faction on the edge track (1.9). The track runs 0 to 50 (from image).
- **Restraint (1 to 5):** sets Raj Sepoy cost, Revolutionaries Unrest cost, Congress space limit, and available Protest markers (1.3.11, 6.6).
- **Unity (1 to 5):** sets League space limit, Base cost in Rally, Congress-moved League Activists, and Constructive Programme size (6.7).
- **Crisis:** Restraint and Unity both 1 (6.8).
- **Boxes:** Jail, Out of Play, Available, Viceroy box (1.3.9, 1.3.10, 2.5).
- **Markers:** one Protest per space. Up to 12 Unrest or Strike markers in total (1.4.2, 1.11, 1.12).
- **Adjacency:** includes spaces joined across Railways and Towns (1.3.8).

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

This game calls it a Campaign Round (6.0). Phases in order:

1. **Victory (6.1):** check all four thresholds. If any is met, the game ends (7.2).
2. **Resources (6.2):** Congress places Strike on Railways with Protest, with more Activists than cubes, or next to an uncontrolled City (6.2.1). Raj gains the Econ of Strike-free Railways, then loses 2 per NV piece in Jail (6.2.2). Revolutionaries gain 2 per Base (6.2.3).
3. **Support (6.3):** Raj Imperialism in up to 4 spaces, then releases 2 Activists per space and releases Gandhi (6.3.1). Constructive Programme: Congress then League shift a combined total of up to Unity spaces (League uses what Congress leaves), each up to 2 levels toward Opposition (6.3.2). League and Revolutionaries each place one Base (6.3.3, 6.3.4). Game ends here after the final Round (6.3.5).
4. **Redeploy (6.4):** new Viceroy (6.4.1). Raj pays 2 per Troop left in a Province or on a Railway, removes cubes from Provinces with no Raj Control and removes all but one Troop from each City (6.4.2). NV and Guerrillas move (6.4.3 to 6.4.5).
5. **Reset (6.5):** remove all Protest, flip Activists Inactive. Remove one Unrest from spaces without Guerrillas. Flip Guerrillas Underground. Remove Strikes. Reset Restraint and Unity to the number of Campaign cards left. Make all Factions Eligible.

- Crisis triggers automatically in the Reset before the final Campaign (6.8).
- After the final Round, highest margin wins (7.3).

## Non-player (bot) system

Called Arjuna (8.0). It runs any Faction without a player, for 1 to 3 players (8.1).

- **Parts:** NP Eligibility table, Arjuna deck (6 cards per NP Faction), Space Selection Priorities tables, Move Priorities table, Adversary Priorities table, Random Spaces map (8.1.1).
- **Not verified:** the content of the Arjuna cards and tables. The file `gandhi--pac1-factions.md` holds Faction aid sheets, not Arjuna text.
- **Eligible NP, 1st Eligible:** choose the Event if Critical and effective. Else Op only if the next Faction has a Critical Event. Else Pass if guaranteed first next card with Critical Event. Else Op plus Special Activity (8.2.1).
- **Eligible NP, 2nd Eligible:** Pass, Event, Limited Op or Op plus Special Activity by a similar rule list (8.2.1).
- **Arjuna card use:** cycle cards face-up to the bottom until one matches the Faction. Read the flowchart: blue condition boxes, green or red arrows, a "flip card" icon, Operation then Special Activity (8.3).
- **Space choice:** pick the top row of the column that matches the action, then continue down the rows until one space remains (8.4.1). Ties use the Random Spaces map with two dice (8.1.2).
- **Raj and Revolutionaries NP:** no Resources. An Activation Number sets how many spaces they operate in. A die roll at or below it stops Sepoy use (Raj) or ends the Operation (Revolutionaries) (8.6.2).
- **Events:** NP Raj uses unshaded text. Others use shaded unless the icon has a square halo (8.8.2). Only effective Events are chosen (8.8.3).
- **Special rules:** NP Raj never Garrisons and never frees Gandhi on request (8.1.4, 8.6.3). NP Congress adds one Protest each Reset (8.9.4).
- **Randomness:** dice for space ties, Activation Number, and Imperialism limits (8.1.2, 8.6.2, 8.7.1).
- **Solitaire:** the player cannot win before the final Round and must keep all NP Factions below threshold. Success is graded by margin over the top NP (8.10).
- **7.1:** if an NP Faction passes a victory check, all players lose.

## Unique mechanics

- **Nonviolent Factions:** new Faction type with Activists, no Resources, and space limits from tracks (Playbook p.46).
- **Restraint and Unity tracks:** shared tracks that tune costs, limits and Protest supply (6.6, 6.7). Low Restraint gives more Protest ("Paradox of Repression", Playbook p.48).
- **Crisis:** a triggered state that unlocks Faction powers and lets Raj bring in cubes (6.8).
- **Leader piece:** Gandhi, first modern-era COIN leader piece (Playbook p.47). He cannot be eliminated, only jailed.
- **Jail box:** arrested pieces return by Rally, Negotiate, Satyagraha or Support Phase (1.3.9, 6.3.1).
- **Active Activists tied to Protest and Railways** (1.4.3). Protest locks Raj cubes in place (1.10).
- **Control by Active pieces only** (1.7.1).
- **Railways as spaces** with Econ and Strikes (1.3.6, 1.12).
- **Viceroy cards** replace Capabilities (2.5).
- **Muslim and Princely States:** Troops cannot enter. Raj uses Sepoys and Treaty (1.3.5, 4.2.1).
- **Four-way scoring** with different metrics for each Faction (7.2).

## Relevance

- Inferred: the NV Faction model (Protest marker before action, collective Active state, space limits instead of Resources) could model an unarmed Kurdish political or civil front next to a PKK armed wing in a Turkey game.
- Inferred: Restraint and Unity style shared tracks could model state repression tolerance and Kurdish factional cohesion, with a Crisis state when both fall.
- Inferred: the Jail box, with Resource drain per prisoner and release in the Support Phase, suits mass arrests after the 1980 coup.
- Inferred: for a digital Falling Sky, the Arjuna method (cycled card flowchart, priority tables, activation number, random-space table) is a compact bot design with little code state.
