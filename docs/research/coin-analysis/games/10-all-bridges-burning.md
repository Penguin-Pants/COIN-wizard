# All Bridges Burning: analysis

## Identity

- Title: All Bridges Burning (Finnish Civil War 1917-18). Rules of Play and Playbook (1.0).
- COIN volume: the tenth COIN Series volume (Playbook, Series Developer's Notes: "10 volumes").
- Credits as printed (Playbook p.40): Game Designer V.P.J. Arponen. Series Creator Volko Ruhnke. Series Developer Jason Carr. Developer Örjan Ariander. Map and Event Cards Chechu Nieto. Art Director and cover Rodger B. MacGowan. Counters and Solitaire System Cards Mark Simonitch. Manuals and Player Aid Cards Charles Kibler.
- Edition: "Living Rules", November 2023. The German Actions flowchart is a "Living Document", October 2020 (flowcharts file).
- Setting: Finland, run-up to the Red Revolt and the war of 1918. Central and southern Finland (1.2).
- Scale: Towns and Provinces only. Population 0 to 2 per space (1.2.1). Map names seen in rules: Helsinki, Uusimaa, Tampere, Häme, Turku, Viipuri, Vaasa, Pohjanmaa, Mikkelin lääni (Setup, 6.3).
- Players: 1 to 3. Three Factions. Any Faction without a player is a Non-player (1.3, 8.0).
- Scenarios: one. Setup is fixed. The game has two Phases (2.4) and four Propaganda Rounds (2.3.1, Setup).
- Components: 47 Event cards, 17 Solitaire cards, 88 wooden pieces, 3 dice (1.1).
- Deck is not Faction-keyed. Event cards carry no Faction symbols on the top row (2.2.1).

## Factions

Three Factions plus two Powers. Each Faction is enemy to all others (1.3.1).

| Faction | Type | Victory (7.2, 7.3) | Resources | Pieces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reds | Insurgent (revolt) | Opposition + Reds Administrations on map exceeds 11 AND Russian Vassalage + Polarization is 5 or less | 0 to 30 (1.5). Start 5 | 20 Cells, 3 Administrations, 3 political cubes (1.4, Setup) |
| Senate (white) | Government and elite | Senate-Controlled Town Population exceeds 3 AND German Vassalage + Polarization is less than 6 | 0 to 30. Start 5 | 20 Cells (10 start Out of Play, 6.5.5), 3 political cubes |
| Moderates (blue) | Non-violent third party | Moderates Resources exceed 14 AND Resolved Issues + Networks + 1 is equal to or greater than Polarization | 0 to 30. Start 5 | 6 Cells, 2 Networks, Personality marker, 2 News markers |

- Powers: Germany (friend of Senate, 6 Troops) and Russia (friend of Reds, 6 Troops). They never spend Resources. They act only in Phase II. Russia never acts alone (1.3.1, 3.1).
- Victory margins (7.3):
  - Reds: Opposition + Administrations - 11. If Russian Vassalage + Polarization exceeds 5, margin is zero.
  - Senate: Senate Town Population - 3. If German Vassalage + Polarization exceeds 5, margin is zero.
  - Moderates: the lower of (Resources - 14) or (Resolved Issues + Networks + 1 - Polarization).
- Powers win on the last Propaganda Round (6.4.3) only if no Faction has a positive margin. The higher Vassalage Power wins. A tie means nobody wins (7.3).
- Ties in rank go to Non-players, then Moderates, then Senate (7.1).
- Active and Inactive status is reversed from most COIN volumes. Cells enter Inactive (Reds and Senate) or Underground (Moderates). New Cells are placed Inactive or Underground (1.4.1).
- Control: Reds or Senate Control a space if their Cells plus Administrations exceed both other Factions combined. Moderates pieces count against. Powers' Troops do not count. Moderates and Powers never Control (1.7).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- Card flow: reveal current card and the next card (2.1). Cards are Events or Propaganda cards (2.3).
- Eligibility order is set only by actions taken. Cards show no Faction order (2.2.1). Start order is random.
- Three Factions can all act on each card. The third Eligible Faction also acts (2.2.3).
- Options for the first Eligible Faction: Limited Command, Command with optional Special Activity, or Event (2.2.3). Pass is also possible (2.2.2).
- Later Factions choose Pass or the remaining boxes in the row picked by the first Faction (2.2.3).
- Pass: move cylinder to the Pass box. Gain 1 Resource. Stay Eligible (2.2.2).
- Limited Command: one space, no Special Activity (2.2.4).
- Adjust Eligibility: only the Faction that took Command + Special Activity becomes Ineligible. Others return to Eligible in the order Pass, Ineligible, Command/Event boxes (2.2.5).
- News marker: a Senate or Reds player that does only a Limited Command may discard a held News marker to add one more non-free Limited Command (3.3.2, 2.2.4).
- German Action Phase: on cards with the symbol, in Phase II only, the Senate player rolls 1d6. On 1 to 3 the Germans act before the Factions. Otherwise after (3.4).
- Changes from series core: no 1st/2nd Faction symbols on cards. The deck does not set order. A third acting Faction exists. Propaganda swaps with the current card (2.3). Eligibility is not reset after a Propaganda Round (6.5).
- Phases: Phase I until Red Revolt! (#24, Pivotal Event). Phase II after (2.4).

## Operations and Special Activities

Reds and Senate Commands (3.2). Attack and March are Phase II only.

| Command | Effect | Cost and key limits |
|---|---|---|
| Rally (3.2.1) | Place Cells: start at 1, +1 per Support (Senate) or Opposition (Reds), -1 per enemy Terror and per opposing level. Reds may swap 2 Cells for an Administration in up to one selected space where no Cells were placed in that Rally | 1 per space. Spaces up to Polarization (at least 2). Offset costs 1 per marker or level |
| Activism (3.2.2) | Remove own Active Cell (lowers Polarization) or flip enemy Cells. Then Activate 1d6 own Cells evenly, or Reds Agitate | 1 per space. Agitate needs Reds Administration and Control |
| Terror (3.2.3) | Remove 1 enemy (2 if Polarization 6 or more) or one cube in Political Display. Place Terror. Polarization +1 per marker | 1 per space or Display. Needs own Active Cell. Terror may not remove the last enemy Cell if that would remove a Capability or Prepared marker (3.2.3). Max 2 Terror per space. Second Terror in Phase II adds News |
| Attack (3.2.4) | Roll 1d6 vs Strength. Casualties by Engagement or Retreat. Attrition and Prisoners follow | 1 per space plus optional extra Resources (+1 Strength each). Roll of 6 always fails. Trains allow Attack into an adjacent space |
| March (3.2.5) | Move Cells with Capabilities. Reds may move 1 Russian Troop per Cell | 1 per 3 Cells (round up) entering a space. Trains move only on Railways across Unsabotaged borders |

Attack Strength table (3.2.4):
- Start at 0. +1 for 3 or more Attacking Cells.
- Attacker +2 per Jaeger or German Troop. Defender -2 each.
- Russian Troop: +2 or -2 if Russian Vassalage is 2 or more. Otherwise +1 or -1.
- Defending Prepared marker: -2. Commander: +1 or -1.
- Cannons and Trains: +2 or -2 with a friendly Jaeger. Otherwise +1 or -1.
- Max 2 Capabilities per Faction count. Germans have a minimum Strength of 2.

Reds and Senate Special Activities (4.2). Each may accompany any Command unless noted.
- Political Event: +1 Resource per space, up to 4 spaces with Active Cell and Control (4.2.1).
- Foreign Relations: raise friendly Vassalage by 1. Or lower it by 1 on a Success Roll. Each Resource adds +2 to the Target Value. Roll 1d6 at or below Target (4.2.2). Three Resources guarantee success.
- Prepare: place a Prepared marker in one space with a friendly Cell. In Phase II, place or remove Sabotage on a border instead (4.2.3).
- Coordinate (Senate only, Phase II): lets the Senate decide the next German action (4.2.4).
- Crackdown (Senate only, Phase II, not with Attack or March): in one Controlled space with an Active Cell, place white Terror and shift Opposition one level toward Neutral. Polarization +1 per Terror (4.2.5).

Moderates Commands (3.3). No new Commands in Phase II (3.3).
- Rally: replace 2 Cells with a Network. Or flip Cells Underground (up to Population plus Networks). Or place Cells (start 1, -1 per level of Support or Opposition). Cost 1 per space. The total drops by 1 per Polarization level above 5 (3.3.1).
- Message: move a Cell up to three steps through selected adjacent spaces. Cost 1 per moving Cell. A Cell with News or the Personality faces a Capture roll (3.3.2).
- Negotiate: in spaces with an Underground Moderates Cell and Active enemy Cells, Activate own Cell and Deactivate one enemy. Then lower Polarization by the number Deactivated. Cost 1 per space (3.3.3).
- Politics: pay up to 3 (set by Polarization track). Place one cube in the Political Display. Polarization must be 5 or less (3.3.4).

Moderates Special Activities (4.3):
- Personality: place the Personality in a Town. Or in Phase II, in the selected space that holds the Personality and an Underground Moderates Cell with News, spend the News to place a cube and then gain 1d6 Resources or lower Polarization by half of 1d6 (round up). Placement needs a Town with a Moderates Cell (4.3.1).
- Dialogue: Activate an Underground Cell. Shift Support or Opposition one level toward Neutral, or replace an Inactive enemy Cell. Not with Rally (4.3.2).
- Publish: up to 2 spaces. +1 per Town. In each selected space, Activate at most 1 Underground Cell if the Personality is present plus at most 1 Underground Cell if any Moderates Network is present. +2 per Cell Activated (4.3.3). Not with Rally or Negotiate.

## Event cards and deck

- 47 Event cards: 21 marked 1917, 21 marked 1918, 4 Propaganda, plus Red Revolt! #24 (1.1, Setup). Setup numbers add to 46 plus #24.
- Deck build (Setup): per Campaign Deck, shuffle 4 Event cards with 1 Propaganda card. Put 5 more Event cards on top, unshuffled. Draw deck is two 1917 Campaign Decks over two 1918 Campaign Decks. Three cards per year stay unseen.
- Card parts: title, year, image, unshaded text, shaded text, solitaire symbol (5.0). Dual Use cards allow one choice. Unshaded helps the Senate. Shaded often helps Moderates or Reds (5.2).
- Capabilities: markers for Trains, Cannons, Commander, Jaeger and others. Moderates cannot hold them. In Phase II a Capability needs a friendly Cell in its space (5.3, 1.4.3).
- Free Commands from Events cost nothing and do not change Eligibility (5.4, 3.1.2). Agitation still costs (3.1.2).
- Event text beats rules but not Stacking and not the 30 Resource cap (5.1).
- Pivotal Event #24 Red Revolt! sits outside the deck (2.4). See next sections for triggers.
- Some cards carry a German Action Phase marker (3.4). No Momentum or Coup cards exist.
- Historical notes: the Playbook gives a background entry per card (for example #10 Jaeger, #15 Trains, #18 Mobilization in the Periphery, #27 Reds Major Offensive) (Playbook Events Background).

## Map, spaces and tracks

- Space types: Town and Province (1.2.1). Sea areas are not in play (1.2.3).
- Railways run through Provinces and are not borders. Trains move along them (1.2.2). Each border between touching spaces is separate (1.2.4).
- German Landing Sites on the map have die roll ranges (1.2, 3.4).
- Support and Opposition: five levels around Neutral, always measured toward the Senate. Active counts double Population for Total Opposition (1.6). Zero-Population Provinces may hold markers and Control (1.6, 1.7).
- Stacking: max 2 Administrations plus Networks per space. Max 2 Terror. Max 1 Prepared per Faction (1.4.3).
- Tracks (1.2, 1.12): Resources 0 to 30. Victory markers on the edge track. Polarization track. Vassalage for each Power. Cells on Map. Sequence of Play track.
- Polarization: starts at 3. It sets Rally limits, Politics cost, Terror strength and Moderates earnings (1.10). Exact range not verified in the rules text (track is an image).
- Political Display: chamber with two Faction cube boxes plus three Issues. Resolve Issues in the Politics Phase (1.11, 6.2).
- Prisoners of War box holds captured Cells (3.2.4, 6.5.1).
- Setup values (Setup): Polarization 3. Vassalage German 3 and Russian 3. Oppose + Admins 3. Cells on Map 13. Senate Town Pop 1.

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

Propaganda Round phases (6.0):
1. Victory Phase. Any positive margin ends the game, unless a friendly Power blocks it (6.1, 7.2).
2. Politics Phase. Roll 1d6 against cubes in the Display. Roll at or below cube count resolves an Issue. Colored marker goes to the larger cube side. Ties go to the Moderates (6.2). Then Personal Leadership: no Personality on map costs Moderates 3 Resources and a forced placement (6.2.2).
3. Resources Phase. Senate +1 per Support level. Reds + Controlled Population. Moderates +1 per two Polarization levels up to 5, then +1 per level above 5. Cap 30 (6.3).
4. Support Phase. Russian Turmoil: Russian Vassalage -1 (6.4.1). Agitation in order Senate, Reds, Moderates (6.4.2). Game End check at the final round (6.4.3).
5. Reset Phase. Prisoners of War release and Polarization +1 per 2 Cells held (Phase II only, 6.5.1). Pivotal Event check (6.5.2). Troops match Vassalage (6.5.3). Old News: remove all Terror, Sabotage and News (6.5.4). Senate Conscription after round one: add 10 Senate Cells (6.5.5).

- Resolved white markers raise Reds Agitation cost. Red markers raise Senate cost. Only the first Agitation space is hit (6.2.1).
- Final round: if no one wins at the fourth Propaganda card's Victory Phase, the round continues through the Support Phase (6.4) and then ends without the Reset Phase. This reading runs the Politics, Resources and Support phases (6.2 to 6.4) in order (inferred from 2.3.1 "continue through the Support Phase only (6.4)"). Rank by margin (2.3.1, 7.3).
- No Coup or Winter phase exists.

## Non-player (bot) system

- Type: three mini-decks of cards, one per Faction (17 cards, #48 to #64). Reds 6, Senate 6, Moderates 5 (8.2, 8.3 to 8.5).
- No Resource tracking for Reds and Senate. Moderates Resources are tracked for Victory only. Non-players treat Offset and Terror removal costs as paid (8.1.2, 8.1.3).
- Attack Strength starts at 7 for the Senate and 5 for the Reds (8.1.3).
- Decision order (8.1.4): NP-to-play marker; Pass to play the next card if it carries the Faction's symbol; Capability handling (die roll against 2 per held Capability, Reds +2); Event if the card shows the Faction's symbol; draw an NP card for Cmd+SA or Limited Command; Event only; Pass.
- Command + Special Activity only if the Non-player is last Eligible. Otherwise Limited Command (8.1.4).
- NP card layout: green Condition Box, Command priorities, Special Activity priorities (8.2). Numbered priorities are sequential. Lettered priorities are nested tie-breakers (8.2.5).
- If the Command cannot run, discard and redraw. If no Condition is met, Pass (8.2.1, 8.2.3). Then reshuffle (8.1.4).
- Ineffective Events are skipped (8.1.5). Random choices use the Random Spaces map (8.6).
- General priorities (8.1.7): Senate raises Support then cuts Opposition. Reds raise Opposition. Moderates seek Neutral and Uncontrolled Population. Prefer Towns. Always Engage in Attack.
- German bot (flowchart): if a Coordinate marker is in play, the Senate decides. Else Landing if Troops are Available and none on map (News marker added). Else Attack if in a space with Reds or Russians. Else March toward Reds, then random (flowcharts file).
- One-player victory: avoid any Non-player win in each Propaganda Round and have the highest margin at the end (8.7).

## Unique mechanics

- Three Factions, all can act on each card (2.2.3).
- Eligibility driven by player action only, no Faction symbols on cards (2.2.1).
- Phase I and Phase II split with Pivotal Event. Triggers: 27 or more Reds plus Senate Cells at end of a turn, or after the second Propaganda Round (2.4, 6.5.2).
- No March, Attack or Powers in Phase I. Local militias only (3.2 design note).
- Polarization as a core resource and victory brake. Both Powers' Vassalage plus Polarization cap Reds and Senate wins (7.2).
- Foreign Power Troops with Vassalage tracks. German bot flowchart. Russia only helps Reds (1.3.1, 1.9).
- Active and Inactive meaning is reversed (1.4.1).
- Moderates Faction: non-violent, wins via Resources, Networks and Resolved Issues (7.2).
- News and Personality markers with Capture rolls (3.3.2).
- Political Display with Issues, and cost effects on Agitation (1.11, 6.2.1).
- Railways pass through Provinces. Sabotage on borders blocks Trains (1.2.2, 4.2.3).
- Prisoners of War box raises Polarization (6.5.1).
- Non-players use mini-decks with Condition Boxes instead of flowcharts. No Resource spending (8.1.2).
- Victory margin formula includes zeroing by Powers (7.3).

## Relevance

- Inferred: Polarization with a threshold effect on Terror strength and on win conditions fits a PKK game. It could model state repression versus social cohesion across 1984 to the late 1980s.
- Inferred: Phase I (local, no movement) to Phase II (armed march and attack) via a Pivotal Event could map to the 1984 start of the PKK campaign. Foreign Power Vassalage could model Syria, Iraq or Iran support or Turkish allies.
- Inferred: the Non-player mini-deck with Condition Boxes and no Resource tracking is simple to code. A digital Falling Sky could reuse it for a lean bot. Random choice from a priority list fits software well.
- Inferred: eligibility driven only by past action, with no card symbols, lets a digital game build turn order from a small state machine. The 3-Faction third-slot option needs care.
