# Cuba Libre (COIN Series Volume II)

## Identity

- Title: Cuba Libre, subtitle "Castro's Insurgency" (Rules p.1).
- COIN volume: Volume II (Rules p.1).
- Designers as printed: Jeff Grossman and Volko Ruhnke (Rules p.1). Playbook credits: Game Design Jeff Grossman and Volko Ruhnke; Development Örjan Ariander and Steve Caler; Solitaire Systems Örjan Ariander and Volko Ruhnke (Playbook credits).
- Calixto bot booklet (2025): Original COIN design Volko Ruhnke; Calixto system design Kevin Crooks; development Jason Carr and Joe Dewhurst; COIN Series development Jason Carr (Calixto Credits).
- Rules file: "3rd Printing, Living Rules March, 2019", copyright 2018 GMT Games (Rules p.1). Calixto booklet is copyright 2025.
- Setting: insurgent and counterinsurgent conflict in Cuba, 1957 to 1958 (1.0).
- Scale: 1 to 4 players (1.0). Map shows the island with 7 Provinces, 3 Cities and 3 Economic Centers (Playbook Spaces List). One Population point is about 1 million people (Key Terms Index).
- Components: 17x22 inch board, 52 cards, 86 wooden pieces, 8 cylinders, dice, Faction aid foldouts (1.2).
- Scenarios: no named scenarios. Two deployments: Standard and Variable (Set Up, p.20).
- Options: Short Game, Sudden Propaganda, Victory Deception, No Non-Player (2.1, 2.3.8, 7.3, 1.5).

## Factions

Four Factions. Each is enemy to all others (1.5). Resources run 0 to 49 for each (1.8).

| Faction | Type | Victory check (7.2) | Victory margin at game end (7.3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Government (Govt, blue) | Government | All Cities at Active Support AND Total Support > 18 | Total Support - 18 |
| 26July (M26, red) | Insurgent | Total Opposition + 26July Bases > 15 | Total Opposition + 26July Bases - 15 |
| Directorio (DR, yellow) | Insurgent | Population in DR-Controlled spaces + DR Bases > 9 | DR-Controlled Pop + DR Bases - 9 |
| Syndicate (green) | Insurgent (organized crime) | More than 7 open Casinos AND Resources > 30 | Lower of (open Casinos - 7) or (Syndicate Resources - 30) |

Formulas (1.6.3):
- Total Support = (2 x Pop in Active Support) + (1 x Pop in Passive Support).
- Total Opposition = (2 x Pop in Active Opposition) + (1 x Pop in Passive Opposition).
- Ties in margin go to Non-players, then Syndicate, then Directorio, then 26July (7.1).

Pieces at start of game, total before set up (Available Forces, p.20):

| Faction | Troops | Police | Guerrillas | Bases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Government | 15 | 15 | none | 2 |
| 26July | none | none | 15 | 4 |
| Directorio | none | none | 15 | 4 |
| Syndicate | none | none | 6 | 10 (Casinos) |

Standard start Resources: Govt 15, Syndicate 15, 26July 10, Directorio 5. Aid 15. US Alliance Firm (Set Up, p.20).

Faction notes:
- Government: costs per space depend on the US Alliance track (6.3.1). It has no lasting Capabilities. It has temporary Momentum Events (5.4; Playbook p.14).
- 26July: Rally can place many Guerrillas at Bases (3.3.1). It builds Opposition with Terror and Agitation (3.3.4, 6.3.3).
- Directorio: Rallies only in Neutral or Passive spaces (3.3.1). Its victory depends on Control, not Support (1.6.3).
- Syndicate: its Bases are Casinos. Other Factions cannot remove them, only close them (1.4.5). It can be paid by, and pays, others via Skim and Cash (6.2.3, 4.5.2).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- Deck: cards play one at a time. One card ahead is revealed (2.2, 2.3.8). No hands (1.1).
- Faction order is the symbol order on the card, left to right (2.3.2).
- Eligible Factions that did not execute an Operation or Event on the last card stay Eligible. Others are Ineligible (2.3.1, 2.3.7). All start Eligible.
- 1st Eligible options: Pass, Event, or Operation with or without a Special Activity (2.3.3, 2.3.4).
- 2nd Eligible options depend on the 1st (2.3.4):
  - 1st did Operation only: 2nd may do a Limited Operation (LimOp).
  - 1st did Operation and Special Activity: 2nd may do a Limited Operation or, instead, the Event (2.3.4).
  - 1st did the Event: 2nd may do an Operation, with a Special Activity if desired.
- Pass gives +1 Resource, or +3 for Government. The Passing Faction stays Eligible (2.3.3).
- Limited Operation: one space only, no Special Activity. For Garrison, Sweep or March, pieces may come from many spaces but go to one destination (2.3.5).
- Launder (2.3.6): a 1st or 2nd Eligible Faction that paid for an Operation and used no Special Activity may remove one Cash marker it owns. It then gets a free LimOp of any type except Construct. One Cash marker per card per Faction. Another Faction may remove its own Cash marker to enable this.
- Final Event card before the last Propaganda card: player Operations must be Limited (2.3.9).
- Sudden Propaganda option: when Propaganda is revealed next, swap the two showing cards and run the Propaganda Round now (2.3.8).
- Change from series core (inferred from Playbook p.14): US Alliance sets Govt Operation costs. Capabilities belong to Insurgents. Government gets Momentum. Cash gives a free LimOp.

## Operations and Special Activities

Operations and Special Activities are in 3.0 and 4.0. A Special Activity may occur before, during or after its Operation (4.1).

### Government (Train, Garrison, Sweep, Assault; Transport, Air Strike, Reprisal)

COIN Operations cost 2 (Firm), 3 (Reluctant) or 4 (Embargoed) Resources per space. Garrison costs that amount in total (6.3.1).

| Name | Effect | Key limit |
|---|---|---|
| Train (3.2.1) | Place up to 4 cubes in each selected Province with a Govt Base and in each selected City. In 1 space, replace 2 cubes with a Base OR do Civic Action. | Civic Action needs Troops, Police and Control, and extra Resources (6.3.2). |
| Garrison (3.2.2) | Move cubes to ECs or Cities. Then Activate 1 Guerrilla per cube in each EC. Then optional free Assault in 1 EC. | Limited: one destination. |
| Sweep (3.2.3) | Move adjacent Troops in. Activate 1 Guerrilla per cube. | Forest: 1 per 2 cubes. |
| Assault (3.2.4) | Remove 1 Active Guerrilla per Troop. Then Bases (Bases last). City or EC: also 1 piece per Police. | Mountain: 1 piece per 2 Troops. Seizure takes Cash. |
| Transport (4.2.1) | Move up to 3 Troops from a City or Base to 1 space. | None else. |
| Air Strike (4.2.2) | Remove 1 Active Guerrilla, or a Base if no Guerrillas. | With Garrison, Sweep or Assault. Province or EC only. Not when Embargoed. |
| Reprisal (4.2.3) | Terror marker, shift Opposition 1 toward Neutral, relocate 1 Guerrilla. | With Garrison, Sweep or Assault. 1 Govt-Controlled space. |

### 26July (Rally, March, Attack, Terror; Infiltrate, Ambush, Kidnap)

| Name | Effect | Key limit |
|---|---|---|
| Rally (3.3.1) | Cost 1 per space. Place 1 Guerrilla, or replace 2 with a Base. With a Base: flip Underground, or place up to 2 x Bases + 2 x Pop Guerrillas. | Only Neutral or Opposition spaces. |
| March (3.3.2) | Cost 1 per City or Province destination, 0 for ECs. Guerrillas Activate if entering Support or an EC and Guerrillas plus cubes there exceed 3. | Each Guerrilla moves once. |
| Attack (3.3.3) | Cost 1 per space. Activate all, roll a die. If roll is at most the Guerrilla count, remove up to 2 enemy pieces. Roll of 1 adds a Guerrilla. | Needs 1 Guerrilla and 1 enemy piece. Cubes protect Casinos. |
| Terror (3.3.4) | Cost 1 per City or Province, 0 for ECs. Activate 1 Underground Guerrilla. Terror marker and shift toward Neutral (26July: or toward Active Opposition). EC: Sabotage marker. | 20 Terror/Sabotage markers. |
| Infiltrate (4.3.1) | Replace 1 cube (Police first) with a Guerrilla. | With Rally or March. One space without Support (including an EC) that has or is adjacent to an Underground 26July Guerrilla. |
| Ambush (4.3.2) | Attack in 1 space succeeds with no roll. Activates only 1 Guerrilla. Adds a Guerrilla. | Needs Underground Guerrilla. |
| Kidnap (4.3.3) | Take a die roll of Resources from Govt or Syndicate, or Cash. Closes 1 open Casino. | In a space selected (or to be selected) for Terror this Operation, that is a City, EC or has an open Casino, with more 26July Guerrillas than Police. |

### Directorio (Rally, March, Attack, Terror; Subvert, Ambush, Assassinate)

- Rally (3.3.1): same as 26July but only Neutral or Passive spaces. Bonus placement is up to 1 x Bases + 1 x Pop.
- March, Attack, Terror (3.3.2 to 3.3.4): same as 26July. Terror shifts only toward Neutral.
- Subvert (4.4.1): with Rally or March, in 1 DR-Controlled Province. Gain its Pop in Resources. Set it to Neutral.
- Ambush (4.4.2): as 26July.
- Assassinate (4.4.3): in one space selected for Directorio Terror this Operation, where DR Guerrillas outnumber Police. Remove or close any 1 enemy piece, even a protected Base. Commandeer takes Cash.

### Syndicate (Rally, March, Terror, Construct; Profit, Muscle, Bribe)

- Rally (3.3.1): cost 1. Places a single Guerrilla only. If an open Casino is there, may flip Guerrillas Underground.
- March and Terror (3.3.2, 3.3.4): as other Insurgents.
- Construct (3.3.5): cost 5 per space, never free. In a City or Province that Govt or Syndicate Controls. Place a closed Casino or open a closed one.
- Profit (4.5.1): with Rally, March or Construct. Place Cash in up to 2 open-Casino spaces. Or close Casinos and remove Cash for +3 Resources each.
- Muscle (4.5.3): with Rally, March or Construct. Move 1 to 2 Police to a City, or Troops to a Province or EC, with an open Casino or EC.
- Bribe (4.5.4): cost 3 Resources. In 1 space, remove up to 2 cubes, or remove or flip up to 2 Guerrillas, or remove an enemy Base. It is the only Special Activity with a cost (4.5.4 note).

## Event cards and deck

- Deck: 52 cards. 48 Event cards and 4 Propaganda cards (1.2, Set Up p.20).
- Build: split the 48 Event cards into 4 roughly equal piles. Shuffle 1 Propaganda card into each. Stack the piles (Set Up p.20). Short Game: set aside 8 random Events first (2.1).
- Each card shows 4 Faction symbols in order (Playbook Card List lists orders such as GMDS).
- Dual Use (5.2): many cards have unshaded and shaded text. The executor may choose either, regardless of Faction. Shaded text usually favors the Government (5.2 note).
- Insurgent Capabilities (5.3): lasting effects. A marker goes in the Capabilities box. They last for the rest of the game. Examples named in the rules: Santo, Pact of Caracas, Mafia Offensive, Morgan, El Che, Raúl, The Guerrilla Life (6.2.3, 8.5 to 8.7).
- Government Momentum (5.4): shaded text. The card stays near the Government corner until the next Reset phase (6.5). Any number may be in play. Examples named: Armored Cars, Sánchez Mosquera, Rolando Masferrer, S.I.M., Guantánamo Bay, MAP (8.8.1; Calixto p.20).
- Precedence (5.1): Event text beats rules. Events never place unavailable pieces, never break stacking unless stated ("Sinatra"), never exceed 49, and never give a Faction an Operation it does not own, unless stated ("Fat Butcher") (5.1.1).
- Free Operations from Events cost nothing and do not affect Eligibility (3.1.2, 5.5).
- Historical content: card titles and flavor text name real people and events (for example Echeverría, Morgan, Fauré Chomón, Meyer Lansky, Batista Flees). Flavor text has no game effect (5.0; Playbook Card List).
- No Pivotal cards. There is no Coup card type. A card named "Coup" is a normal Event (Playbook Card List, number 9).

## Map, spaces and tracks

- Space types (1.3.1): Provinces, Cities, Economic Centers (ECs). All can hold forces.
- Provinces: Pop 1 or 2; terrain Forest, Mountain or Grassland (1.3.2).
- Cities: Pop 1 or 6 (1.3.3). EC: Econ 2 or 3 (1.3.4). ECs have no Pop and never hold Support or Opposition (1.6.2 note).
- Playbook Spaces List: Cities Havana 6, Camagüey 1, Santiago de Cuba 1 (total 8). Provinces: Pinar del Río Forest 1, La Habana Grass 1, Matanzas Grass 1, Las Villas Mountain 2, Camagüey Forest 1, Oriente Forest 2, Sierra Maestra Mountain 1 (total 9). ECs: Cigars 3, Factory 3, Sugar Cane 2 (total 8).
- Support and Opposition: 5 levels, Active Support, Passive Support, Neutral, Passive Opposition, Active Opposition (1.6.1). Active counts double.
- Control: a Faction has more pieces in a space than all others combined. Only open Casinos count (1.7).
- Stacking: at most 2 non-Casino Bases plus 2 Casinos per Province or City. No Bases in ECs (1.4.2).
- Guerrillas are Underground or Active. Casinos are open or closed (1.4.3, 1.4.4).
- Cash: 4 markers, stacked with a Guerrilla or cube, owned by that piece's Faction (4.5.2).
- Terror and Sabotage markers: 20 in total (3.3.4).
- Edge track 0 to 49: Resources, Aid, and the victory markers Total Support, Opposition + Bases, DR Pop + Bases, Open Casinos (1.6.3, 1.8).
- US Alliance track: Firm, Reluctant, Embargoed (6.3.1).
- Adjacency: not verified (no map image in this task).

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

Propaganda Round, in order (6.0). Never run 2 rounds in a row (6.0 exception).

1. Victory Phase (6.1): check 7.2 for any player's Factions. If all of a player's Factions pass, the game ends.
2. Resources Phase (6.2):
   - Govt earnings: first Sabotage ECs where 26July + DR Guerrillas outnumber cubes. Then add Econ of unSabotaged ECs plus Aid (6.2.1).
   - 26July gains Resources equal to the number of its Bases. DR gains the number of spaces where it has pieces. Syndicate gains the Pop of each City and the Econ of each unSabotaged EC where its Guerrillas outnumber Police, plus 2 per open Casino (6.2.2).
   - Skim: for each space with open Casinos, Syndicate pays 2 Resources to the Controlling Faction (6.2.3).
   - Cash Deposits: each marker gives its owner a Base (or open Casino) at its location, or +6 Resources (6.2.4).
3. Support Phase (6.3):
   - US Alliance: if Total Support is 18 or less, lower the Alliance one box and cut Aid by 10 (6.3.1).
   - Civic Action: Govt pays 4 Resources per Terror removal or shift toward Active Support, in Govt-Controlled spaces with Troops and Police (6.3.2).
   - Agitation: 26July pays 1 Resource per Terror removal or shift toward Active Opposition in 26July-Controlled spaces (6.3.3).
   - Expat Backing: Directorio gets a free Rally in 1 space with no Active Support, no Active Opposition and no Control by another Faction (6.3.4).
   - Game End check (6.3.5): final round stops here.
4. Redeploy Phase (6.4): Govt may move Police to ECs or Govt-Controlled spaces. Troops on ECs or in Provinces without Govt Bases must move to Govt-Controlled Cities or Base spaces (Havana if none). Other Troops may move there.
5. Reset Phase (6.5): all Factions Eligible. Remove Terror and Sabotage. Discard Momentum cards. Flip Guerrillas Underground and Casinos open. Play the next card.

Game end: the 4th Propaganda card ends the game after its Support Phase if no victory (2.4.1). Highest margin wins (7.3). Final round omits Redeploy and Reset (6.3.5).

## Non-player (bot) system

Two systems exist.

### Original flowchart bots (Rules section 8.0)

- Covers all 4 Factions. Use for 1 to 3 players, or 2 or 3 players with the No Non-Player option off (1.5, 8.0).
- Event or Operation (8.1): Non-players pick the Event when given the choice, unless it has no effect. They pick Operations when 2nd Eligible in some pairings (Syndicate and Govt when the other is a player; DR and 26July when the other is a player). Govt picks Operations under any Momentum.
- Non-players never use a LimOp. They do a full Operation, usually with a Special Activity (8.1). They cannot use Launder.
- Low Resources: Pass if they cannot pay for any non-EC space (8.1).
- Dual Use: DR and 26July use unshaded. Syndicate and Govt use shaded (8.4.2). A gray halo on a card symbol means check a special Event Instruction (8.4.1).
- Priorities are flowcharts. Each Faction has an ordered list (8.5 to 8.8). Examples:
  - Govt: Train, else Garrison, else Sweep, else Assault, else Pass. Operations cap at 9 Resources. Spaces limited to 4/3/2 by US Alliance level (8.8).
  - 26July: Terror (with Kidnap), else Rally (with Infiltrate), else March, else Attack (with Ambush) (8.7).
  - DR: Terror (with Assassinate), else Rally (with Subvert), else March, else Attack (8.6).
  - Syndicate: Rally, else March, else Construct, else Terror (8.5).
- Randomness: equal-priority spaces use two dice on a Random Spaces table with arrows (8.2). ECs use a Random EC table (8.3).
- 1-player victory (8.9): the player wins only after the final round. The player must beat every Non-player margin. The gap sets the result: 1 to 4 is Stalemate, 5 or more is Model Counterinsurgency or Model Rebellion.
- Third-printing booklet (nonplayer.md) repeats 8.0 with small text updates and a 1-player example by Örjan Ariander.

### Calixto card-based bots (2025 booklet)

- Replaces section 8.0 (Calixto C8.1). Credit: Kevin Crooks.
- Each NP Faction has 6 cards. All NP cards are shuffled into one deck. Draw: move cards to the bottom until the active Faction's card shows (C8.5.3). Reshuffle only at setup and each Reset.
- A card is a flowchart: blue condition boxes with green and red arrows, then an Operation box, then a Special Activity box (C8.5.3). A card has two sides and can be flipped or redrawn.
- Eligibility table plus Event Cards table (Critical or Performed) decide Event versus Operation. An Event must also be "Effective" (C8.5.2, C8.5.5).
- Activation Number (AN): after each space, roll 1d6. If the roll is at most the AN, stop selecting spaces. Govt AN equals the US Alliance value (C8.5.4).
- Space selection uses per-Faction priority tables with ordered dots. Shared Move Priorities and Piece Priorities tables exist (C8.5.6 to C8.5.8).
- Resources: only NP Syndicate tracks them. NP Govt, 26J and DR ignore Resources (C8.2.1).
- NP Govt, 26J and DR use Cash for a free LimOp. NP Syndicate does not (C8.2.1).
- Rule "When in Doubt, Randomize" (C8.2.1). Illegal instructions are skipped (C8.1).
- Solitaire victory: the player may win only in the final Propaganda Round. NP Factions may win in any round (C8.3.1).
- Designer note: the Operation-first card design gives fewer branches than Fire in the Lake bots. The cards react to the board (Calixto Designer Notes).

## Unique mechanics

- US Alliance track (Firm, Reluctant, Embargoed). It sets Government Operation cost, Air Strike access and NP spaces (6.3.1, 8.8).
- Casinos: Bases that cannot be removed, only closed. They count for Control only when open (1.4.5, 1.7).
- Cash markers: stash, transfer between Factions, and Launder for a free LimOp (4.5.2, 2.3.6).
- Skim: forced Resource transfer from Syndicate to Controlling Factions (6.2.3).
- Directorio victory by Control of Population plus Bases (7.2).
- Four-way asymmetric victory with different formulas, plus Victory Deception markers (7.3).
- Economic Centers: non-Pop spaces that give Government income and Syndicate income, and take Sabotage (1.3.4, 6.2).
- Insurgent Capabilities versus Government Momentum (5.3, 5.4).
- Per Playbook Design Notes, the Government bot was an addition for solo play as either side (Volko Ruhnke note, Playbook p.31).
- Small deck: 4 campaigns with 12 Event cards each in a standard game (Set Up p.20; derived from 48 cards over 4 piles, inferred).

## Relevance

- Inferred: US Alliance is a single track that changes the counterinsurgent's cost per space. A PKK game could use a similar "external support" track (for example Western arms or Gulf and Iraqi sanctuary) that scales state costs and unlocks or blocks air power.
- Inferred: Control-based victory for a weak centrist Faction (Directorio) shows how a third insurgent group can win by holding population, not by shifting opinion. A PKK design could model rival Kurdish or leftist groups with a Control formula.
- Inferred: Cash and Launder tie a political economy asset to tempo. A PKK game could use smuggling or diaspora funds as a marker that buys a free limited action.
- Inferred: Calixto card bots with Activation Numbers and one shared deck fit a digital Falling Sky port. Priorities tables map cleanly to code. The AN die roll gives variable bot scale without a long flowchart.
