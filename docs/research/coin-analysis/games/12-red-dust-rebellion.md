## Identity

- Title: Red Dust Rebellion (rulebook cover and 3.0 Introduction).
- COIN volume: Volume XII of the GMT COIN series (Introduction, p.3).
- Credits (printed in the Playbook p.47, not in the rulebook): Game design Jarrod Carmichael. Development Adam Blinkinsop and Joe Dewhurst. Original COIN series design Volko Ruhnke. COIN series development Jason Carr. Curiosity solitaire system design and development Joe Dewhurst. Jacquard solitaire system design Bruce Mansfield. Art and layout Terry Leeds and Marcos Villarroel Lara.
- Edition: rulebook (c) 2024 GMT Games. Curiosity booklet design notes dated February 2024 (Curiosity p.30).
- Setting: fictional Mars colony, 2250, the Martian Bicentennial (Playbook p.3 background text). Not a historical game.
- Scale: three populated Sectors plus a Wilderness, an Orbit, and the Earth-Transit-Phobos route called the Aldrin Cycler (1.2).
- Players: one to four. Missing players are replaced by Curiosity bots. Two and three player games without bots are possible (1.1).
- Scenarios: one. Setup is fixed (3.1). No scenario list exists. The game ends after the third Dust Storm Round (4.3).
- Components: 51 Event cards, 30 Asset cards, 12 Campaign cards, 24 Curiosity cards (Components).

## Factions

| Faction | Type | Victory (checked in Dust Storm Round Victory Phase) | Resource |
|---|---|---|---|
| Martian Government (MarsGov, MG) | COIN | Total Support + EarthGov Confidence exceeds 34 (2.0) | Resources, 0 to 50 (1.4) |
| Corporations (CORP) | COIN | Profits exceed 36 (2.0) | Profits, max 50. No Resources (1.4, 1.5) |
| Red Dust Movement (RD) | Insurgent | Total Opposition + RD Bases on Mars exceeds 32 (2.0) | Resources, 0 to 50 (1.4) |
| Church of the Reclaimer (CR) | Insurgent | Spaces with CR Control + CR Bases exceeds enemy (non-CR) Bases on Mars (2.0) | Asset cards, hand limit 6 (1.5) |
| Earth Government (EarthGov, EG) | Non-player COIN | Cannot win (1.1) | None |

- Victory margin: MG = Support + EG Confidence - 34. CORP = Profits - 36. RD = Opposition + RD Bases - 32. CR = Control spaces + CR Bases - enemy Bases (2.0).
- If nobody wins by the end, the highest margin wins. Ties go to Reclaimers, then MarsGov, then Red Dust (2.0).
- Start values: Support + EG 12, Profits 0, Opposition + Bases 14, CR Control + Bases 7, Enemy Bases 10, MG Resources 18, RD Resources 14 (3.1).
- Pieces (Components, 1.3, 3.1):
  - MG: 30 blue Troops, 3 Bases.
  - EG: 16 white Troops, 4 Satellites (no EG Base).
  - CORP: 16 Security, 8 SpecOps, 9 Bases.
  - RD: 30 Rebels, 9 Bases.
  - CR: 20 Rebels, 15 Bases.
- Hidden or Active: Rebels and SpecOps have both states. Troops, Security, Bases and Satellites are always Active (1.3).
- Upgraded Bases (1.3): CORP Terraforming Base, RD Dug-In Base, CR Conversion Center.
- Friends and enemies: MG, CORP and EG are friends. RD and CR are enemies of everyone, including each other. Rebels do not protect each other's Bases (1.1).
- EarthGov Controller: MG or CORP controls EG Troops and Satellites, set by the EG Confidence marker position. At the bottom of the track nobody controls EG (1.2).
- No-bot option: with fewer players one player runs both COIN or both Rebel Factions and uses the lower victory margin of the two (1.1).

## Sequence of play and eligibility

- Two cards are face up: Current Event and Next Event (4.0). Players see one card ahead.
- Each Event card shows a Flashpoint value (0 to 4 seen in the Playbook) and a Faction order of M, C, D, R (4.0, Playbook card list).
- When a Next Event is revealed, the Flashpoint track advances by its value. At the end of the track a Flashpoint Round runs at once (4.0).
- Reclaimer initiative: Reclaimers are last on every card. They may discard up to three Asset cards before anyone acts. Each card moves them one place forward. They must pay for every Faction icon they pass, even Ineligible ones (4.0, 4.1).
- Eligibility: a Faction that did not act last card is Eligible. All Factions become Eligible in the Dust Storm Reset Phase (4.1).
- 1st Eligible options: Operation only, Operation + Special Activity, Event (Reclaimers may use an Asset card Event), or Pass (4.1).
- 2nd Eligible options (4.1):
  - After Op only: Limited Operation only.
  - After Op + Special Activity: Limited Operation or Event.
  - After Event: Operation, with a Special Activity if wanted.
- Limited Operation: one space, no Special Activity. Secure, Recon and March may use many origins but one destination (4.1).
- Pass benefits (4.1):
  - MG gains 3 Resources.
  - CORP must activate the Aldrin Cycler.
  - RD gains 1 Resource.
  - CR draws 1 Asset card.
- Desert Efficiency: a 2nd Eligible Reclaimer Limited Operation may use many spaces but only one Asset card for payment. Then CR draws an Asset card (4.1).
- Haboob: when a Dust Storm card is the Next Event, no Recon or March may run (4.0). Reclaimers may still Travel (Playbook, card 49 to 51 text).
- When a Dust Storm card becomes the Current Event, a Dust Storm Round runs before the Next Event is revealed (4.0).
- Free Operations from Events cost nothing and do not change Eligibility (5.0).
- Series changes: shifting initiative by discarding cards (CR only); Flashpoint track instead of a fixed number of cards; two Rounds types besides the Event Round.

## Operations and Special Activities

MarsGov pays Resources. CORP pays Profits only in special cases. RD pays Resources. CR pays by discarding Asset cards (5.0).

| Faction | Operation | Effect, cost, key limits |
|---|---|---|
| COIN (all) | Secure | Move units to Labyrinths, Activate 1 Rebel per unit, may place a Base. MG pays 3 per destination (5.3) |
| COIN (all) | Recon | Same for Deserts. MG pays 3 per destination. 1 Rebel per 2 units in the Wilderness (5.4) |
| COIN (all) | Assault | Remove 1 Active Rebel or Base per cube or Active SpecOps. MG pays 3 per space. Rebels may free Attack back. Mercenaries: CORP gains 1 Profit per two Rebel forces removed in a space with Corporate Security or Active SpecOps, counted per space, even in a MarsGov Assault (5.5) |
| MG | Train | Place up to 4 Troops in MG Base spaces or controlled Labyrinths, 3 each. Then Pacify in one space (5.1) |
| CORP | Logistics | Place CORP units on Earth, run the Aldrin Cycler, upgrade Bases to Terraforming, place Security for 1 Profit (5.2) |
| Rebels (RD, CR) | Rally | RD pays 1 Resource per space. CR discards Asset cards with total value at least the cost (1.5). Place a Rebel, or swap 2 Rebels for a Base. With a Base: more Rebels, hide, or (CR) upgrade and draw (5.6) |
| Rebels (RD, CR) | Attack | RD pays 1 Resource per space. CR discards Asset cards with total value at least the cost (1.5). Two dice: sum at most forces gives Damage. Each die at most Rebels removes 2 enemy forces. Each EG Troop counts as 2 forces removed, so one die removes only one EG Troop (5.9) |
| RD | March | 1 Resource per destination. Rebels move. Hidden Rebels may become Active in Support spaces (5.7) |
| RD | Campaign | Populated spaces with an RD Rebel, 1 Resource per space. Activate a Rebel if none is Active, then shift the space one level toward Active Opposition. If it is now at (or already was at) Active Opposition, draw a Campaign card. At Passive Support: Damage, EG-, and CORP Base cuts Profits by 2 (5.10) |
| CR | Travel | Discard Asset cards with total value at least 1 per origin (Wilderness free) (1.5). Moves Rebels and Bases. Ignores Raging Storms. Chain moves via CR Bases (5.8) |
| CR | Preach | Discard Asset cards with total value at least 1 per space (1.5). Populated spaces with a CR Rebel (Activate one if none is Active). Shift 1 level toward Neutral. Only if the space was already Neutral, instead place Rebels equal to Population and remove a Population marker or, if none, place Damage (5.11) |

Special Activities (6.0):

| Faction | Special Activity | Effect and limits |
|---|---|---|
| MG | Entrench | Swap Troop for Base in 1 or 2 spaces. Fortified Troops sit on Infrastructure squares and cancel Damage (6.1) |
| MG | Petition | Place Supply markers on Earth. Costs CORP Profits. May set EG+ (6.2) |
| MG | Transport | Move Troops among Phobos, MG Base spaces and 1 more space (2 more if MarsGov is EarthGov Controller) (6.3) |
| CORP | Public Relations | Repair for +2 Profits each, House once. Penalizes RD (-3 Resources) or CR (discard) present (6.4) |
| CORP | Exploit | Up to 2 spaces. Profits equal Population, shift to Neutral. Benefits RD or CR Control (6.5) |
| CORP | Raid | Up to 2 spaces. Move in Hidden SpecOps and remove 2 forces or 1 EG Troop. Sets EG- if MG or EG hit (6.6) |
| RD | Redistribute | Up to 4 spaces. Gain Population + 1 per CORP Base in Resources (6.7) |
| RD | Coordinate | Up to 2 spaces without Support, each with a Hidden RD Rebel (Activate it). House or Repair if desired. Then shift the space 1 level toward Active Opposition, or, only if already at Active Opposition, remove 2 MG Troops or Security or replace 1 with an RD Rebel (6.8) |
| RD, CR | Ambush | With Attack. Set the dice to any value in up to 2 spaces (6.9, 6.12) |
| CR | Purify | Replace an enemy unit with a CR Rebel (a second one with a Conversion Center), or replace an enemy Base with a Conversion Center only if no friendly units remain with it, counting cubes and SpecOps of both COIN Factions (6.10) |

EarthGov Controller bonuses (5.3 to 5.5): Drop Pods (Secure), Navigation Beacons (Recon), Bombard and Suppress (Assault). Satellites from Orbit power these.

House and Repair (1.7): House moves a Population marker from Displaced Population into a space. Repair removes Damage and also costs a Displaced Population marker. Each Faction has its own EG Confidence side effect.

## Event cards and deck

- 51 Event cards, 3 of them Dust Storm cards (3.3, Components). Events are dual use: unshaded or shaded text (7.1).
- Deck build: remove the 3 Dust Storm cards. Shuffle 48 Events. Deal 3 piles of 12. Discard 12 unseen. Shuffle one Dust Storm into the bottom 6 of each pile. Stack the piles. Result: 39 cards (3.3).
- No Pivotal events and no Coup cards. The Dust Storm card plays the Coup role (4.3).
- Capabilities exist only as Reclaimer Asset cards. They stay in play and are never reshuffled. Playing one grants a free Operation in up to 2 spaces and an Asset draw (1.5).
- Asset deck: 30 cards. Each has a Resource value. Some have Events. CR starts with 3 cards (3.3, 1.5).
- Campaign deck: 12 cards, RD only. One is in play at setup. Campaign cards end at the next Dust Storm Reset (1.5, 3.3, 4.3).
- Event rules (7.0): events override rules but cannot place unavailable pieces, break stacking, or target Raging Storm Deserts (7.3). Many events set EG+ or EG- (7.5).
- Content: fictional. Playbook card 10 "Bicentennial Day" ties to the setting. Designer cites no real history (Playbook p.40 to 42).

## Map, spaces and tracks

- Spaces (1.2): Earth, Transit, Phobos, 4 Orbit circles, 3 Sectors, Wilderness.
- Sectors (3.1 setup lists):
  - Tharsis Montes: Europa, Tenzing, Tereshkova, Shepard, Ascraeus Mons, Tharsis Tholus (Labyrinths and Deserts mixed; exact type per name not verified without map).
  - Arabia Terra: New Cordoba, Shenzhou, Rutherford, Radau, Marth, Trouvelot.
  - Hellas Planitia: Gandhi, Sharma, Alpheus Colles, Coronae Montes, Hellas Chaos.
  - The setup list also names Arsia Mons, Pavonis Mons, Syria Planum, Noctis Labyrinthus, Daedalia Planum, Sinai Planum.
- Types: Labyrinth (circular, Spaceport, no Terraforming), Desert (irregular, has Stations for Bases), Wilderness (a Desert, never Populated, 6 Base limit) (1.2, 1.3).
- Adjacency: shared black or blue (Maglev) border. Wilderness touches edge Deserts. Aldrin Cycler spaces are not adjacent to anything. Maglev between Europa and Tereshkova is unusable until Event 14 (1.2). Full adjacency list: not verified (no map).
- Stacking: 2 Bases per space, 6 in Wilderness, 0 on Phobos (1.3).
- Population: 0 to 4. Infrastructure track of 2 or 4 boxes. Damage covers green boxes (1.7).
- Displaced Population box holds homeless Population markers (1.7).
- Support and Opposition: five levels from Active Support to Active Opposition. Passive counts 1 per Population, Active counts 2 (1.8).
- Control: COIN needs more forces than all Rebels. RD or CR needs more than all others combined (1.9).
- Tracks: Edge Track 0 to 50 (victory, Resources, Profits). Flashpoint Track. EG Confidence Track (its numeric range: not verified, the 4 CORP and 6 MG boundary is named in 4.2). Sequence of Play Track (1.2).
- Dust Storm markers: Approaching and Raging side. Raging Desert cannot be selected. Raging Labyrinth cannot be entered or left (1.10).

## Propaganda / Coup / Winter round

Two round types replace the series Coup.

Flashpoint Round (4.2), in order:

1. Aldrin Cycler: Transit to Phobos, Population to Displaced, Satellites to Orbit, Supply to MG (3 Resources each). Controller moves 1 Population and 5 other pieces from Earth to Transit. CORP may pay Profits to move more.
2. Corporate Casualties: Profits -1 per Base, -1 per 2 Security. Forces return to Available.
3. EarthGov Confidence: Satellites to Orbit. Marker moves one step per EG+ or EG-. Controller may change. Add EG Troops, Supply and Population to Earth by marker position.
4. Terraforming: +2 for the first Terraforming Base per Desert, +1 for each more, -1 per Conversion Center in a Desert.
5. Attrition and Conversion: remove MG Troop, Security and RD Rebel from empty or stormy Deserts without own Base. Place a CR Rebel at each Populated Conversion Center.
6. Reset the Flashpoint track.
7. Dust Storms: Raging removed, Approaching flipped, new storms rolled (count = Next Event Flashpoint, max 6 markers). A repeat roll makes it Raging and CR removes one enemy piece.

Dust Storm Round (4.3), in order:

1. Victory Phase: Corporate and EarthGov Casualties penalties. Displaced Population penalty (-3 MG Resources and -1 Profits per 2 markers). Then the victory check.
2. Resources Phase: MG gains Population of COIN-controlled spaces without Opposition. CORP gains 2 Profits per Labyrinth Base. RD gains Active Opposition Population plus RD Bases. CR draws cards per card symbol on its Available Bases track.
3. Support Phase: Pacify (MG), Lobby (MG, 5 Resources for +1 EG Confidence), Agitate (RD).
4. Redeploy Phase: storms removed. EG, MG, RD and CR forces move.
5. Reset Phase: hide Rebels and SpecOps, end Campaign cards, reshuffle Asset deck, all Eligible, new cards, new storms. After the third round the game ends here.

## Non-player (bot) system

System name: Curiosity, by Joe Dewhurst. It adapts the Jacquard system of Bruce Mansfield (Curiosity 8.1).

- Each NP Faction has 6 flowchart cards, shuffled into one deck (8.4, 8.5.3). Drawing cycles cards to the bottom until one matches the active Faction.
- Cards test conditions, then give an Operation, a Special Activity list (first workable one is used), or a flip to the reverse side (8.5.3).
- Priority tables: Space Selection (one per Faction), Move, Piece (8.5.6 to 8.5.8). Ties are broken at random with a die (8.4.1, 8.5.6).
- Activation Number: after each space, roll 1d6. If at or below the AN, stop. Else pick another space. CORP has no AN (8.5.4).
- Resource stand-ins (8.2.1, 8.4):
  - NP MG: Supply Total.
  - NP RD: Agitate Total, start 1d3.
  - NP CR: Asset Total, start 3.
  - NP CORP: real Profits.
- Failed AN rolls can be saved by spending Supply Total (MG) or Asset Total (CR) (8.5.4).
- NP CR initiative: roll 3d6 against Asset Total. The count of dice at or below Asset Total must equal the discards needed (8.5.2).
- NP CR Limited Operations: at least 2 and at most 5 spaces (8.5.4).
- Eligibility table: each NP Faction has a fixed line list. Events marked Critical or Performed or Not Performed on cards (8.5.5). 1st Eligible runs an Event only if Critical and effective. 2nd Eligible runs Critical or Performed effective Events.
- NP MG and CORP choose unshaded text. NP RD and CR choose shaded text (8.5.5).
- NP CORP sets EG+ if Confidence is below 4, else EG- (8.4.1).
- NP CORP never spends Profits for extra Earth or Transit moves (8.5.9).
- Solitaire: the human wins only at the final Dust Storm Round. Bots may win in any (8.3.1).
- Bots follow the rules, skip illegal instructions, and read text literally (8.2.1).

## Unique mechanics

- Hidden-information Faction: CR holds an Asset hand that pays for Operations, buys initiative, and holds Events (1.5, 4.1). Curiosity says this is the first major hidden information in COIN (Curiosity p.30).
- Flashpoint track and Rounds: event cards drive a countdown that triggers an economic and logistic round (4.0, 4.2).
- Aldrin Cycler: a supply pipeline through Earth, Transit and Phobos with delay and risk (1.2, 4.2).
- Population as movable pieces with Damage, House and Repair, plus a Displaced Population penalty (1.7, 4.3).
- Shared control of EarthGov by two Factions, switched by a confidence track (1.2).
- Dust Storms as moving map hazards that block actions and trigger attrition (1.10, 4.2).
- Different Base upgrades per Faction: Terraforming, Dug-In, Conversion Center (1.3).
- Victory markers computed from both Base counts and Support or Control, and CR victory compares own score to enemy Bases (2.0).
- Asymmetric costs: CORP spends Profits, which are also its score (1.4, 5.2).

## Relevance

- Hidden or Active Rebels with Base-protected removal and "Bases last" is a ready model for PKK cells and camps (5.5, 5.9). Inferred.
- Displaced Population and House or Repair give a model for forced village evacuation and return (1.7, 4.3). Inferred, needs cultural care.
- Card-based Eligibility with a resource-bought initiative could model a Faction that trades supplies for tempo (1.5, 4.1). Inferred.
- For a digital Falling Sky: the Curiosity pattern of flowchart cards, priority tables, Activation Numbers and "skip illegal, execute literally" is a clean software spec (8.2.1, 8.5.3 to 8.5.8). Inferred.
